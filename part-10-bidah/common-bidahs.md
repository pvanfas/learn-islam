# Common Bid'ahs Practiced Today

**Difficulty:** Intermediate &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Read time:** ~8 min &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Part:** Part 10 Bid'ah

---

## How to Use This Chapter

This chapter describes practices that Islamic scholarship classifies as Bid'ah or potentially Bid'ah. The goal is knowledge, not condemnation. Muslims who practice these do so with sincere hearts and sincere hearts deserve gentle, evidence-based guidance, not harsh judgment.

---

## 1 Commemorating the 40th Day After Death

**The practice:** Gathering on the 40th day (or the anniversary) after a person's death to recite Quran, feed people, and pray for the deceased.

**The issue:** No evidence from the Prophet or Companions for designating the 40th day as a specific occasion. This practice traces to pre-Islamic cultural traditions (ancient Egyptian, Persian).

**What IS established:** Reciting Quran and making du'a for the deceased at any time no specific day required.

---

## 2 Congregational Dhikr in Specific Unestablished Formulas

Dhikr in congregation is established in the Sunnah. The issue arises when specific:

- Numbers not established in the Sunnah
- Specific days not established in the Sunnah
- Specific formulas invented after the Prophet
- Physical movements (swaying, etc.) added to dhikr

**What IS established:** The morning and evening adhkar, the post-prayer dhikr, the general encouragement to gather for dhikr all from authentic Hadith.

---

## 3 Specific Night Prayers Not Established

Some communities perform special prayers on specific nights (15th of Sha'ban, first Friday of Rajab, etc.) with claims of specific rewards. When investigated, these narrations are typically fabricated or very weak.

**The principle:** Voluntary night prayer (_Tahajjud/Qiyam al-Layl_) is always encouraged but attaching specific claims to specific nights requires specific Sunnah evidence.

---

## 4 Innovations in the Funeral Process

**Examples:**

- Reciting specific Surahs together at the graveside in a designated group format
- Hiring someone to recite Quran for the deceased's soul
- Playing recordings of Quran at the grave

**What IS established:** Du'a for the deceased, visiting graves to remember death, individual recitation and prayer for the deceased.

---

## 5 Adding Prayers to the Adhan

In some regions, additional phrases are added to the Adhan phrases not found in the established Sunnah narrations. The Adhan is a fixed text transmitted from the Prophet through Bilal (RA) additions are Bid'ah regardless of their content.
