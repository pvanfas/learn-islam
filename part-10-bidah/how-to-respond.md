# How to Respond to Bid'ah The Prophetic Method

**Difficulty:** Beginner &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Read time:** ~6 min &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Part:** Part 10: Bid'ah

---

## The Goal Is Guidance, Not Condemnation

The Prophet corrected innovations with:

- Clear evidence
- Gentleness
- Patience
- Concern for the person, not just the practice

He never declared a person who committed Bid'ah as outside Islam. He corrected the practice while maintaining the relationship.

---

## The Practical Steps

### 1. Know the Sunnah Deeply First

The best response to Bid'ah is a life lived beautifully on the Sunnah. When your practice is evidently rooted in the Prophet's example, the contrast itself is da'wah.

### 2. Choose the Right Moment

Not every encounter requires correction. The Prophet said: _"Religion is sincere advice."_ (Sahih Muslim) but sincere advice is given at the right time, in the right way, with the right intention.

Correcting someone in public who is performing a Bid'ah humiliating them in front of others is itself a violation of Islamic manners.

### 3. Use Evidence, Not Opinion

_"The Prophet said..."_ or _"there is no evidence for this practice in the Sunnah..."_ is more effective than _"this is wrong"_ or _"scholars say..."_ without specifics.

### 4. Accept That People May Differ

On issues where major scholars genuinely disagree (like the Mawlid), the appropriate response is presenting both positions not declaring the other side's practice invalid. Bid'ah has degrees not all are equal.

### 5. Focus on Your Own Practice

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih al-Bukhari**
_"It is enough of a sin for a person to neglect those he is responsible for."_
{% endhint %}

Spending more energy correcting others' Bid'ah than perfecting one's own worship and character is itself a kind of misprioritization. Focus on your own Sunnah first.
