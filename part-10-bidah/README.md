# Part 10 Bid'ah (Religious Innovation)

**Difficulty:** Intermediate &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Chapters:** 6 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Estimated reading time:** ~55 min

---

## What You Will Learn

Bid'ah religious innovation is the introduction of acts of worship without basis in the Quran and Sunnah. This part explains what Bid'ah is, its categories, common examples, and how to respond wisely protecting the purity of worship while maintaining community.
