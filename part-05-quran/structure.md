## Makki and Madani Surahs

The 114 Surahs are classified by where they were revealed:

| Type       | Where Revealed             | Characteristics                                                             |
| ---------- | -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Makki**  | Before the Hijrah (Makkah) | Shorter, focus on Tawhid, Judgement Day, Prophetic stories                  |
| **Madani** | After the Hijrah (Madinah) | Longer, cover social law, community governance, relations with other faiths |

**Roughly:** 86 Makki Surahs, 28 Madani.

## The Juz' System: For Daily Reading

The Quran is divided into 30 equal portions (_Ajza'_ plural of Juz') to facilitate reading one per day during Ramadan, completing the entire Quran in 30 days.

**Juz' 1** begins at (Surah Al-Baqarah 1:1) (after Al-Fatihah which is its own Surah).

## Al-Fatihah: The Opening

Surah Al-Fatihah (7 verses) stands alone as the opening of the Quran, not counted in the numbering of the remaining 113 Surahs. It is:

- Recited in every Rak'ah of every prayer
- Called _Umm al-Quran_ (Mother of the Quran)
- The most repeated Surah in human history

## The Structure of Each Surah

Every Surah except Al-Tawbah (Surah 9) begins with:

**بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ**
_Bismillahir-rahmanir-rahim_
"In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful"

This is called the _Basmala_. It is counted as an Ayah at the beginning of Surah Al-Fatihah.

## Mysterious Letters: Al-Huruf al-Muqatta'at

29 Surahs begin with mysterious Arabic letters, _Alif Lam Mim_, _Ha Mim_, _Ya Sin_, etc. Their precise meaning is known only to Allah. They are among the wonders of the Quran that demonstrate its divine origin. The Arabs, who were masters of language, could not explain or replicate them.
