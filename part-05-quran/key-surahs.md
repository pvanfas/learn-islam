# Key Surahs Every Muslim Should Know

**Difficulty:** Beginner &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Read time:** ~14 min &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Part:** Part 5: The Quran

---

## In This Chapter

> The Quran has 114 Surahs but certain chapters hold a special place in the Muslim's daily life, recommended by the Prophet for specific times, occasions, and purposes. This chapter presents the most essential Surahs with their Arabic text, transliteration, translation, and the specific prophetic guidance on when and why to recite them.

## Surah Al-Fatihah The Opening (Chapter 1)

**The most important Surah in the Quran recited in every Rak'ah of every prayer.**

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih al-Bukhari**

The Prophet said: _"There is no prayer for the one who does not recite Al-Fatihah."_

And: _"It is the greatest Surah in the Quran."_
{% endhint %}

**Arabic:**

> بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ ۝ الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ ۝ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ ۝ مَالِكِ يَوْمِ الدِّينِ ۝ إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ ۝ اهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ الْمُسْتَقِيمَ ۝ صِرَاطَ الَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ الْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا الضَّالِّينَ

**Translation:**
_In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. All praise is for Allah, Lord of all worlds. The Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. Master of the Day of Judgement. You alone we worship, and You alone we ask for help. Guide us to the straight path the path of those You have blessed, not those who have earned anger, nor those who have gone astray._

**Why it matters:** Al-Fatihah is the Quran summarized in seven verses Tawhid, praise, dependence, supplication, and guidance. It is also a dua that the believer makes a minimum of 17 times per day in the obligatory prayers alone.

## Ayat al-Kursi The Verse of the Throne (Al-Baqarah 2:255)

**The greatest verse in the Quran protection and Tawhid in one.**

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih Muslim**

The Prophet asked Ubayy ibn Ka'b (RA): _"Which verse in the Book of Allah is the greatest?"_
He replied: _"Allah and His Messenger know best."_
The Prophet asked again and again until Ubayy said: _"Ayat al-Kursi."_
The Prophet struck his chest and said: _"Rejoice in your knowledge, O Abu Mundhir!"_
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih al-Bukhari**

_"Whoever recites Ayat al-Kursi after every obligatory prayer nothing prevents him from entering Paradise except death."_
{% endhint %}

**Arabic:**

> اللَّهُ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ الْحَيُّ الْقَيُّومُ ۚ لَا تَأْخُذُهُ سِنَةٌ وَلَا نَوْمٌ ۚ لَّهُ مَا فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ وَمَا فِي الْأَرْضِ ۗ مَن ذَا الَّذِي يَشْفَعُ عِندَهُ إِلَّا بِإِذْنِهِ ۚ يَعْلَمُ مَا بَيْنَ أَيْدِيهِمْ وَمَا خَلْفَهُمْ ۖ وَلَا يُحِيطُونَ بِشَيْءٍ مِّنْ عِلْمِهِ إِلَّا بِمَا شَاءَ ۚ وَسِعَ كُرْسِيُّهُ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضَ ۖ وَلَا يَئُودُهُ حِفْظُهُمَا ۚ وَهُوَ الْعَلِيُّ الْعَظِيمُ

**Translation:**
_Allah there is no god except Him, the Ever-Living, the Sustainer of all. Neither drowsiness nor sleep overtakes Him. To Him belongs whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth. Who is it that can intercede with Him except by His permission? He knows what is before them and what will be after them, and they do not encompass a thing of His knowledge except for what He wills. His throne extends over the heavens and the earth, and their preservation tires Him not. And He is the Most High, the Most Great._

**When to recite:** After every obligatory prayer. Before sleeping (for protection through the night).

## Surah Al-Ikhlas The Sincerity (Chapter 112)

**Equal to one-third of the Quran in reward.**

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih al-Bukhari**

The Prophet said: _"Say: He is Allah, the One is equal to one-third of the Quran."_
{% endhint %}

**Arabic:**

> قُلْ هُوَ اللَّهُ أَحَدٌ ۝ اللَّهُ الصَّمَدُ ۝ لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ ۝ وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ

**Transliteration:**
_Qul huwa Allahu ahad. Allahus-samad. Lam yalid wa lam yulad. Wa lam yakun lahu kufuwan ahad._

**Translation:**
_Say: He is Allah, the One. Allah, the Eternal Absolute. He neither begets nor was He begotten. And there is nothing comparable to Him._

**When to recite:** Morning and evening adhkar (3 times each). After every obligatory prayer. Before sleeping (with Al-Falaq and An-Nas blow into palms and wipe over body, ×3).

**Why it matters:** Four verses that contain the essence of Islamic theology about God Tawhid at its purest.

## Surah Al-Falaq The Daybreak (Chapter 113)

**Seeking refuge from external harms.**

**Arabic:**

> قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ الْفَلَقِ ۝ مِن شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ ۝ وَمِن شَرِّ غَاسِقٍ إِذَا وَقَبَ ۝ وَمِن شَرِّ النَّفَّاثَاتِ فِي الْعُقَدِ ۝ وَمِن شَرِّ حَاسِدٍ إِذَا حَسَدَ

**Transliteration:**
_Qul a'udhu bi rabbil-falaq. Min sharri ma khalaq. Wa min sharri ghasiqin idha waqab. Wa min sharrin-naffathati fil-'uqad. Wa min sharri hasidin idha hasad._

**Translation:**
_Say: I seek refuge in the Lord of the daybreak from the evil of what He has created, from the evil of darkness when it settles, from the evil of those who blow on knots, and from the evil of an envier when he envies._

---

## Surah An-Nas Mankind (Chapter 114)

**Seeking refuge from internal whispers.**

**Arabic:**

> قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ النَّاسِ ۝ مَلِكِ النَّاسِ ۝ إِلَٰهِ النَّاسِ ۝ مِن شَرِّ الْوَسْوَاسِ الْخَنَّاسِ ۝ الَّذِي يُوَسْوِسُ فِي صُدُورِ النَّاسِ ۝ مِنَ الْجِنَّةِ وَالنَّاسِ

**Transliteration:**
_Qul a'udhu bi rabbin-nas. Malikin-nas. Ilahin-nas. Min sharril-waswasil-khannas. Alladhi yuwaswisu fi sudurin-nas. Minal-jinnati wan-nas._

**Translation:**
_Say: I seek refuge in the Lord of mankind, the Sovereign of mankind, the God of mankind from the evil of the retreating whisperer, who whispers into the hearts of mankind from among the jinn and mankind._

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sunan an-Nasa'i (Sahih)**

The Prophet said to Uqbah ibn Amir (RA): _"Shall I not guide you to the best verses with which one seeks refuge? 'Say: I seek refuge in the Lord of the daybreak' and 'Say: I seek refuge in the Lord of mankind.' Recite them when you sleep at night and when you wake."_
{% endhint %}

## Surah Al-Kahf The Cave (Chapter 18)

**Recited every Friday protection from the Dajjal.**

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih Muslim**

The Prophet said: _"Whoever memorizes ten verses from the beginning of Surah Al-Kahf will be protected from the Dajjal."_

And: _"Whoever reads Surah Al-Kahf on Jumu'ah (Friday) will have light shining for him between the two Fridays."_
{% endhint %}

Surah Al-Kahf contains four great stories the People of the Cave (steadfastness in faith), the Man with Two Gardens (the danger of arrogance with wealth), Musa and al-Khidr (humility before divine wisdom), and Dhul-Qarnayn (just leadership). Each story addresses a trial of the end times.

**When to recite:** Every Friday beginning from Thursday sunset to Friday sunset.

---

## Surah Al-Mulk The Sovereignty (Chapter 67)

**Protection from the punishment of the grave.**

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sunan Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi (Hasan)**

The Prophet said: _"There is a Surah in the Quran of thirty verses that intercedes for its companion until he is forgiven. It is: 'Blessed is He in whose hand is the dominion.'"_
{% endhint %}

Surah Al-Mulk 30 verses begins with _"Tabarak alladhi biyadihil-mulk"_ and reminds the believer of Allah's sovereignty over life, death, and all creation. The Prophet recited it every night before sleeping.

**When to recite:** Every night before sleeping.

---

## Surah Ya-Sin (Chapter 36)

**The heart of the Quran.**

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sunan Abu Dawud (Hasan)**

The Prophet said: _"Everything has a heart, and the heart of the Quran is Ya-Sin."_
{% endhint %}

Surah Ya-Sin covers the mission of prophethood, the resurrection, the Day of Judgement, and Allah's signs in creation making it a comprehensive summary of the Quran's central themes. It is traditionally recited for the dying and the deceased, though this specific practice has scholarly discussion.

---

## Surah Al-Waqi'ah The Inevitable Event (Chapter 56)

**Protection from poverty.**

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Shu'ab al-Iman by al-Bayhaqi**

The Prophet said: _"Whoever recites Surah Al-Waqi'ah every night will never be afflicted by poverty."_
{% endhint %}

Surah Al-Waqi'ah vividly describes the three categories of people on the Day of Judgement the foremost, the companions of the right, and the companions of the left and the distinction between them.

---

## Surah Al-Baqarah The Cow (Chapter 2)

**The longest Surah light in the home and protection from Shaytan.**

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih Muslim**

The Prophet said: _"Recite Surah Al-Baqarah in your homes, for Shaytan does not enter a house in which Surah Al-Baqarah is recited."_

And: _"The one who recites Surah Al-Baqarah and Surah Al-Imran will have them come as two clouds or two shades or two flocks of birds pleading on their behalf on the Day of Judgement."_
{% endhint %}

The last two verses of Surah Al-Baqarah (2:285-286) _"Amana ar-rasulu..."_ are particularly recommended:

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih al-Bukhari**

_"Whoever recites the last two verses of Surah Al-Baqarah at night they will suffice him."_
{% endhint %}

## Quick Reference When to Recite What

| Time / Occasion               | Recommended Surah                                   |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Every prayer (obligatory)     | Al-Fatihah (mandatory)                              |
| After every obligatory prayer | Ayat al-Kursi                                       |
| Morning and evening (×3 each) | Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq, An-Nas                         |
| Before sleeping               | Ayat al-Kursi, Al-Mulk, last 2 verses of Al-Baqarah |
| Every Friday                  | Al-Kahf                                             |
| Every night                   | Al-Mulk                                             |
| In the home regularly         | Al-Baqarah                                          |
| Times of anxiety and fear     | Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq, An-Nas                         |

## Key Takeaways

- Al-Fatihah is the greatest Surah recited in every Rak'ah of every prayer a conversation with Allah.
- Ayat al-Kursi is the greatest verse after every prayer it opens the gates of Paradise until death.
- Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq, and An-Nas together recited morning and evening and before sleep provide comprehensive spiritual protection.
- Al-Kahf recited every Friday gives light between two Fridays and protects from the Dajjal.
- Al-Mulk recited every night intercedes for its reader in the grave.
- Al-Baqarah in the home drives Shaytan out.
