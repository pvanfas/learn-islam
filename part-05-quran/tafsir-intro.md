## What Is Tafsir?

**Tafsir** (تَفْسِير) means explanation or interpretation the scholarly discipline of explaining the meaning of Quranic verses. It is the oldest Islamic academic discipline, with roots in the Prophet's own explanations to his Companions.

The Quran contains:

- Clear verses (_Muhkamat_) self-evident in meaning
- Less clear verses (_Mutashabihat_) requiring interpretation
- Verses requiring historical context (_Asbab al-Nuzul_) to understand
- Verses using figurative language
- Legal verses requiring juristic analysis

Tafsir addresses all of these dimensions.

## The Levels of Tafsir

### 1. The Quran Explains Itself

The most authoritative form. A verse that is brief in one place is explained in detail elsewhere. Example: the command to pray (_Salah_) is detailed in verses about specific times, postures, and conditions.

### 2. The Sunnah Explains the Quran

The Prophet was the living Tafsir of the Quran his practice demonstrates what the commands actually mean. Aisha (RA) said: _"His character was the Quran."_

### 3. The Companions' Explanations

The Companions witnessed the revelation in context their explanations carry unique authority as eyewitnesses.

### 4. Scholarly Deduction (_Ijtihad_)

Using Arabic linguistic analysis, hadith, legal principles, and scholarly consensus to interpret passages not directly explained above.

## The Major Classical Tafsir Works

| Work                                  | Scholar                  | Known For                                                                           |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Tafsir al-Tabari** (Jami' al-Bayan) | Al-Tabari (d. 923 CE)    | Most comprehensive classical Tafsir; gathers all Companion explanations             |
| **Tafsir Ibn Kathir**                 | Ibn Kathir (d. 1373 CE)  | Most widely used in English; hadith-focused; accessible                             |
| **Tafsir al-Qurtubi**                 | Al-Qurtubi (d. 1272 CE)  | Strong on legal rulings derived from verses                                         |
| **Tafsir al-Baghawi**                 | Al-Baghawi (d. 1122 CE)  | Balanced; widely respected across madhabs                                           |
| **Fi Zilal al-Quran**                 | Sayyid Qutb (d. 1966 CE) | Modern literary Tafsir; spiritually engaging though controversial in some positions |

## Conditions for Valid Tafsir

Not everyone may interpret the Quran independently. Valid Tafsir requires:

- Mastery of classical Arabic and Quranic sciences
- Knowledge of the Sunnah and its relationship to the Quran
- Knowledge of _Asbab al-Nuzul_ (occasions of revelation)
- Knowledge of abrogating and abrogated verses (_Nasikh wa Mansukh_)
- Knowledge of scholarly consensus

**The danger of unqualified interpretation:** The Prophet said: _"Whoever interprets the Quran by his own opinion let him take his seat in the Fire."_ (Tirmidhi Hasan)
