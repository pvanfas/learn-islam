## What You Will Learn in This Part

The Quran is the most important text in the life of every Muslim and one of the most significant documents in human history. This part covers everything a student of Islam needs to know about the Quran: what it is, how it was revealed and preserved, its structure and themes, the science of Tajweed, the discipline of Tafsir (interpretation), and the relationship between the Quran and modern science.

Every Muslim should have a deep, personal relationship with the Quran. This part provides the intellectual foundation for that relationship the knowledge that transforms the Quran from a text you recite into a book that speaks to you.

## Opening The Quran's Invitation

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Muhammad 47:24**

_أَفَلَا يَتَدَبَّرُونَ الْقُرْآنَ أَمْ عَلَىٰ قُلُوبٍ أَقْفَالُهَا_

**Translation:** "Do they not reflect upon the Quran or are there locks upon their hearts?"

_The Quran's own invitation: not just to read, but to reflect. The Arabic word Tadabbur means deep, sustained contemplation not a quick read-through. This part equips you to respond to that invitation._
{% endhint %}
