# Science in the Quran

**Difficulty:** Intermediate &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Read time:** ~15 min &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Part:** Part 5 The Quran

## In This Chapter

> The Quran was revealed in 7th-century Arabia yet it contains descriptions of natural phenomena that align remarkably with discoveries made centuries or millennia later. This chapter surveys the most significant scientific signs in the Quran, examines them honestly, and addresses the responsible approach to this subject avoiding both dismissal and overclaiming.

## Opening Reflection

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Fussilat 41:53**

_سَنُرِيهِمْ آيَاتِنَا فِي الْآفَاقِ وَفِي أَنفُسِهِمْ حَتَّىٰ يَتَبَيَّنَ لَهُمْ أَنَّهُ الْحَقُّ_

**Translation:** "We will show them Our signs in the horizons and within themselves until it becomes clear to them that it is the truth. Is it not sufficient that your Lord is Witness over all things?"

_This verse was revealed 1,400 years ago. It promised that humanity would over time discover signs of divine truth in the universe and within themselves. The scientific era is, in many ways, the ongoing fulfillment of this promise._
{% endhint %}

## How to Approach This Topic

Before examining the verses, three principles are essential:

**1. The Quran is primarily a book of guidance not a science textbook.**
Its purpose is to guide humanity to righteousness, correct belief, and a good life. Scientific signs are embedded within that larger framework they are not the primary subject.

**2. Responsible interpretation requires caution.**
Not every observation of apparent alignment between a Quranic verse and a scientific discovery is a genuine miracle. Some claimed "Quranic scientific miracles" are based on creative reinterpretation of verses that, in their context, carry a different meaning. This chapter will present only the most well-documented and widely cited examples.

**3. The strongest evidence is what 7th-century humans could not have known.**
The standard of a genuine scientific sign is not simply that the Quran mentions something that science later confirms. It is that the Quran's description was _impossible to arrive at through the science and knowledge of the 7th century_ yet is accurate. That is the standard this chapter applies.

## 1 The Expanding Universe

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Adh-Dhariyat 51:47**

_وَالسَّمَاءَ بَنَيْنَاهَا بِأَيْدٍ وَإِنَّا لَمُوسِعُونَ_

**Translation:** "And the heaven We constructed it with power, and indeed, We are [its] expander."
{% endhint %}

The Arabic word _Musi'un_ (مُوسِعُون) comes from _Wasi'a_ to expand, to widen. The verse describes the heavens as being continuously expanded.

The expansion of the universe was discovered by Edwin Hubble in 1929 when he observed that galaxies were receding from each other. Albert Einstein's field equations had predicted it, but he initially rejected the implication, calling it his "greatest blunder." The universe has been expanding since the Big Bang.

In 7th-century Arabia, the cosmological view was of a fixed, static sky. No scientific framework available to Muhammad could have produced the concept of an expanding cosmos.

## 2 The Big Bang

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Al-Anbiya 21:30**

_أَوَلَمْ يَرَ الَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا أَنَّ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضَ كَانَتَا رَتْقًا فَفَتَقْنَاهُمَا_

**Translation:** "Do the disbelievers not see that the heavens and the earth were a joined entity (_Ratq_), and We separated them (_Fataq_)? And from water We made every living thing. Will they not then believe?"
{% endhint %}

The words _Ratq_ (joined, fused, closed) and _Fataq_ (split, separated, opened) describe a primordial unity that was then separated a description that aligns with the modern cosmological model of the Big Bang, in which all matter and energy were in a single point (singularity) before expanding outward.

The verse also contains a second remarkable claim: _"From water We made every living thing."_ Modern biology confirms that all known life is water-based. The first living cells emerged in aqueous environments. The human body is approximately 60% water.

## 3 Embryology and Human Development

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Al-Mu'minun 23:1214**

**Translation:** "And certainly did We create man from an extract of clay. Then We placed him as a sperm-drop in a firm lodging. Then We made the sperm-drop into a clinging clot, and We made the clot into a lump of flesh, and We made from the lump, bones, and We covered the bones with flesh; then We developed him into another creation. So blessed is Allah, the best of creators."
{% endhint %}

This verse describes the stages of human embryonic development:

| Quranic Term             | Arabic  | Modern Understanding                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------ | ------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Nutfah                   | نُطْفَة | Sperm / fertilized ovum                                                                                                |
| 'Alaqah                  | عَلَقَة | Clinging clot / suspended embryo (also means "leech-like" the embryo resembles a leech and clings to the uterine wall) |
| Mudghah                  | مُضْغَة | Chewed lump / somite stage (the embryo looks like a chewed substance with somite ridges)                               |
| Bones covered with flesh |         | Skeletal formation followed by muscle development                                                                      |

The description of the embryo as _'Alaqah_ a word that simultaneously means "clinging," "leech-like," and "blood clot" is remarkably apt. At the early embryonic stage, the embryo clings to the uterine wall, is nourished by maternal blood, and visually resembles a leech under microscope.

Professor Keith Moore, a renowned embryologist and author of _The Developing Human_ (a standard medical textbook), stated after studying these verses: _"It has been a pleasure for me to help clarify statements in the Quran about human development. It is clear to me that these statements must have come to Muhammad from God."_

The 2nd-century physician Galen, whose embryological writings dominated European medicine until the 17th century, had a very different and inaccurate understanding of embryonic development. No 7th-century physician would have described these stages as the Quran does.

## 4 The Barrier Between Two Seas

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Al-Furqan 25:53**

**Translation:** "And it is He who has released the two seas this one fresh and sweet, and this one salty and bitter and He placed between them a barrier and a prohibition against passage."

**Surah Ar-Rahman 55:1920**

**Translation:** "He released the two seas, meeting side by side. Between them is a barrier they do not transgress."
{% endhint %}

Oceanographers have discovered that where fresh water rivers meet the saltwater ocean and where two ocean bodies of different salinity meet (like the Atlantic and Pacific at the tip of South America) there exists a **halocline** a physical boundary zone where the two bodies of water do not fully mix immediately. They maintain distinct temperature, salinity, and density properties even at the point of meeting.

Jacques Cousteau, the pioneering oceanographer, reportedly expressed astonishment upon learning of these verses after discovering the Mediterranean-Atlantic boundary in the Strait of Gibraltar.

## 5 The Water Cycle

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Az-Zumar 39:21**

**Translation:** "Do you not see that Allah sends down rain from the sky and makes it flow as springs in the earth? Then He produces thereby crops of varying colors, then they dry and you see them turned yellow; then He makes them debris. Indeed in that is a reminder for those of understanding."
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah An-Nur 24:43**

**Translation:** "Do you not see that Allah drives clouds? Then He brings them together, then He makes them into a mass, and you see the rain emerge from within it. And He sends down from the sky, mountains of clouds, in which is hail..."
{% endhint %}

The complete water cycle evaporation, cloud formation, precipitation, groundwater is described across multiple Quranic verses. The scientific understanding of the water cycle was not formalized until the 17th century (by Pierre Perrault and Edme Mariotte). Before that, theories about where springs came from ranged from underground seas to subterranean rivers with no connection to rain.

## 6 Pain Receptors in the Skin

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah An-Nisa 4:56**

**Translation:** "Indeed, those who disbelieve in Our verses We will drive them into a fire. Every time their skins are roasted through, We will replace them with other skins so they may taste the punishment. Indeed, Allah is ever Exalted in Might and Wise."
{% endhint %}

This verse describes the punishment of Hell in physical terms and specifically that the _skin_ is the organ of pain sensation. Modern neuroscience confirms that pain receptors (_nociceptors_) are concentrated in the skin the skin is the primary organ of pain sensation. The internal organs have relatively fewer pain receptors. If you want to cause pain, damaging the skin is the most effective method.

The fact that the verse specifies that the _skins_ are replaced (not the body as a whole) so that the pain experience is renewed shows a level of anatomical specificity that was not part of 7th-century medical knowledge.

## 7 Mountains as Pegs / Stabilizers

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah An-Naba 78:67**

**Translation:** "Have We not made the earth a resting place? And the mountains as pegs (_Awtad_)?"

**Surah Al-Anbiya 21:31**

**Translation:** "And We have placed within the earth firmly set mountains lest it shift with you..."
{% endhint %}

The Quran describes mountains as _Awtad_ (pegs) implying they have roots that extend deep into the earth and stabilize it. Modern geology confirms this through the discovery of **isostasy** the principle that mountain ranges have deep roots (crustal extensions) that extend far below the surface and anchor the continental plates. The "root" of a mountain is often several times deeper than the mountain is tall.

In the 7th century, mountains were simply observed as tall rock formations. The concept of deep geological roots stabilizing tectonic plates was completely unknown.

## 8 Iron Sent from Space

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Al-Hadid 57:25**

**Translation:** "...And We sent down iron, in which is great might and benefits for the people..."
{% endhint %}

The Arabic phrase _Anzalna al-hadid_ "We sent down iron" uses the same word (_Anzala_) used for the revelation of the Quran and for rain. This suggests iron came from _above_ from outside the earth.

Modern astrophysics confirms that **iron is not native to the earth**. The iron found on earth including the earth's iron core arrived from space. Iron is formed in the nuclear cores of massive stars through stellar nucleosynthesis. When those stars explode in supernovae, iron is dispersed through space and eventually incorporated into planetary bodies. The earth's iron arrived through meteorite impacts and during the formation of the solar system.

The specific use of the word "sent down" for iron unlike any other metal mentioned in the Quran is notable.

## 9 The Honeybee and Healing

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah An-Nahl 16:6869**

**Translation:** "And your Lord inspired to the bee: 'Take for yourself among the mountains, houses, and among the trees and in that which they construct. Then eat from all the fruits and follow the ways of your Lord laid down for you.' There emerges from their bellies a drink varying in colors in which there is healing for people. Indeed in that is a sign for a people who give thought."
{% endhint %}

Honey has been used medicinally across cultures for millennia so this is not the remarkable part. What is remarkable is:

1. The verse specifies the bee is inspired by God using the same Arabic word (_Awha_) used for divine inspiration to prophets indicating bees operate by an intricate divine programming.
2. The grammatical forms used for the bee in Arabic are **feminine** the Quran consistently refers to the worker bee as female. Worker bees are all female. This was not known to beekeepers until the 18th century.
3. Modern science has confirmed that honey contains hydrogen peroxide, methylglyoxal, defensin-1, and other compounds with documented antibacterial, antifungal, and wound-healing properties.

## Responsible Limits What to Be Careful About

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Important scholarly caution:**

Not every alignment between a Quranic verse and a scientific fact is a genuine scientific miracle. The following are cautions from Muslim scholars who study this field carefully:

1. **Don't force modern meanings onto classical Arabic.** A verse must be examined in its classical Arabic context not retrofitted with a modern interpretation just because it sounds impressive.

2. **Science changes.** Scientific theories are revised. Founding an argument for Islam on a scientific theory that is later revised weakens, rather than strengthens, the case for the Quran. The strongest arguments are based on well-established, repeatedly confirmed scientific observations.

3. **The primary purpose of the Quran is guidance.** Using the Quran primarily as a "science book" misrepresents its purpose and may distract from its more fundamental message.

4. **Some claimed "Quranic scientific miracles" are weak.** This is acknowledged by serious Muslim scholars. The examples in this chapter represent the most widely accepted, carefully analyzed cases not the entire field of claimed miracles.
   {% endhint %}

## Key Takeaways

- The Quran contains descriptions of natural phenomena that align remarkably with modern scientific discoveries.
- The strongest examples include: the expanding universe, the Big Bang, embryonic development, the barrier between seas, the water cycle, pain receptors in skin, mountains as geological pegs, the origin of iron from space, and properties of honey.
- The standard of a genuine sign is that the description was impossible to arrive at through 7th-century knowledge yet is scientifically accurate.
- The Quran is primarily a book of guidance scientific signs are embedded within that, not the primary focus.
- Responsible scholarship requires caution not every claimed alignment is a genuine sign, and science itself changes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<details>

<summary>Did the Prophet have access to earlier Greek or Persian scientific texts?</summary>

This is a common objection. The Prophet was _Al-Ummi_ unlettered. He could not read. Beyond that, the specific descriptions in the Quran often _differ from_ and are more accurate than the Greek and Persian scientific understanding of the era. Greek embryology (Galen), for example, was substantially less accurate than the Quranic description. If the Prophet had borrowed from Greek sources, we would expect Greek errors to appear in the Quran they don't.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Doesn't the Quran say the sun sets in a "murky spring"?</summary>

This verse (Surah Al-Kahf 18:86) describes the narrative of Dhul-Qarnayn: _"Until, when he reached the setting of the sun, he found it setting in a spring of dark mud."_ This is a narrative verse describing what Dhul-Qarnayn _perceived_ from his vantage point it is not a cosmological statement about where the sun actually goes. Classical scholars understood this as a description of his perspective, like a person watching a sunset over the ocean it appears to "set" in the water. This is a literary description, not a cosmological claim.

</details>
