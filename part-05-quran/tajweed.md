# Tajweed The Rules of Quranic Recitation

**Difficulty:** Intermediate &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Read time:** ~8 min &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Part:** Part 5 The Quran

---

## What Is Tajweed?

**Tajweed** (تَجْوِيد) comes from the root _jawwada_ to make excellent, to improve. It is the set of rules governing the correct pronunciation of Quranic Arabic ensuring the Quran is recited as the Prophet recited it, as Jibreel (AS) taught it.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Al-Muzzammil 73:4**
**Translation:** "...And recite the Quran with measured recitation (_tartilan_)."
{% endhint %}

## Why Tajweed Matters

The Quran was revealed in Arabic and Arabic pronunciation is precise. A single letter mispronounced can change the meaning of a word. Tajweed preserves:

- The integrity of Allah's words
- The connection to the prophetic chain of transmission
- The beauty and spiritual impact of the recitation

## Core Rules of Tajweed

### 1. Makharij al-Huruf (Points of Articulation)

Each Arabic letter has a specific point of articulation in the throat, tongue, or lips. Many letters have no English equivalent:

- **ع** ('Ayn) deep throat constriction
- **ح** (Ha) soft throat breathiness
- **خ** (Kha) uvular friction
- **ص** (Sad) emphatic dental

### 2. Ghunnah (Nasal Sound)

The letters **ن** (Nun) and **م** (Meem) produce a nasal resonance (_Ghunnah_) of 2 beats in certain contexts.

### 3. Rules of Nun Sakinah and Tanwin

When ن (Nun) with sukoon or Tanwin occurs before the next letter:

- **Idgham** merging into the next letter
- **Iqlab** converting to Meem
- **Ikhfa'** concealment with Ghunnah
- **Izhar** clear pronunciation

### 4. Madd (Prolongation)

Long vowels (_Alif, Waw, Ya'_) are extended for 2, 4, or 6 beats depending on the context. Proper Madd is one of the most audible qualities distinguishing proper recitation.

### 5. Waqf (Stopping)

Rules for where to pause when stopping mid-recitation marked in the Quran with symbols (_م_ must stop, _ج_ permitted stop, _لا_ do not stop here).

## Learning Tajweed

**The essential principle:** Tajweed cannot be learned from a book alone it requires listening to and repeating after a qualified teacher. The sound must be heard and corrected.

**Resources:**

- **Local mosque Tajweed classes** most offer beginner classes
- **Quran.com** color-coded Tajweed Quran shows rules visually
- **YouTube: Mishary Rashid Alafasy, Abdul Basit, Maher Al-Muaiqly** models of Tajweed mastery
- **Tajweed Quran (color-coded Mushaf)** each rule shown in a different color

## The Reward of Struggling Recitation

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih al-Bukhari**
_"The one who recites the Quran proficiently will be with the noble, righteous scribes (angels). And the one who recites it with difficulty, while struggling with it, will have a double reward."_
{% endhint %}

Do not let imperfect pronunciation stop you from reciting. Start where you are. The struggle is itself rewarded.
