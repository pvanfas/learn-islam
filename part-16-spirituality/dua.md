# The Art of Du'a Supplication to Allah

Difficulty: Beginner | Part: Part 16 Islamic Spirituality

---

## What Is Du'a?

Du'a (supplication) is the direct conversation between the servant and the Lord asking, praising, seeking. The Prophet called it "the essence of worship."

"And your Lord said: Call upon Me, I will respond to you." (Surah Ghafir 40:60)

The Arabic word du'a comes from the root meaning to call out, to invite du'a is the believer calling out to Allah from wherever they are, in whatever condition.

---

## The Conditions That Aid Response

The Prophet described specific conditions under which du'a is most likely to be answered:

**Certainty of response.** "Call upon Allah while being certain of being answered." (Tirmidhi Hasan) Doubt in du'a is itself an obstacle.

**Presence of heart.** Du'a recited mechanically without the heart's engagement is far weaker than du'a made with full attention and sincerity.

**Clean hands.** The Prophet said: "Your Lord is Alive and Generous He feels shy to return empty the hands of His servant when he raises them to Him." (Abu Dawud Sahih)

**Avoiding prohibited sustenance.** The Prophet described a man making du'a with raised hands then noted his food, drink, and clothing were from haram sources "How then will his du'a be answered?" (Sahih Muslim)

---

## The Times of Answered Du'a

Certain times carry special promise of response:

The last third of the night when Allah descends to the lowest heaven.
After the obligatory prayers.
Between the Adhan and Iqamah.
The hour of Jumu'ah (Friday) specifically in the last hour before Maghrib.
When prostrating in Sujood "Make much supplication in prostration." (Sahih Muslim)
When breaking the fast (Iftar).
On the day of Arafah.
On Laylatul Qadr.
During rain.
When ill.
When traveling.

---

## The Du'as of the Prophets

The Quran preserves the du'as of the prophets as models:

Ibrahim (AS): "Our Lord, accept from us. Indeed You are the Hearing, the Knowing." (2:127)

Musa (AS): "My Lord, expand for me my chest, and ease for me my task." (20:25-26)

Ayyub (AS): "Adversity has touched me, and you are the Most Merciful of the merciful." (21:83)

Yunus (AS): "There is no deity except You; exalted are You. Indeed, I have been of the wrongdoers." (21:87)

Zakariyyah (AS): "My Lord, do not leave me alone, and You are the best of inheritors." (21:89)

---

## When Du'a Appears Unanswered

The Prophet said: "There is no Muslim who calls upon Allah with any du'a that is not a sin or severing of family ties, but Allah gives him one of three things: He either answers his du'a, or stores it for him in the Hereafter, or diverts from him a harm equivalent to it." The Companions said: "Then we will ask for much." He said: "Allah is more than that." (Musnad Ahmad Sahih)

Nothing is lost. Every sincere du'a is received and responded to sometimes in the form requested, sometimes in the form that is better.
