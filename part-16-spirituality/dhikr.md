# Dhikr Remembrance of Allah

**Difficulty:** Beginner &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Read time:** ~11 min &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Part:** Part 16 Islamic Spirituality

---

## In This Chapter

> Dhikr the remembrance of Allah is the heartbeat of Islamic spiritual life. This chapter explains what dhikr is, why it is the nourishment the heart cannot survive without, the most powerful phrases of remembrance, the morning and evening adhkar, and how to integrate dhikr into ordinary daily life.

## Quranic Foundation

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Ar-Ra'd 13:28**

_أَلَا بِذِكْرِ اللَّهِ تَطْمَئِنُّ الْقُلُوبُ_

**Translation:** "Verily, in the remembrance of Allah do hearts find rest."

_This is one of the most frequently quoted verses in the Quran because it answers one of the deepest questions of the human experience: where does the heart find its peace? Not in wealth, not in status, not in pleasure but in the remembrance of the One who created it._
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Al-Ahzab 33:4142**

**Translation:** "O you who have believed, remember Allah with much remembrance and exalt Him morning and evening."
{% endhint %}

## Why Dhikr Is the Life of the Heart

The Prophet said:

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih al-Bukhari**

_"The example of the one who remembers his Lord and the one who does not is like the example of the living and the dead."_
{% endhint %}

Dhikr is not a supplementary practice it is the spiritual oxygen of the believer's life. Without it, the heart becomes hard, forgetful, and susceptible to the diseases of arrogance, envy, and attachment to dunya.

The great scholar Ibn al-Qayyim (RA) wrote in _Al-Wabil al-Sayyib_: _"Dhikr is nourishment for the heart the heart without dhikr is like the body without food."_

## The Most Beloved Words to Allah

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih Muslim**

The Prophet was asked: _"Which words are most beloved to Allah?"_
He said: _"Subhanallahi wa bihamdihi, Subhanallahil-'azim."_

**Translation:** "Glory be to Allah and with His praise; glory be to Allah, the Most Great."\*

He added: _"They are light on the tongue, heavy on the Scale, and beloved to the Most Merciful."_
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih al-Bukhari**

Abu Hurairah (RA) reported: _"Saying Subhanallah 100 times in the morning and 100 times in the evening is equivalent to a hundred battles of Jihad and no one comes on the Day of Judgement with better deeds, unless they have done the same or more."_
{% endhint %}

## The Morning Adhkar (After Fajr)

Recite these after the Fajr prayer they establish protection and Barakah for the entire day.

### 1. Ayat al-Kursi (×1)

Protection through the morning until the evening.

### 2. Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq, An-Nas (×3 each)

_"Whoever recites them three times in the morning and three times in the evening they suffice him against everything."_ (Abu Dawud Sahih)

### 3. The Master of Istighfar (_Sayyid al-Istighfar_)

{% hint style="info" %}
_اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ رَبِّي لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ، خَلَقْتَنِي وَأَنَا عَبْدُكَ، وَأَنَا عَلَىٰ عَهْدِكَ وَوَعْدِكَ مَا اسْتَطَعْتُ، أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا صَنَعْتُ، أَبُوءُ لَكَ بِنِعْمَتِكَ عَلَيَّ، وَأَبُوءُ لَكَ بِذَنْبِي فَاغْفِرْ لِي فَإِنَّهُ لَا يَغْفِرُ الذُّنُوبَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ_

**Transliteration:** _Allahumma anta rabbi la ilaha illa ant, khalaqtani wa ana 'abduk, wa ana 'ala 'ahdika wa wa'dika mastata't, a'udhu bika min sharri ma sana't, abu'u laka bini'matika 'alayya, wa abu'u laka bidhanbi, faghfir li fa innahu la yaghfirudh-dhunuba illa ant._

**Translation:** "O Allah, You are my Lord there is no god but You. You created me and I am Your servant. I am upon Your covenant and Your promise as best as I can. I seek refuge in You from the evil of what I have done. I acknowledge Your blessing upon me and I acknowledge my sin so forgive me, for no one forgives sins except You."

_The Prophet said: "Whoever says this in the morning with certainty and dies before evening will be from the people of Paradise. And whoever says it in the evening with certainty and dies before morning will be from the people of Paradise."_ (Sahih al-Bukhari)
{% endhint %}

### 4. Protection Dua (×3 morning and evening)

{% hint style="info" %}
_بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الَّذِي لَا يَضُرُّ مَعَ اسْمِهِ شَيْءٌ فِي الْأَرْضِ وَلَا فِي السَّمَاءِ وَهُوَ السَّمِيعُ الْعَلِيمُ_

**Transliteration:** _Bismillahil-ladhi la yadurru ma'asmihi shay'un fil-ardi wa la fis-sama'i wa huwas-sami'ul-'alim._

**Translation:** "In the name of Allah with whose name nothing on earth or in heaven can cause harm. And He is the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing."

_"Whoever says this three times in the morning, nothing will harm him until evening. Whoever says it three times in the evening, nothing will harm him until morning."_ (Abu Dawud Sahih)
{% endhint %}

### 5. Subhanallah, Alhamdulillah, Allahu Akbar (×33 each)

After every obligatory prayer as taught in the Tasbihat prayer.

## The Evening Adhkar (After Asr / At Maghrib)

The evening adhkar mirror the morning ones creating a complete cycle of divine protection and remembrance around the day.

All the morning adhkar above are also recited in the evening with the intention shifted to evening protection.

### Additional Evening Dua:

{% hint style="info" %}
_اللَّهُمَّ بِكَ أَمْسَيْنَا وَبِكَ أَصْبَحْنَا وَبِكَ نَحْيَا وَبِكَ نَمُوتُ وَإِلَيْكَ الْمَصِير_

**Transliteration:** _Allahumma bika amsayna wa bika asbahna wa bika nahya wa bika namutu wa ilaykal-masir._

**Translation:** "O Allah, by You we enter the evening and by You we enter the morning, by You we live and by You we die, and to You is the final destination." _(Abu Dawud Hasan)_
{% endhint %}

## Dhikr After Every Prayer The Tasbihat

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih Muslim**

_"Whoever glorifies Allah (Subhanallah) 33 times after each prayer, praises Allah (Alhamdulillah) 33 times, and declares Allah's greatness (Allahu Akbar) 33 times that is 99 and completes the 100 with: 'La ilaha illallahu wahdahu la sharika lah, lahul-mulku wa lahul-hamd, wa huwa 'ala kulli shay'in qadir' his sins will be forgiven even if they are like the foam of the sea."_
{% endhint %}

## Sending Blessings on the Prophet (Salawat)

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Al-Ahzab 33:56**

**Translation:** "Indeed, Allah and His angels send blessing upon the Prophet. O you who have believed, ask blessing upon him and ask peace for him."
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih Muslim**

_"Whoever sends one blessing upon me Allah sends ten blessings upon him."_
{% endhint %}

**The short form:** _"Allahumma salli 'ala Muhammad"_ "O Allah, send blessings upon Muhammad"

**The full Ibrahimiyyah form:** Recited in every prayer's Tashahhud see [How to Pray](how-to-pray.md) for the full text.

The best day to increase Salawat: **Friday** the Prophet commanded abundant Salawat on Fridays.

## Integrating Dhikr Into Daily Life

Dhikr does not require stillness or isolation it is designed for a life in motion:

| Activity                    | Dhikr                                                         |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Waking up                   | _Alhamdulillahil-ladhi ahyana ba'da ma amatana..._            |
| Dressing                    | _Bismillah_ + dua for new clothing                            |
| Eating                      | _Bismillah_ (before), _Alhamdulillah_ (after)                 |
| Leaving home                | _Bismillah, tawakkaltu 'ala Allah..._                         |
| Driving                     | _Bismillah_ + vehicle dua (Surah Az-Zukhruf 43:13)            |
| Entering the mosque         | Dua for entering                                              |
| In traffic / waiting        | _Subhanallah, Alhamdulillah, La ilaha illallah, Allahu Akbar_ |
| Before sleeping             | Full bedtime adhkar routine                                   |
| After waking from nightmare | _A'udhu billahi minash-shaytan_                               |
| When anxious                | _La hawla wa la quwwata illa billah_                          |
| When pleased                | _Alhamdulillah_                                               |
| When surprised              | _Subhanallah_                                                 |
| When making a promise       | _Inshallah_                                                   |

## Key Takeaways

- Dhikr is the nourishment of the heart the heart without it is like the body without food.
- The most beloved words to Allah: _"Subhanallahi wa bihamdihi, Subhanallahil-'azim"_ light on the tongue, heavy on the Scale.
- Morning and evening adhkar form a complete daily shield of divine protection Ayat al-Kursi, Al-Mu'awwidhat, Sayyid al-Istighfar, and more.
- After every obligatory prayer: Subhanallah (×33), Alhamdulillah (×33), Allahu Akbar (×33), then the closing Tahlil sins forgiven even if like the foam of the sea.
- Sending Salawat on the Prophet returns tenfold increase it especially on Fridays.
- Dhikr is designed for a life in motion every moment of the day has a remembrance attached to it.
