## Additional Diseases of the Heart

Beyond the six major diseases covered in the main chapter, Islamic scholars identify additional conditions:

**Hasad vs. Ghibtah.** The distinction between forbidden envy (hasad wishing the blessing be removed from another) and permitted emulation (ghibtah wishing you had the same blessing without wishing it removed from the other) is crucial. Only hasad is forbidden; ghibtah in good matters is permitted and can be motivating.

**Wahn Love of This World Combined with Hatred of Death.** The Prophet described the condition of weakness in which nations fall upon the Muslim community as "wahn" a disease of the heart that combines excessive love of the present world with fear of death. This is the spiritual root of Muslim geopolitical weakness according to the Hadith.

**Hiqd Grudge-bearing.** Holding grudges refusing to forgive, nurturing resentment is described as burning the heart from within. The Prophet said: "Do not harbor mutual hatred, do not turn your backs on each other, do not artificially inflate prices, and be servants of Allah as brothers." (Sahih al-Bukhari)

**'Ujb Self-admiration.** Related to kibr (pride) but distinct: ujb is satisfaction with one's own deeds and qualities to the point of forgetting that all good comes from Allah. The Prophet warned against it explicitly.
