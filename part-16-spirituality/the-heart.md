# The Heart in Islam Diseases and Their Cures

**Difficulty:** Intermediate &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Read time:** ~12 min &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Part:** Part 16 Islamic Spirituality

---

## In This Chapter

> Islamic spiritual tradition places the heart at the center of the believer's life. The heart's health determines the quality of worship, character, and relationship with Allah. This chapter covers the Islamic concept of the heart, its diseases as described in Quran and Sunnah, their cures, and the path of purification (_Tazkiyah al-Nafs_).

---

## The Centrality of the Heart

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih al-Bukhari and Muslim**

The Prophet said: _"Verily in the body there is a piece of flesh if it is sound, the entire body is sound. If it is corrupt, the entire body is corrupt. Verily it is the heart."_
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Ash-Shu'ara 26:89**

**Translation:** "The day when neither wealth nor children will benefit except one who comes to Allah with a sound heart (_qalbun salim_)."
{% endhint %}

The _Qalbun Salim_ the sound, pure heart is what Allah is looking for on the Day of Judgement. Not wealth. Not children. Not even necessarily beautiful worship form without a present heart. The sound heart.

---

## The Three States of the Heart

Islamic scholars describe three conditions the heart may be in:

| State           | Arabic        | Description                                                 |
| --------------- | ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Sound heart** | Qalbun Salim  | Free from diseases; turned toward Allah; the goal           |
| **Dead heart**  | Qalbun Mayyit | No light of faith; in a state of heedlessness of Allah      |
| **Sick heart**  | Qalbun Marid  | Between the two has Iman but also has diseases weakening it |

Most practicing Muslims have a sick heart not dead, but not yet sound. The work of Tazkiyah is to move from sick toward sound.

---

## The Major Diseases of the Heart

Imam al-Ghazali's _Ihya Ulum al-Din_ and Ibn al-Qayyim's _Madarij al-Salikin_ are the two greatest classical works on the diseases of the heart. The most destructive diseases:

---

### Disease 1 - Pride (_Kibr / Ujub_)

**Definition:** Seeing oneself as superior to others; refusing to accept truth when it contradicts one's self-image.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih Muslim**

_"Whoever has even an atom's weight of pride in his heart will not enter Paradise."_
The Companions asked: "What if a man likes his clothes to be clean and his shoes to be good?"
He replied: _"Allah is Beautiful and loves beauty. Pride means rejecting the truth and looking down on people."_
{% endhint %}

**Its source:** The root of all sin it was the first sin ever committed when Iblees refused to prostrate to Adam out of pride (_"I am better than him you created me from fire and created him from clay."_)

**Its cure:**

- Regular contemplation of one's sins and shortcomings
- Remembering one's origins (_"From a drop of despised fluid"_ Surah Al-Mursalat 77:20)
- Serving others especially the poor and vulnerable
- Regular Sujood the physical humbling of the highest part of the body
- Seeking knowledge of one's own ignorance

---

### Disease 2 - Envy (_Hasad_)

**Definition:** Wishing that a blessing Allah has given another person be removed from them even if it does not come to you.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih al-Bukhari**

_"Beware of envy, for envy devours good deeds the way fire devours dry wood."_
{% endhint %}

The Prophet commanded seeking refuge from the envier in Al-Falaq: _"...and from the evil of an envier when he envies."_ (Surah Al-Falaq 113:5)

**Its cure:**

- Recognizing that the envied person's blessing is Allah's gift and resenting it is resenting Allah's decree
- Actively making dua FOR the person you feel envy toward this dismantles the envy from inside
- Reflecting on your own blessings Shukr is the antidote to Hasad
- Understanding that Allah's distribution of blessings is based on wisdom _"Allah is more knowing of where to place His message"_ (Surah Al-An'am 6:124)

---

### Disease 3 - Riya' (Showing Off / Ostentation)

**Definition:** Performing acts of worship or good deeds for the praise and approval of people rather than for Allah alone.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih Muslim**

The Prophet said: _"What I fear most for you is minor Shirk."_ He was asked: "What is minor Shirk?" He said: _"Showing off. On the Day of Judgement, Allah will say to those who showed off: 'Go to those for whom you used to perform see if you find any reward with them.'"_
{% endhint %}

Riya' is called the "hidden Shirk" (_Ash-Shirk al-Khafi_) because it is almost invisible it can corrupt the most outwardly beautiful act of worship.

**Its cure:**

- Making the intention (_Niyyah_) private and sincere before every act
- Avoiding sharing worship on social media when the motivation is unclear
- Noticing when you feel disappointed that no one recognized your good deed and using that feeling as a mirror
- Regular Muhasabah (self-accounting) did I do that for Allah or for praise?

---

### Disease 4 - Hubb al-Dunya (Excessive Love of the World)

**Definition:** Prioritizing worldly gain, comfort, and status over the commands of Allah and the Hereafter.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sunan Abu Dawud (Sahih)**

_"The love of the world is the origin of every sin."_
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Al-Munafiqun 63:9**

**Translation:** "O you who have believed, let not your wealth and your children divert you from the remembrance of Allah. And whoever does that then those are the losers."
{% endhint %}

**Its cure:**

- Contemplating death the Prophet recommended frequent remembrance of death (_"Increase in remembrance of the destroyer of pleasures death"_) (Tirmidhi Hasan)
- Visiting the sick and attending funerals
- Zuhd (detachment from the world) not asceticism, but not allowing the world to own your heart
- Studying the Hereafter making it real and vivid in the mind

---

### Disease 5 - Anger (_Ghadab_)

**Definition:** Uncontrolled anger that leads to harmful speech, actions, and loss of reason.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih al-Bukhari**

A man asked the Prophet for advice. He said: _"Do not become angry."_ The man asked again. He said: _"Do not become angry."_ He asked a third time. He said: _"Do not become angry."_
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih al-Bukhari**

_"The strong person is not the one who overcomes others by wrestling. The strong person is the one who controls themselves when angry."_
{% endhint %}

**Its cure:**

- Say _"A'udhu billahi minash-shaytan"_ seek refuge in Allah
- Change your physical position if standing, sit; if sitting, lie down
- Make Wudu the Prophet said anger is from fire and water extinguishes fire
- Silence not speaking when angry prevents the words that cannot be taken back

---

### Disease 6 - Kibr of the Scholars Self-Deception in Worship

This is a subtle disease of the "religious" person believing that one's worship and knowledge have elevated one above others. It combines Kibr and Riya' a particularly dangerous combination.

**The cure:** Remembering that all good is from Allah _"And my guidance is only through Allah"_ (Surah Hud 11:88). The one who prays well, fasts beautifully, and knows much all of it was given to them. Their gratitude should be greater than their sense of superiority.

---

## Tazkiyah The Path of Purification

_Tazkiyah al-Nafs_ (purification of the soul) is the Islamic discipline of working on the heart over time removing its diseases and developing its virtues.

The Quran mentions it as one of the Prophet's primary missions:

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Al-Jumu'ah 62:2**

**Translation:** "He it is who sent among the unlettered people a Messenger from among themselves reciting to them His verses and purifying them (_yuzakkihim_)..."
{% endhint %}

### The Tools of Tazkiyah

| Tool                            | How it purifies                                                      |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Salah with Khushu'**          | Continuous return to Allah breaking the world's grip on the heart    |
| **Fasting**                     | Disciplines the nafs breaks the habit of giving desire what it wants |
| **Dhikr**                       | Fills the heart with light gradually displacing its diseases         |
| **Quran recitation and study**  | The Quran is "a cure for what is in the chests" (Surah Yunus 10:57)  |
| **Muhasabah (self-accounting)** | Regular honest review of one's spiritual state                       |
| **Suhbah (righteous company)**  | The Prophet said a person is on the deen of their closest friend     |
| **Seeking forgiveness**         | Istighfar before sleep wipes the day's spiritual accumulation        |
| **Contemplation of death**      | Puts everything into perspective dissolves pride and attachment      |

---

## Key Takeaways

- The heart is the center of Islamic spiritual life its soundness determines the soundness of all deeds.
- The three states: dead (no faith), sick (faith with diseases), and sound (the goal Qalbun Salim).
- The major heart diseases: Pride (_Kibr_), Envy (_Hasad_), Showing Off (_Riya'_), Love of the World (_Hubb al-Dunya_), Anger (_Ghadab_).
- Each disease has specific cures drawn from the Quran and Sunnah not vague spiritual effort but specific practices.
- Tazkiyah is the ongoing discipline of purifying the heart through worship, dhikr, fasting, righteous company, and self-accounting.
- The Quran is itself described as a cure for what is in the chest regular recitation with understanding is the most direct medicine for the heart.
