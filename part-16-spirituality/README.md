## What You Will Learn in This Part

Islam is not only a religion of correct beliefs (Aqeedah) and correct practice (Fiqh) it is a religion of the heart. The interior dimension of Islam the purification of the soul, the cultivation of virtue, and the drawing near to Allah is called _Tazkiyah_ (purification) and _Tasawwuf_ (Islamic spirituality).

This part covers the foundational concepts: the heart's health and its diseases, the key virtues of Islamic character, the practice of Dhikr (remembrance of Allah), the morning and evening adhkar, an introduction to the Sufi tradition, and the masterwork of al-Ghazali. Together, these chapters provide the tools for the most important work any Muslim can do the work on their own heart.
