## The Arabian Peninsula The Origin of Islam

Makkah (Mecca): 21.3891 N, 39.8579 E the city of the Ka'bah, the Masjid al-Haram, and the birthplace of the Prophet Muhammad.

Madinah (Medina): 24.5247 N, 39.5692 E the city of the Prophet's migration, Masjid al-Nabawi, and his burial place.

Mount Hira (Jabal al-Nur): Near Makkah the mountain cave where the first revelation descended.

Mount Thawr: South of Makkah the cave where the Prophet and Abu Bakr hid during the Hijrah.

Mina, Muzdalifah, Arafah: The plains east of Makkah used during the Hajj pilgrimage.

Ta'if: 90 km east of Makkah the city whose inhabitants drove out the Prophet; now a major Saudi city.

## The Broader Islamic World Key Sites

Jerusalem (Al-Quds): 31.7683 N, 35.2137 E Al-Masjid al-Aqsa, the third holiest mosque in Islam; the site of the Prophet's Night Journey; first Qiblah direction before Makkah.

Damascus: 33.5138 N, 36.2765 E capital of the Umayyad Caliphate; site of the white minaret of the Umayyad Mosque where scholars say Isa AS will descend.

Baghdad: 33.3152 N, 44.3661 E capital of the Abbasid Caliphate; site of the House of Wisdom; sacked by Mongols in 1258.

Cairo: 30.0444 N, 31.2357 E Al-Azhar University (970 CE); seat of Fatimid Caliphate.

Istanbul (Constantinople): 41.0082 N, 28.9784 E conquered by the Ottomans in 1453; the great mosques of Hagia Sophia and the Blue Mosque.

Fez, Morocco: 34.0333 N, 5.0 W Al-Qarawiyyin University (859 CE), the world's oldest continuously operating university.

Cordoba (Qurtubah), Spain: 37.8882 N, 4.7794 W capital of Andalusian Islam; the Great Mosque of Cordoba.

Samarkand, Uzbekistan: 39.6542 N, 66.9597 E center of Central Asian Islamic civilization; where Imam al-Bukhari was born.
