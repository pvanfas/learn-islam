## The Arabian Peninsula: The Origin of Islam

| Location                      | Coordinates          | Description                                                                           |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Makkah (Mecca)**            | 21.3891°N, 39.8579°E | City of the Ka'bah, the Masjid al-Haram, and the birthplace of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ |
| **Madinah (Medina)**          | 24.5247°N, 39.5692°E | City of the Prophet's migration, Masjid al-Nabawi, and his burial place               |
| **Mount Hira (Jabal al-Nur)** | Near Makkah          | The mountain cave where the first revelation descended                                |
| **Mount Thawr**               | South of Makkah      | The cave where the Prophet ﷺ and Abu Bakr hid during the Hijrah                       |
| **Mina, Muzdalifah, Arafah**  | East of Makkah       | The plains used during the Hajj pilgrimage                                            |
| **Ta'if**                     | 90 km east of Makkah | The city whose inhabitants drove out the Prophet ﷺ; now a major Saudi city            |

## The Broader Islamic World: Key Sites

| Location                      | Coordinates          | Description                                                                                                           |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Jerusalem (Al-Quds)**       | 31.7683°N, 35.2137°E | Al-Masjid al-Aqsa, the third holiest mosque in Islam; site of the Prophet's Night Journey; first Qiblah before Makkah |
| **Damascus**                  | 33.5138°N, 36.2765°E | Capital of the Umayyad Caliphate; site of the Umayyad Mosque's white minaret, where scholars say Isa AS will descend  |
| **Baghdad**                   | 33.3152°N, 44.3661°E | Capital of the Abbasid Caliphate; site of the House of Wisdom; sacked by the Mongols in 1258                          |
| **Cairo**                     | 30.0444°N, 31.2357°E | Home of Al-Azhar University (970 CE); seat of the Fatimid Caliphate                                                   |
| **Istanbul (Constantinople)** | 41.0082°N, 28.9784°E | Conquered by the Ottomans in 1453; home of Hagia Sophia and the Blue Mosque                                           |
| **Fez, Morocco**              | 34.0333°N, 5.0°W     | Home of Al-Qarawiyyin University (859 CE), the world's oldest continuously operating university                       |
| **Cordoba (Qurtubah), Spain** | 37.8882°N, 4.7794°W  | Capital of Andalusian Islam; home of the Great Mosque of Cordoba                                                      |
| **Samarkand, Uzbekistan**     | 39.6542°N, 66.9597°E | Center of Central Asian Islamic civilization; birthplace of Imam al-Bukhari                                           |
