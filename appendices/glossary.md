# Glossary of Arabic Islamic Terms

**Part:** Appendices &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Use:** Reference

---

> This glossary covers the key Arabic terms used throughout this guide. Terms are listed alphabetically by their English transliteration. Arabic script is provided alongside each entry.

---

## A

**Ahl al-Kitab** أَهْلُ الْكِتَاب
People of the Book. The term used in the Quran for Jews and Christians, who received earlier divine scriptures.

**Ahl al-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah** أَهْلُ السُّنَّةِ وَالْجَمَاعَة
The People of the Sunnah and the Community. The mainstream tradition of Islamic belief and practice, followed by the vast majority of Muslims.

**Akhirah** الْآخِرَة
The Hereafter. The eternal life after death encompassing resurrection, judgement, and the final destinations of Jannah and Jahannam.

**Alhamdulillah** الْحَمْدُ لِلَّه
"All praise is for Allah." One of the most frequently recited phrases in a Muslim's day used to express gratitude.

**Allah** اللّٰه
The proper name of God in Arabic. Not "a god" but the One God, the Creator, the Sustainer of all existence. Used by Arab Muslims and Christians alike.

**Allahu Akbar** اللَّهُ أَكْبَر
"Allah is the Greatest." The Takbir used at the beginning of prayer and as a general expression of greatness of God.

**Amanah** أَمَانَة
Trustworthiness. One of the essential qualities of prophets. Also used for trusts and responsibilities in general Islamic ethics.

**Amin** آمِين
"Ameen" said after dua or at the end of Al-Fatihah in prayer. Means "O Allah, respond to this."

**Aqeedah** عَقِيدَة
Creed or theology. The set of beliefs that a Muslim's heart is anchored to. The six pillars of Iman are the core of Islamic Aqeedah.

**Arkan al-Islam** أَرْكَانُ الإِسْلَام
The pillars of Islam the five foundational practices: Shahada, Salah, Zakat, Sawm, Hajj.

**Arkan al-Iman** أَرْكَانُ الإِيمَان
The pillars of Faith the six beliefs: in Allah, His Angels, His Books, His Messengers, the Last Day, and Divine Decree.

**Asr** الْعَصْر
The afternoon obligatory prayer. Also the name of Surah 103.

**Awliya** أَوْلِيَاء
Allies, friends, or helpers. Often used for those close to Allah (_Awliya Allah_).

**Awrah** عَوْرَة
The parts of the body that must be covered. Different requirements for men and women.

**Ayah** آيَة
A verse of the Quran. Also means "sign" natural phenomena are called Ayat of Allah.

---

## B

**Barakah** بَرَكَة
Divine blessing a multiplied and sustained goodness in time, wealth, or deeds. Sought through Sunnah practices.

**Barzakh** الْبَرْزَخ
The intermediate state between death and resurrection. The soul remains in Barzakh until the Day of Judgement.

**Bid'ah** بِدْعَة
Religious innovation an act of worship introduced into the religion without basis in the Quran or Sunnah.

**Bismillah** بِسْمِ اللَّه
"In the name of Allah." Said before beginning any action of significance eating, working, traveling.

---

## D

**Da'wah** دَعْوَة
Invitation to Islam. The act of sharing Islamic knowledge and calling people to the religion.

**Dajjal** الدَّجَّال
The False Messiah the one-eyed deceiver who will appear before the Day of Judgement.

**Deen** دِين
A complete way of life / religion. Islam is a _Deen_ not merely a set of rituals but a comprehensive system of living.

**Dhikr** ذِكْر
Remembrance of Allah. Specific words and phrases _Subhanallah_, _Alhamdulillah_, _Allahu Akbar_, _La ilaha illallah_ recited to maintain awareness of Allah.

**Dua** دُعَاء
Supplication speaking directly to Allah. Described by the Prophet as the essence of worship.

**Dunya** الدُّنْيَا
The worldly life this life as opposed to the Akhirah (Hereafter). The word literally means "the lower" or "the nearer."

---

## F

**Fajr** الْفَجْر
The dawn prayer the first of the five daily obligatory prayers.

**Fard** فَرْض
Obligatory. An act whose performance is required and whose omission is sinful.

**Fatwa** فَتْوَى
A scholarly legal opinion given by a qualified Islamic scholar (_Mufti_) on a specific question.

**Fiqh** فِقْه
Islamic jurisprudence. The detailed study and derivation of legal rulings from the Quran and Sunnah.

**Fitnah** فِتْنَة
Trial, tribulation, or temptation. Also refers to chaos, civil strife, or anything that tests a person's faith.

**Fitrah** فِطْرَة
The innate natural disposition of human beings toward truth, toward God, toward goodness. Every person is born on Fitrah.

---

## G

**Ghusl** غُسْل
The major ritual bath full-body purification required after sexual intercourse, ejaculation, and the end of menstruation.

---

## H

**Hadith** حَدِيث
A recorded saying, action, or approval of the Prophet Muhammad . The Hadith collections are the second primary source of Islamic knowledge after the Quran.

**Halal** حَلَال
Permissible. What is allowed in Islamic law food, actions, and conduct.

**Haram** حَرَام
Prohibited. What is forbidden in Islamic law.

**Hawl** حَوْل
One complete lunar year. Zakat becomes due after wealth has been held for one full Hawl above the Nisab.

**Haya** حَيَاء
Modesty a deeply valued quality in Islam. Described as a branch of faith. Covers modesty of dress, gaze, speech, and conduct.

**Hijab** حِجَاب
Literally "barrier" or "partition." In modern usage, the headscarf worn by Muslim women.

**Hijrah** هِجْرَة
Migration. Specifically, the migration of the Prophet from Makkah to Madinah in 622 CE the event that marks the beginning of the Islamic calendar.

---

## I

**Ibadah** عِبَادَة
Worship. In Islam, worship is broader than prayer it encompasses any act done sincerely for the sake of Allah.

**Ihram** إِحْرَام
The sacred state entered for Hajj or Umrah specific dress, intention, and restrictions on conduct.

**Ijma** إِجْمَاع
Scholarly consensus. The third source of Islamic law unanimous agreement of qualified scholars.

**Ijtihad** اِجْتِهَاد
Independent legal reasoning by a qualified scholar in areas not directly addressed by Quran or Sunnah.

**Imam** إِمَام
Leader. The person who leads prayer in congregation. Also used for great scholars of Islamic history.

**Iman** إِيمَان
Faith belief in the six pillars of Islamic creed.

**Inshallah** إِن شَاءَ اللَّه
"If Allah wills." Said when expressing intent to do something in the future acknowledging that all outcomes are in Allah's hands.

**Isha** الْعِشَاء
The night prayer the fifth and final obligatory daily prayer.

**Isnad** إِسْنَاد
The chain of narrators in a Hadith from the collector back to the person who witnessed the Prophet .

**Istigfar** اِسْتِغْفَار
Seeking forgiveness from Allah. Saying _"Astaghfirullah"_ "I seek Allah's forgiveness."

**Istikhara** اِسْتِخَارَة
The prayer of seeking guidance when making a decision.

---

## J

**Jahiliyyah** جَاهِلِيَّة
The Age of Ignorance pre-Islamic Arabia. Also used to describe any state of ignorance of divine guidance.

**Jama'ah** جَمَاعَة
The community the main body of Muslims. Also used for congregation prayer.

**Janazah** جَنَازَة
Funeral prayer a specific prayer performed for a deceased Muslim.

**Jannah** جَنَّة
Paradise. The final destination of the believers described in the Quran in vivid detail.

**Jahannam** جَهَنَّم
Hell. The place of punishment for those who rejected truth and died without repentance.

**Jihad** جِهَاد
Striving or struggling in the path of Allah. Encompasses internal struggle against the ego, struggle in da'wah, and in specific contexts, armed struggle under strict conditions.

**Jibreel** جِبْرِيل
The Archangel Gabriel the angel of divine revelation who delivered the Quran to the Prophet .

**Jumu'ah** جُمُعَة
Friday the day of the weekly congregational prayer that replaces Dhuhr for men.

---

## K

**Ka'bah** الْكَعْبَة
The cube-shaped structure in the center of Masjid al-Haram in Makkah built by Ibrahim (AS) and Ismail (AS) the direction Muslims face in prayer.

**Kafir** كَافِر
One who disbelieves specifically one who rejects truth after it has been made clear. A loaded term requiring precision in use.

**Khilafah** خِلَافَة
Stewardship or vicegerency. The Quran describes humans as _Khulafa_ (stewards) of the earth responsible for its care.

**Khushu** خُشُوع
Concentration and humility in prayer the internal quality of full presence before Allah that makes prayer meaningful.

---

## L

**La ilaha illallah** لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّه
"There is no god but Allah." The first half of the Shahada the most important statement in Islam.

**Laylatul Qadr** لَيْلَةُ الْقَدْر
The Night of Power in the last ten nights of Ramadan. Better than a thousand months in reward.

---

## M

**Madhab** مَذْهَب
A school of Islamic jurisprudence. The four Sunni madhabs are Hanafi, Maliki, Shafi'i, and Hanbali.

**Maghrib** الْمَغْرِب
The sunset prayer the fourth obligatory daily prayer. Consists of 3 Rak'ahs.

**Mahdi** الْمَهْدِي
"The guided one." A figure from the end times a Muslim leader who will unite the believers against the Dajjal.

**Makkah** مَكَّة
The holiest city in Islam birthplace of the Prophet and site of the Ka'bah.

**Madinah** الْمَدِينَة
"The city" formally Al-Madinah al-Munawwarah (the Illuminated City). The second holiest city in Islam where the Prophet migrated and is buried.

**Maqasid al-Sharia** مَقَاصِدُ الشَّرِيعَة
The objectives of Islamic law to protect five essentials: religion, life, intellect, lineage, and wealth.

**Matn** مَتْن
The text of a Hadith what the Prophet actually said or did.

**Mihrab** مِحْرَاب
The prayer niche in a mosque indicating the direction of Makkah (Qiblah).

**Miqat** مِيقَات
The designated boundary points around Makkah at which pilgrims must enter the state of Ihram.

**Mizan** الْمِيزَان
The Scale on which deeds will be weighed on the Day of Judgement.

**Mu'min** مُؤْمِن
A believer one with complete and sincere faith.

**Muhaddith** مُحَدِّث
A scholar of Hadith one who has mastered the sciences of Hadith collection and criticism.

---

## N

**Nabi** نَبِيّ
A Prophet one who receives divine revelation.

**Nafs** نَفْس
The self or soul. Islamic spirituality focuses on the purification (_Tazkiyah_) of the nafs.

**Najasah** نَجَاسَة
Physical impurity on the body, clothing, or place of prayer.

**Niyyah** نِيَّة
Intention. The internal act of heart that transforms ordinary actions into worship. _"Actions are by their intentions."_

**Niqab** نِقَاب
The face veil worn by some Muslim women subject to scholarly debate regarding its obligation.

**Nisab** نِصَاب
The minimum threshold of wealth above which Zakat becomes obligatory.

---

## Q

**Qada'** قَضَاء
Divine judgement what comes to pass by Allah's decree.

**Qadar** قَدَر
Divine decree the sixth pillar of Islamic faith. The belief that Allah has complete knowledge of all things and that everything occurs within His will.

**Qiblah** قِبْلَة
The direction of the Ka'bah in Makkah the direction Muslims face in prayer.

**Qiyas** قِيَاس
Analogical reasoning the fourth source of Islamic law.

**Quran** الْقُرْآن
The literal word of Allah revealed to the Prophet Muhammad over 23 years in Arabic. The primary source of Islamic knowledge.

---

## R

**Rak'ah** رَكْعَة
One unit of Islamic prayer consisting of standing, bowing, prostrating, and sitting.

**Ramadan** رَمَضَان
The ninth month of the Islamic lunar calendar the month of obligatory fasting.

**Rasul** رَسُول
A Messenger a prophet sent with a new scripture or law.

**Riba** رِبَا
Interest or usury prohibited in Islamic law.

**Rida** رِضَا
Contentment with Allah's decree. A high spiritual station.

**Ruh** رُوح
The spirit or soul breathed into Adam (AS) by Allah.

---

## S

**Sabr** صَبْر
Patient perseverance one of the highest virtues in Islamic ethics. Mentioned over 90 times in the Quran.

**Sadaqah** صَدَقَة
Voluntary charity. Broader than Zakat anything given for the sake of Allah.

**Sadaqah Jariyah** صَدَقَة جَارِيَة
Ongoing charity one that continues to generate reward after the giver's death (e.g., a well, a school, knowledge that is taught and passed on).

**Sahih** صَحِيح
Authentic the highest grade of Hadith.

**Salah** صَلَاة
The ritual prayer the second pillar of Islam. Obligatory five times daily.

**Salaf** السَّلَف
The righteous predecessors the first three generations of Muslims.

**Sawm** صَوْم
Fasting the fourth pillar of Islam. Obligatory during the month of Ramadan.

**Shahada** الشَّهَادَة
The declaration of faith the first pillar of Islam. _"I bear witness that there is no god but Allah, and that Muhammad is His messenger."_

**Sharia** الشَّرِيعَة
Islamic law derived from the Quran and Sunnah. Literally means "the path to water" the way to life.

**Shirk** شِرْك
Associating partners with Allah the greatest sin in Islam. The opposite of Tawhid.

**Shukr** شُكْر
Gratitude active recognition and use of Allah's blessings.

**Sirah / Seerah** السِّيرَة
Biography specifically the biography of the Prophet .

**Sirat** الصِّرَاط
The Bridge over Hellfire which all must cross on the Day of Judgement.

**Subhanallah** سُبْحَانَ اللَّه
"Glory be to Allah." An expression of wonder, gratitude, and recognition of Allah's perfection.

**Sujud** سُجُود
Prostration putting the forehead on the ground in prayer. The closest a servant comes to Allah.

**Sunnah** السُّنَّة
The way of the Prophet his words, actions, and approvals. The second source of Islamic knowledge.

---

## T

**Taharah** طَهَارَة
Ritual purity required for prayer and Quran recitation.

**Tahajjud** تَهَجُّد
The voluntary night prayer prayed after sleeping, before Fajr. Highly recommended.

**Talbiyah** التَّلْبِيَة
The special supplication recited during Ihram for Hajj or Umrah: _"Labbayka Allahumma labbayk..."_

**Taqwa** تَقْوَى
God-consciousness awareness of Allah in every moment. The highest virtue in Islam.

**Tashahhud** التَّشَهُّد
The testimony recited while sitting in prayer.

**Taslim** التَّسْلِيم
The closing salutation of prayer turning right and left saying _"As-salamu alaykum wa rahmatullah."_

**Tawaf** طَوَاف
Circling the Ka'bah seven times counter-clockwise a central ritual of Hajj and Umrah.

**Tawakkul** تَوَكُّل
Complete reliance on Allah after taking all available means.

**Tawbah** تَوْبَة
Repentance returning to Allah after sin, with regret, cessation, and resolve not to return.

**Tawhid** تَوْحِيد
The Oneness of God the most fundamental concept in Islamic theology.

**Tayammum** تَيَمُّم
Dry purification using clean earth a substitute for Wudu or Ghusl when water is unavailable.

**Tazkiyah** تَزْكِيَة
Purification of the soul the spiritual discipline of cleansing the heart from its diseases.

---

## U

**Ulama** عُلَمَاء
Scholars those qualified in Islamic knowledge.

**Ummah** أُمَّة
Community or nation. The global Muslim community is collectively the _Ummah_ of the Prophet Muhammad .

**Umrah** عُمْرَة
The lesser pilgrimage to Makkah can be performed at any time of year.

---

## W

**Wahy** وَحْي
Divine revelation the communication from Allah to the prophets.

**Witr** وِتْر
An odd-numbered voluntary prayer the last prayer of the night. At minimum 1 Rak'ah.

**Wudu** وُضُوء
Minor ritual ablution washing specific body parts before prayer.

---

## Y

**Ya'juj wa Ma'juj** يَأْجُوج وَمَأْجُوج
Gog and Magog two nations mentioned in the Quran and Hadith as major signs of the Last Days.

**Yawm al-Qiyamah** يَوْمُ الْقِيَامَة
The Day of Resurrection the Last Day.

---

## Z

**Zakat** زَكَاة
The obligatory annual charity the third pillar of Islam. 2.5% of zakatable wealth above the Nisab.

**Zina** زِنَا
Fornication or adultery prohibited in Islamic law.

---

## How to Use This Glossary

- Terms are introduced with **Arabic script** in each chapter use this glossary for quick reference.
- When a term appears in the text with an asterisk (\*), you can look it up here.
- Arabic pronunciation follows classical Quranic Arabic not regional dialects.
- For deeper study of any term, see the dedicated chapter where it is treated at length.
