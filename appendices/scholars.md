# Recommended Scholars — Classical and Contemporary

**Part:** Appendices &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Use:** Reference and further study

---

> This appendix lists scholars whose works are recommended for each level of Islamic study. It is organized by era (classical vs. contemporary) and by subject. This is not an exhaustive list — it is a curated starting point for the student of knowledge.

---

## 🏛️ CLASSICAL SCHOLARS

*These scholars laid the foundations of Islamic knowledge. Their works are studied in Islamic universities worldwide to this day.*

---

### Theology & Aqeedah

**Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal** (164–241 AH)
The founding Imam of the Hanbali madhab and a towering Hadith scholar. His steadfastness during the Mihna (Inquisition) made him the symbol of Ahlus Sunnah's commitment to principle.
*Key works:* Musnad Ahmad (27,000+ Hadith), Al-Radd ala al-Zanadiqah.

**Imam al-Ash'ari** (260–324 AH)
Founder of the Ash'ari theological school — the most widely followed school of Sunni theology. A former Mu'tazilite who returned to Ahlus Sunnah.
*Key works:* Al-Ibanah, Maqalat al-Islamiyyin.

**Imam al-Maturidi** (238–333 AH)
Founder of the Maturidi theological school — dominant in the Hanafi tradition. His theology is nearly identical to Ash'ari with minor differences.
*Key works:* Kitab al-Tawhid.

**Ibn Taymiyyah** (661–728 AH)
One of the most influential scholars in Islamic history — known for his encyclopedic knowledge, bold scholarship, and commitment to returning to the texts of Quran and Sunnah. A Hanbali scholar with significant impact on later revivalist movements.
*Key works:* Majmu' al-Fatawa (37 volumes), Al-Aqeedah al-Wasitiyyah, Kitab al-Iman.

**Ibn al-Qayyim al-Jawziyyah** (691–751 AH)
The greatest student of Ibn Taymiyyah. Particularly known for his spiritual writings combining deep Quranic knowledge with practical guidance for the heart.
*Key works:* Madarij al-Salikin, Zad al-Ma'ad, Al-Wabil al-Sayyib, I'lam al-Muwaqqi'in.

---

### Hadith Sciences

**Imam Muhammad ibn Ismail al-Bukhari** (194–256 AH)
Compiler of Sahih al-Bukhari — the most rigorously verified Hadith collection in Islamic history. His standard of verification is the benchmark against which all Hadith scholarship is measured.

**Imam Muslim ibn al-Hajjaj** (206–261 AH)
Compiler of Sahih Muslim — the second most authoritative Hadith collection. Known for his excellent organization and rigorous methodology.

**Imam an-Nawawi** (631–676 AH)
One of the greatest scholars of Hadith and Fiqh. His compilation of 40 Hadith (*Al-Arba'een al-Nawawiyyah*) is the most widely memorized collection of hadith in the world. His commentary on Sahih Muslim remains a primary reference.
*Key works:* Riyadh al-Salihin, Al-Arba'een al-Nawawiyyah, Sharh Sahih Muslim.

---

### Tafsir (Quranic Commentary)

**Imam Ibn Kathir** (701–774 AH)
His Tafsir (*Tafsir Ibn Kathir*) is the most widely used classical Tafsir in the English-speaking world. Clear, evidence-based, and accessible.

**Imam al-Tabari** (224–310 AH)
The earliest and most comprehensive classical Tafsir — *Jami' al-Bayan fi Tafsir al-Quran*. The primary reference for understanding what the Companions and Tabi'un said about Quranic verses.

**Imam al-Qurtubi** (d. 671 AH)
His Tafsir (*Al-Jami' li Ahkam al-Quran*) is particularly strong on the legal rulings derived from each verse.

---

### Islamic Law (Fiqh)

**Imam Abu Hanifah** (80–150 AH) — Founder of the Hanafi madhab

**Imam Malik ibn Anas** (93–179 AH) — Founder of the Maliki madhab; compiler of Al-Muwatta

**Imam al-Shafi'i** (150–204 AH) — Founder of the Shafi'i madhab; father of Usul al-Fiqh

**Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal** (164–241 AH) — Founder of the Hanbali madhab

**Ibn Qudamah al-Maqdisi** (541–620 AH)
The great Hanbali jurist. His *Al-Mughni* is one of the most comprehensive works of comparative Fiqh, covering all four madhabs.

---

### Spirituality & Character

**Imam al-Ghazali** (450–505 AH)
Arguably the most influential Muslim scholar after the Companions. His *Ihya Ulum al-Din* (Revival of the Religious Sciences) is the most comprehensive work on Islamic spirituality ever written — covering theology, worship, ethics, and the diseases of the heart.
*Key works:* Ihya Ulum al-Din, Al-Maqsad al-Asna, Minhaj al-Abidin.

**Ibn Ata'illah al-Iskandari** (d. 709 AH)
Author of the *Hikam* (Book of Wisdom) — 264 aphorisms on the spiritual life that are among the most profound writings in Islamic literature.

**Imam al-Muhasibi** (165–243 AH)
One of the earliest systematic writers on the purification of the soul. His *Al-Ri'ayah* is a masterwork on self-examination and spiritual discipline.

---

### Seerah (Biography of the Prophet ﷺ)

**Ibn Hisham** (d. 218 AH)
His *Sirat Ibn Hisham* — an edited version of Ibn Ishaq's earlier biography — is the foundational classical Seerah text.

**Ibn Kathir**
His *Al-Bidayah wa al-Nihayah* (The Beginning and the End) is a comprehensive Islamic history including an extended treatment of the Seerah.

## 🌍 CONTEMPORARY SCHOLARS

*These are scholars whose work is widely respected in Ahlus Sunnah circles and who have made Islamic knowledge accessible in the modern world.*

---

### English-Language Scholars and Teachers

**Hamza Yusuf (Shaykh)**
Co-founder of Zaytuna College, USA. Widely considered the most influential English-speaking Muslim scholar of his generation. Particularly known for his teaching on classical Islamic texts, spirituality, and the intersection of Islam and Western civilization.
*Accessible through:* Zaytuna College, YouTube, Podcast

**Nouman Ali Khan**
Founder of Bayyinah Institute. Renowned for his Arabic language teaching and Quranic tafsir, making Quranic Arabic accessible to millions through structured courses and reflections.
*Accessible through:* Bayyinah TV, YouTube

**Omar Suleiman**
Founder of Yaqeen Institute. Known for accessible Seerah, Quranic reflections, and Islamic social ethics. Particularly strong on the intersection of Islam and civic life.
*Accessible through:* Yaqeen Institute, YouTube

**Yasir Qadhi**
Dean of Academic Affairs at Al-Maghrib Institute. Encyclopedic knowledge of Aqeedah, Seerah, and contemporary issues. Particularly strong on Seerah — his 100+ episode podcast on the Seerah is a landmark resource.
*Accessible through:* YouTube, Al-Maghrib Institute

**Mufti Menk (Ismail ibn Musa Menk)**
Grand Mufti of Zimbabwe. Known for his warm, practical approach to Islamic living — highly accessible and widely followed globally.
*Accessible through:* YouTube, social media

**Tim Humble (Abu Muawiyah)**
British convert. Known for his clear, evidence-based teaching — particularly on Aqeedah, Hadith, and practical Fiqh. Strong on addressing doubts and misconceptions.
*Accessible through:* YouTube

---

### Arabic-Language Scholars (for advanced students)

**Sheikh Ibn Baz** (1910–1999)
Former Grand Mufti of Saudi Arabia. Known for his vast knowledge of Hadith, Fiqh, and Aqeedah and his accessibility to ordinary Muslims.

**Sheikh Ibn Uthaymeen** (1925–2001)
One of the most prolific scholars of the 20th century. His recorded lessons and fatwas cover virtually every area of Islamic law.

**Sheikh Salman al-Awdah**
Contemporary Saudi scholar with a wide reach across the Arab world.

**Sheikh Abdullah bin Bayyah**
Mauritanian scholar of extraordinary depth in Usul al-Fiqh and Maqasid al-Sharia. Widely respected across the spectrum of Islamic scholarship.
