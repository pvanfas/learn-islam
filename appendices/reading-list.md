# Recommended Reading List From Beginner to Scholar

Part: Appendices

---

## For the Complete Beginner

"Islam: A Short History" Karen Armstrong
A concise, readable introduction from a respected comparative religion scholar. Useful first overview, though readers should supplement with Muslim-authored sources.

"No God but God" Reza Aslan
Accessible and engaging, particularly strong on the life of the Prophet and early Islamic history. Some scholarly reservations about certain interpretations, but valuable as an entry point.

"The Study Quran" Seyyed Hossein Nasr et al.
The most comprehensive single-volume Quran translation and commentary available in English. An essential reference for serious students.

---

## On the Prophet's Life (Seerah)

"The Sealed Nectar" (Al-Raheeq al-Makhtum) Safiur Rahman Mubarakpuri
The most widely read modern Seerah in English. Winner of the World Muslim League Seerah competition. Accessible and comprehensive.

"Muhammad: His Life Based on the Earliest Sources" Martin Lings (Abu Bakr Siraj al-Din)
The most beautifully written English Seerah. Based on Ibn Hisham's classical biography. Essential reading.

"In the Footsteps of the Prophet" Tariq Ramadan
More thematic and reflective than chronological; excellent for extracting contemporary lessons from the Seerah.

---

## On Islamic Theology (Aqeedah)

"The Creed of Imam al-Tahawi" translated by Hamza Yusuf
The foundational Sunni creed with commentary. Essential for understanding orthodox Islamic belief.

"The Principles of Islamic Jurisprudence" Mohammad Hashim Kamali
The accessible academic introduction to how Islamic law works.

---

## On Islamic Spirituality

"Ihya Ulum al-Din" Imam al-Ghazali (Translated by various Islamic Texts Society edition recommended)
The masterwork. Start with Quarter 3 (Destructive Vices) and Quarter 4 (Saving Virtues).

"The Book of Wisdom" (Al-Hikam) Ibn Ata'illah al-Iskandari
264 aphorisms on the spiritual life. One of the most quoted works in Islamic literature.

"Struggling to Surrender" Jeffrey Lang
An American mathematician's intellectual journey to Islam. Extraordinary for showing the Quran through fresh eyes.

---

## On Islamic History and Civilization

"The House of Wisdom" Jonathan Lyons
How Islamic scholarship preserved and advanced human knowledge during Europe's medieval period. Accessible and engaging.

"Lost Islamic History" Firas Alkhateeb
A readable overview of Islamic civilization's major contributions and turning points.

"The Venture of Islam" (3 volumes) Marshall Hodgson
The most comprehensive academic history of Islamic civilization in English. For serious students.

---

## For Arabic Study

"Madinah Arabic" (3 volumes) Dr. V. Abdur Rahim
The most widely used structured Quranic Arabic curriculum. Systematic and thorough.

"Arabic Unlocked" Ustadh Asif Meherali
Accessible and practical for self-study.
