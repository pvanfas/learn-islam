## The Most Important Hadith

The **Hadith of Jibreel** (also known as _Hadith al-Iman_) is arguably the most important Hadith in Islamic literature. It is narrated in Sahih Muslim and contains a complete summary of the entire Islamic religion in a single conversation.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Sahih Muslim Hadith of Jibreel (abridged)**

Umar ibn al-Khattab (RA) narrated: "While we were with the Messenger of Allah one day, a man appeared before us with very white clothes and very black hair. No traces of travel were visible on him, and none of us knew him. He sat down facing the Prophet , resting his knees against his knees and placing his palms on his thighs..."
{% endhint %}

## The Three Questions and Their Answers

### Question 1: "What is Islam?"

**The Prophet's answer:** _"Islam is to testify that there is no god but Allah and that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah; to establish the prayer; to pay Zakat; to fast Ramadan; and to make pilgrimage to the House if you are able."_

→ **The Five Pillars of Islam** the outward practice

### Question 2: "What is Iman (Faith)?"

**The Prophet's answer:** _"It is to believe in Allah, His angels, His books, His messengers, the Last Day, and to believe in divine decree both its good and its evil."_

→ **The Six Pillars of Faith** the inner belief

### Question 3: "What is Ihsan (Excellence)?"

**The Prophet's answer:** _"It is to worship Allah as if you see Him and if you do not see Him, know that He sees you."_

→ **The inner quality of worship** the spiritual dimension

---

## The Revelation

After the man left, the Prophet asked: _"Do you know who that was?"_

The Companions said: _"Allah and His Messenger know best."_

He said: _"That was Jibreel he came to teach you your religion."_

---

## The Three Dimensions of the Religion

This Hadith reveals that the religion of Islam has three inseparable dimensions:

| Dimension | Arabic   | Domain                         |
| --------- | -------- | ------------------------------ |
| **Islam** | إِسْلَام | The body outward practice      |
| **Iman**  | إِيمَان  | The mind inner belief          |
| **Ihsan** | إِحْسَان | The heart spiritual excellence |

All three are required. Islam without Iman is hollow. Iman without Ihsan is incomplete. Ihsan without Islam and Iman has no foundation.

The entire guide you are reading from theology (Aqeedah) to practice (the Five Pillars) to spirituality (Tazkiyah) is an exploration of these three dimensions.
