**Difficulty:** Intermediate → Advanced &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Chapters:** 6 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Estimated reading time:** ~55 min

---

## What You Will Learn in This Part

Hadith science is one of the most sophisticated intellectual achievements in human history. The Prophet Muhammad left behind not only the Quran but a living example his words, actions, and approvals that was preserved through a rigorous system of chain verification unmatched in any other tradition. This part covers what a Hadith is, how thousands of narrations were collected and verified, how to read Hadith literature, and the most important Hadith collections every Muslim should know.
