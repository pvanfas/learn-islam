# Understanding Hadith Grades

**Difficulty:** Intermediate &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Read time:** ~7 min &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Part:** Part 7 Hadith Science

---

## Why Grading Matters

Not all Hadith are equal. A Hadith attributed to the Prophet with an unbroken chain of trustworthy, precise narrators is fundamentally different from one with a broken chain or an unreliable narrator. The grading system tells you how much weight to give a narration.

---

## The Main Grades

### Sahih (صَحِيح) Authentic

**Requirements:**

- Unbroken chain from collector back to the Prophet
- Every narrator is _Adil_ (righteous character) and _Dabit_ (precise memory)
- No hidden defect (_Illah_)
- Not anomalous (_Shadhdhah_)

**Use:** Sahih Hadith can be used as evidence in law, theology, and practice.

### Hasan (حَسَن) Good

**Requirements:** Same as Sahih but one or more narrators have slightly weaker (though still acceptable) precision of memory.

**Use:** Also accepted as evidence slightly lower certainty than Sahih.

### Da'if (ضَعِيف) Weak

**Cause:** A flaw in the chain (missing link, unreliable narrator) or in the text.

**Use:** Cannot be used as primary evidence for legal rulings. Some scholars permit citing weak Hadith for encouraging virtuous acts (_Fada'il al-A'mal_) with strict conditions. Many contemporary scholars are more restrictive.

### Mawdu' (مَوْضُوع) Fabricated

**Definition:** Proven to be invented never said by the Prophet .

**Use:** Absolutely rejected. Repeating a fabricated Hadith as though it is from the Prophet is a grave sin.

---

## Compound Grades

| Grade                | Meaning                                               |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| **Sahih li-Ghayrih** | Becomes Sahih through multiple supporting chains      |
| **Hasan li-Ghayrih** | Becomes Hasan through supporting chains               |
| **Da'if Jiddan**     | Very weak serious flaw                                |
| **Munkar**           | Rejected lone weak narrator contradicts stronger ones |
| **Muttafaqun Alayh** | Agreed upon by both Bukhari and Muslim highest grade  |

---

## How to Know a Hadith's Grade

1. **Check the collection** anything in Sahih al-Bukhari or Sahih Muslim is Sahih by scholarly consensus
2. **Check the grading** major collections like Tirmidhi and Abu Dawud note the grade after the Hadith
3. **Use Sunnah.com** shows collector's grading and often contemporary scholar's assessment
4. **Consult scholars** for important legal or theological questions

---

## Common Fabricated Hadith in Circulation

Be wary of these widely shared but unauthentic narrations:

- _"Seek knowledge even unto China"_ **Da'if or Mawdu'**
- _"The learned are the heirs of the Prophets"_ **Sahih** (this one IS authentic Abu Dawud)
- _"Cleanliness is half of faith"_ **Sahih** (Muslim)
- _"God helps those who help themselves"_ **Not a Hadith** this is a Western proverb

Always verify before sharing any Hadith. The Prophet warned: _"It is sufficient as a lie for a person to repeat everything they hear."_ (Sahih Muslim)
