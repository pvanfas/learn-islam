## How to Read a Hadith

A standard Hadith entry has three components:

**1. The Isnad (chain):** "Reported by X from Y from Z from [Companion] who said..."
**2. The Matn (text):** What the Prophet said or did
**3. The Source and Grade:** Which collection it appears in, and its grade

---

## Example 1 The Foundation Hadith

**Source:** Sahih al-Bukhari 1

> _"Indeed actions are by intentions, and for every person is only what they intended. So whoever migrated for Allah and His Messenger, then his migration was for Allah and His Messenger. And whoever migrated to get something from the world or to marry a woman, then his migration was for what he migrated for."_
> Reported from Umar ibn al-Khattab (RA)

**Reading it:** This is Hadith #1 in Sahih al-Bukhari the opening of the entire collection. Al-Bukhari placed it first deliberately: everything depends on intention. The historical context (the Hijrah) makes the point vivid.

**Application:** Before any act of worship, work, or charity check the intention. The reward is determined by it.

---

## Example 2 A Legal Hadith

**Source:** Sunan Abu Dawud, Hasan

> _"Every intoxicant is Khamr, and every Khamr is forbidden."_
> Reported from Ibn Umar (RA)

**Reading it:** This extends the prohibition of wine (_Khamr_) to all intoxicating substances the underlying principle (_'Illah_) is intoxication, not the specific substance.

**Application:** All drugs, spirits, and intoxicating substances are prohibited not just grape wine.

## Example 3 A Spiritual Hadith

**Source:** Sahih Muslim

> _"Allah the Exalted said: 'I am as my servant thinks of Me, and I am with him when he remembers Me. If he mentions Me within himself, I mention him within Myself. If he mentions Me in an assembly, I mention him in a better assembly. If he draws near to Me a hand's span, I draw near to him an arm's length. If he draws near to Me an arm's length, I draw near to him a fathom. If he comes to Me walking, I come to him running.'"_
> Reported from Abu Hurairah (RA)

**Reading it:** This is a Hadith Qudsi Allah's words transmitted through the Prophet (not Quran). It reveals Allah's extraordinary responsiveness to the servant who turns toward Him. The escalating metaphors show that Allah's response always exceeds the servant's initiative.

## Forty Hadith al-Nawawi The Essential Collection

**Imam al-Nawawi's** (d. 676 AH) collection of 40 (actually 42) Hadith is the most widely memorized Hadith compilation covering the foundations of Islamic belief, worship, and ethics. Every serious student of Islam should memorize all 42 with their meanings.

Available at [Sunnah.com/nawawi](https://sunnah.com/nawawi) with English translation.

**The first five (with Hadith #):**

1. Actions are by intentions (Bukhari/Muslim)
2. Islam, Iman, Ihsan Hadith of Jibreel (Muslim)
3. The pillars of Islam five pillars (Bukhari/Muslim)
4. The creation of the human being destiny (Bukhari/Muslim)
5. Innovations are rejected (Bukhari/Muslim)
