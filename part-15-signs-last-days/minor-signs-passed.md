## Signs That Have Already Occurred

These are signs described by the Prophet that have definitively occurred in Islamic history:

---

### 1 The Death of the Prophet (11 AH / 632 CE)

The Prophet himself identified his own sending as a sign that the Hour was near _"I have been sent and the Hour is like these two"_ gesturing to his index and middle fingers.

His death was the first concrete sign the beginning of the final chapter of human history.

### 2 The Conquest of Jerusalem (17 AH / 638 CE)

The Prophet said: _"The conquest of Jerusalem is a portent of the Hour."_ (Musnad Ahmad) Umar ibn al-Khattab (RA) received the keys of Jerusalem in 638 CE.

### 3 The Great Plague of Amwas (18 AH / 639 CE)

A devastating plague struck the Levant killing approximately 25,000 people including major Companions: Abu Ubayda ibn al-Jarrah (RA), Muadh ibn Jabal (RA), and many others. The Prophet had described a great plague as a sign.

### 4 The Prevalence of Wealth and Expansion

The Prophet said: _"The Hour will not come until wealth is abundant among you, overflowing, until the owner of wealth worries about who will accept his Zakat."_ This occurred during the caliphate of Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz when Zakat collectors reportedly found no one poor enough to accept it.

### 5 The First Civil Wars (_Fitan_)

The Prophet foretold the great trials (_Fitan_) that would afflict the Muslim community the killing of Uthman (RA), the Battle of the Camel, Siffin, and Karbala. These shook the Muslim world and were explicitly described by the Prophet as signs.

### 6 The Construction of Tall Buildings

The Prophet mentioned in the Hadith of Jibreel that the Hour would not come until the barefoot, naked shepherds of Arabia compete in constructing tall buildings. The transformation of the Arabian Peninsula into the location of the world's tallest skyscrapers within one generation fulfills this remarkably.

### 7 Spreading Knowledge and the Writing of Books

The Prophet said knowledge would spread first then be taken away. The explosion of Islamic books and learning in the Golden Age, followed by the Mongol destruction and decline, fits this pattern.

---

## The Significance of Past Signs

The fact that so many signs described by the Prophet 1,400 years ago have come to pass exactly is itself evidence of his prophethood. He described events from the specific (the conquest of Jerusalem) to the general (the abundance of wealth and tall buildings of Arabia) that no person could have predicted through human foresight alone.
