## What Is Islamic Eschatology?

Eschatology is the study of the ultimate fate of humanity and the world the "last things." In Islam, this encompasses:

- **The signs before the Day of Judgement** events and conditions that precede the end
- **The Day of Judgement itself** resurrection, reckoning, the crossing of the Sirat
- **The final destinations** Jannah (Paradise) and Jahannam (Hell)

We covered the Day of Judgement and the final destinations in Part 2. This part focuses specifically on the **signs before the Hour** what the Prophet told us to watch for, understand, and prepare for.

---

## The Primary Source: Hadith

Unlike many areas of Islamic law which are derived from the Quran with Hadith as support, the detailed eschatological information comes primarily from the **Hadith** of the Prophet . The Quran gives broad strokes the Sunnah fills in the detail.

The major collections of eschatological hadith include:

- **Sahih al-Bukhari** and **Sahih Muslim** both contain extensive chapters (_Kitab al-Fitan wa Ashrat as-Sa'ah_) on the tribulations and signs of the Hour
- **Sunan Abu Dawud**, **Jami' at-Tirmidhi**, **Ibn Majah** additional detailed hadith
- **Sahih Ibn Hibban** and **Mustadrak al-Hakim** extended coverage

The scholars of hadith have graded these narrations carefully. Throughout this part, we will distinguish between _Sahih_ (authentic), _Hasan_ (good), and weaker narrations, so you can know what stands on firm ground.

## How to Approach This Subject

Islamic eschatology is one of the most misused areas of Islamic knowledge. People have been predicting the imminent end of the world in every generation for 1,400 years. Some have exploited these narrations to justify extremism, to recruit followers, or to cause panic. The balanced, scholarly approach requires several principles:

### 1 Know What Allah Withheld

The exact time of the Hour is known only to Allah. This is from the Five Things that Allah alone knows (_Al-Mafatih al-Ghayb_):

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Luqman 31:34**

**Translation:** "Indeed, Allah alone has knowledge of the Hour. He sends down the rain and knows what is in the wombs. No soul knows what it will earn tomorrow, and no soul knows in what land it will die. Indeed, Allah is Knowing and Acquainted."
{% endhint %}

No scholar, no imam, no astrologer, and no algorithm can tell you when the Hour will come. Anyone who claims to know is a liar.

### 2 The Signs Are for Awareness, Not Obsession

The Prophet described the signs so that Muslims would:

- **Recognize the times they are living in** and respond appropriately
- **Prepare spiritually** increasing good deeds before the window closes
- **Not be deceived** especially by the Dajjal and other fitnahs
- **Hold fast to their faith** when the world appears to be collapsing around them

They were not described so that Muslims could:

- Spend their lives calculating exact timelines
- Declare that the Hour is "next year" or "in our lifetime"
- Abandon worldly responsibility out of eschatological fatalism
- Use signs as a weapon to generate fear and recruit followers

### 3 Match the Signs to Authentic Text Not Current Events Forced onto Prophecy

Every generation has been tempted to map the prophecies of the end onto the political events of their own era. In the 20th century, some mapped the signs to the World Wars, then the Cold War, then the Gulf War. In the 21st century, to 9/11, ISIS, COVID-19.

This approach is almost always wrong and leads to embarrassment and loss of credibility. The correct approach is to understand what the text actually says and note where observable realities align without forcing connections.

## The Two Categories of Signs

### Minor Signs (_Alamat al-Sughra_)

The minor signs are conditions and events that **precede the major signs** some by many centuries. They are called "minor" not because they are unimportant, but because they are less dramatic than the major signs and occur over a long stretch of time.

Many minor signs have already occurred. Some are ongoing. Others are yet to come.

**Examples of minor signs:**

- The death of the Prophet (already occurred this was the first sign)
- The conquest of Jerusalem (occurred under Umar RA)
- The spread of religious ignorance
- The prevalence of Zina, alcohol, and music
- The building of tall structures by people of the Arabian Peninsula
- The disappearance of trustworthy people
- Time seeming to move faster
- Women outnumbering men

### Major Signs (_Alamat al-Kubra_)

The **Ten Great Signs** are specific, dramatic, unmistakable events that will occur in rapid succession near the very end:

1. The Mahdi
2. The appearance of the Dajjal (the False Messiah)
3. The descent of Isa ibn Maryam (Jesus AS)
4. Ya'juj and Ma'juj (Gog and Magog)
5. The great landslide in the East
6. The great landslide in the West
7. The great landslide in the Arabian Peninsula
8. The rising of the sun from the West
9. The Beast of the Earth (_Dabbat al-Ard_)
10. A fire that drives people to their final gathering place

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih Muslim**

Hudhayfah ibn Usayd (RA) reported that the Prophet said: _"The Hour will not come until you have seen ten signs..."_ and he mentioned all ten.
{% endhint %}

## The Hadith on Time Moving Faster

One of the most widely observable signs today:

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih al-Bukhari**

The Prophet said: _"The Hour will not come until time contracts a year will be like a month, a month will be like a week, a week will be like a day, a day will be like an hour, and an hour will be like the burning of a palm frond."_
{% endhint %}

Almost every generation reports that life seems to move faster than it used to. Whether this is a subjective psychological phenomenon, a consequence of increasing activity and stimulation, or something more literal the hadith remains remarkably relatable across centuries.

## The Wisdom of Knowing the Signs

Ibn Kathir (RA), one of the greatest classical scholars of hadith and tafsir, wrote in his _An-Nihayah fil-Fitan wal-Malahim_ (The End in Tribulations and Battles) one of the most comprehensive classical works on Islamic eschatology that knowing the signs serves one purpose above all:

**To strengthen the believer's resolve and deepen their attachment to Allah when the world around them appears to be falling apart.**

When the Muslim sees tribulations, corruption, and chaos increasing and recognizes these as what the Prophet foretold their faith increases rather than their despair. They say: _"He was right about this. He was right about everything. Hold tight."_

---

## Key Takeaways

- Islamic eschatology covers the signs before the Hour, the Day of Judgement itself, and the final destinations.
- The exact time of the Hour is known only to Allah no human being can know it.
- Signs are divided into minor (ongoing, spread over time) and major (ten dramatic events near the very end).
- The signs were revealed to increase awareness, spiritual preparedness, and faith not to generate panic or recruit followers.
- The correct approach: understand what the texts actually say, match only where the evidence is clear, and avoid forcing current events onto prophecy.
- Knowing the signs is an act of faith it demonstrates trust in the Prophet who described events across centuries with accuracy.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<details>

<summary>Is it true that we are in the end times right now?</summary>

In one sense, yes the Prophet said that his being sent was itself a sign that the Hour was near. He said: _"I have been sent and the Hour is like these two"_ gesturing to his index and middle fingers. Every generation since the Prophet has been in "the end times" in this broad sense. Whether we are near the _major_ signs the Dajjal, the Mahdi, the descent of Isa AS only Allah knows. Many minor signs are clearly observable. But certainty about proximity to the major signs requires caution.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Should I be worried or scared about the end times?</summary>

The believer's response to signs of the Hour is not fear it is urgency. The Prophet said: _"If the Day of Judgement comes upon you while you are planting a tree finish planting the tree."_ Increase your good deeds, maintain your prayers, be kind to your family and community, and leave the timeline to Allah. That is the prophetic response.

</details>
