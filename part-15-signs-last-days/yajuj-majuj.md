# Ya'juj and Ma'juj — Gog and Magog

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Intermediate &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Read time:** ~9 min &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Part:** Part 15 — Signs of the Last Days

---

## 📌 In This Chapter

> Ya'juj and Ma'juj (Gog and Magog) are mentioned in both the Quran and the authentic Sunnah as a nation that will be released near the end of times — causing tremendous destruction before being destroyed by divine intervention. This chapter presents what the Quran and authentic hadith say about them, where they are, and what their release will mean.

## 🧭 Who Are Ya'juj and Ma'juj?

Ya'juj and Ma'juj are **a massive human nation** — descendants of Adam (AS) — who were causing destruction in the ancient world. Dhul-Qarnayn, a righteous powerful king mentioned in the Quran, built an iron and copper barrier to contain them. They have been unable to pass it — but they try to break through every day.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith — Sahih Muslim**

The Prophet ﷺ said: *"Ya'juj and Ma'juj dig every day until they can almost see the light of the sun. Then their leader says: 'Go back — you can dig again tomorrow.' The next day they return and find the barrier as solid as it was — as if it was never dug. Then when their time comes and Allah wishes to release them, they will dig until they almost see the light of the sun. Their leader will say: 'Go back — you can dig again tomorrow, if Allah wills.' The next day they return and find a small hole. They dig through it and are released."*
{% endhint %}

The addition of *"if Allah wills"* (*In sha'Allah*) on the final day — which their leader had never said before — is what causes the barrier to break through. This detail is remarkable: their own leader's use of the Islamic phrase *Inshallah* — perhaps mockingly, perhaps mechanically — becomes the means of their release.

## 🌊 Their Release and Destruction

Their release happens **after the death of the Dajjal** — after Isa (AS) has killed the Dajjal at the Gate of Ludd.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith — Sahih Muslim**

*"While he (Isa AS) is thus, Allah will reveal to him: 'I have brought forth servants of Mine whom no one can defeat in battle. So take My servants to safety on Tur (a mount/sanctuary).' Then Allah will send Ya'juj and Ma'juj — they will rush down from every slope. The vanguard of them will pass by the Sea of Tiberias and drink it dry. Those at the rear will pass and say: 'There was once water here.' They will march on until they reach Mount Khamr (near Jerusalem). They will say: 'We have killed those on earth. Now let us kill those in heaven.' They will shoot their arrows toward the sky — and Allah will return their arrows to them stained with blood. Ya'juj and Ma'juj will rejoice and say: 'We have killed those in heaven!' But Isa (AS) and his companions will be besieged on Tur, until an ox head becomes more valuable to them than a hundred dinars today."*
{% endhint %}

Their arrogance will be total — claiming to have killed those in heaven, not understanding that what they see is Allah returning their own arrows to them.

### The Divine Destruction

Then Isa (AS) and his companions make dua — and Allah sends a plague:

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith — Sahih Muslim**

*"Then Allah will send against them a worm in the nape of their necks, and they will all die overnight — like the death of a single soul. Then Isa (AS) and his companions will descend and find that there is not a handspan of land that is not covered with their rotting corpses. Isa (AS) and his companions will invoke Allah, and He will send birds with necks like the necks of camels to carry them away and cast them wherever Allah wills. Then Allah will send rain that no house of clay or hair will shelter from — and it will wash the earth until it leaves it like a mirror."*
{% endhint %}

The earth will then be filled with blessing — plants growing abundantly, animals multiplying, prosperity spreading — as a period of peace and plenty follows their destruction.

## 📍 Where Is the Barrier of Dhul-Qarnayn?

The location of the iron and copper barrier built by Dhul-Qarnayn is not specified in the Quran or authentic Sunnah — and Islamic scholars have debated it for centuries. Various proposals have been made (the Caucasus region, Central Asia, the steppes of Asia) — but none with definitive evidence.

What is clear from the Quran: the barrier exists, it is real, it will be leveled when Allah decrees, and Ya'juj and Ma'juj will be released. The specific location is a matter of *Ghayb* (unseen knowledge) — we accept the reality of what has been revealed without needing to locate it precisely.

---

## 🤔 Are Ya'juj and Ma'juj Alive Today?

Yes — according to the Islamic tradition. They are confined behind the barrier of Dhul-Qarnayn, trying to break through every day, and will continue until their appointed time.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**🎓 Scholarly caution on modern interpretations:**

Some contemporary Muslims have interpreted Ya'juj and Ma'juj symbolically — as representing certain nations or ideological forces. The mainstream Ahlus Sunnah position is that they are a literal, physical nation as described in the Quran and Sunnah. Their allegorical identification with specific modern nations (Russia, China, Mongol descendants, etc.) is speculative and not supported by clear evidence — and should be approached with extreme caution.
{% endhint %}

## 📊 The Scale of Ya'juj and Ma'juj

The hadith hint at their enormous numbers:

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith — Sahih al-Bukhari and Muslim**

The Prophet ﷺ said: *"Allah will say on the Day of Judgement: 'O Adam!' He will say: 'Here I am, at Your service.' Then a voice will call out: 'Bring forth the people of the Fire.' Adam will say: 'How many are the people of the Fire?' Allah will say: 'From every thousand, nine hundred and ninety-nine are for the Fire and one is for Paradise.' The people said: 'Who will that one be?' He said: 'From Ya'juj and Ma'juj will be a thousand, and from you will be one.'"*
{% endhint %}

This hadith establishes the enormous scale of Ya'juj and Ma'juj — and simultaneously the enormous value of being the one in a thousand who is saved.

## ✅ Key Takeaways

- Ya'juj and Ma'juj are a real human nation contained behind a barrier built by Dhul-Qarnayn, as described in Surah Al-Kahf.
- They try to break through the barrier every day — and will succeed when Allah permits, near the end of times.
- They are released after the Dajjal's death — when Isa (AS) has returned.
- They cause massive destruction — drinking seas dry, shooting arrows toward the sky in arrogance.
- They are destroyed overnight by a divinely sent plague — their corpses covering the earth.
- The earth is then cleansed by rain and enters a period of unprecedented prosperity.
- Their location is unknown — and their modern identification with specific nations is speculative.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

<details>

<summary>Are Ya'juj and Ma'juj the same as the Biblical Gog and Magog?</summary>

The names correspond — both the Quran and the Bible mention Gog and Magog (or Ya'juj and Ma'juj). However, the details differ significantly. The Biblical accounts (Genesis, Ezekiel, Revelation) place them in different contexts and timelines. Muslims accept the Quranic account as the definitive description, acknowledging that the earlier scriptures contain truth that was not perfectly preserved.

</details>

<details>

<summary>If Ya'juj and Ma'juj are behind a barrier, shouldn't we be able to find it?</summary>

Not necessarily. The Quran says it is between two mountain cliffs — but does not specify where. The fact that no explorer has definitively found it could mean many things: it is in an inaccessible location, it is not identifiable to those who encounter it, or it is in a dimension of geography not yet explored. The matter is from the Ghayb — we accept it without being able to locate it physically.

</details>
