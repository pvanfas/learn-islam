## The Hadith

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih Muslim**

Hudhayfah ibn Usayd al-Ghifari (RA) reported: The Prophet came to us while we were discussing. He said: _"What are you discussing?"_ We said: _"We are talking about the Hour."_ He said: _"It will not come until you have seen ten signs before it."_ Then he mentioned:

**The Ten Major Signs:**

1. The Smoke (_Dukhan_)
2. The Dajjal (False Messiah)
3. The Beast of the Earth (_Dabbat al-Ard_)
4. The Rising of the Sun from the West
5. The Descent of Isa ibn Maryam (Jesus AS)
6. Ya'juj and Ma'juj (Gog and Magog)
7. Three landslides one in the East
8. One in the West
9. One in the Arabian Peninsula
10. A fire that will drive people to their place of gathering

## Overview of Each Sign

### 1 The Smoke (_Ad-Dukhan_)

A great smoke will fill the earth, affecting believers (causing something like a cold) and severely afflicting disbelievers. Referenced in Surah Ad-Dukhan 44:10.

### 2 The Dajjal

The False Messiah covered in full detail in [the Dajjal chapter](dajjal.md). The greatest individual trial humanity will face.

### 3 The Beast of the Earth (_Dabbat al-Ard_)

A creature will emerge from the earth and mark people believers and disbelievers distinguishing between them. Referenced in Surah An-Naml 27:82. Its exact nature is from the unseen.

### 4 The Rising of the Sun from the West

The sun will rise from the west and on that day, the door of repentance will close. The Prophet said: _"The Hour will not come until the sun rises from its setting place. When it rises and the people see it, those in the east and west will believe but that will not benefit anyone whose faith had not already been established."_ (Sahih al-Bukhari)

This is perhaps the most catastrophically disruptive of the physical signs reversing the rotation as humanity has known it.

### 5 The Descent of Isa ibn Maryam (AS)

Covered in full in [the Descent of Isa chapter](descent-of-isa.md). He descends, kills the Dajjal, and governs by Islamic law.

### 6 Ya'juj and Ma'juj

Covered in full in [the Ya'juj and Ma'juj chapter](yajuj-majuj.md). Released after the Dajjal's death; destroyed by divine plague.

### 79 Three Great Landslides (_Khasf_)

Three enormous earth-swallowing landslides will occur one in the eastern lands, one in the western lands, and one in the Arabian Peninsula. These will be catastrophic geological events of a scale beyond modern experience.

### 10 The Fire

A fire will emerge from Yemen that will drive all remaining people toward their final gathering place (_Al-Hashr_) the beginning of the process that ends in the resurrection.

---

## The Order of the Major Signs

Scholars generally understand the order as: Mahdi → Dajjal → Isa (AS) descends → Ya'juj and Ma'juj → then the geological signs (landslides, sun rising from west) → then the fire. But the exact sequence of some signs is debated among scholars.
