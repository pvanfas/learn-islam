## What You Will Learn

Islamic eschatology the study of end-times is one of the most detailed subjects in the Sunnah. This part covers the framework, the minor signs (past and present), the ten major signs, the Dajjal, the Mahdi, the descent of Isa (AS), Ya'juj and Ma'juj, and the Day of Judgement step by step.
