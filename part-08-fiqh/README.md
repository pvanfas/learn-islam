## What You Will Learn in This Part

**Fiqh** (فِقْه) Islamic jurisprudence is the detailed derivation of legal rulings from the Quran and Sunnah. While the Quran and Hadith provide the principles and many specific rulings, Fiqh is the human scholarly work of applying those principles to the full range of life situations. This part covers: what Sharia actually is, the four Sunni madhabs and their differences, the key areas of practical Islamic law (purification, halal/haram, family, business, finance), and Islamic medical ethics.
