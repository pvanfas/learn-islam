# Purification (Taharah) Fiqh of Ritual Purity

**Difficulty:** Beginner &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Read time:** ~8 min &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Part:** Part 8 Islamic Law

---

## What Is Taharah?

**Taharah** (طَهَارَة) ritual purity is a prerequisite for prayer, Quran recitation (while holding the Mushaf), and circumambulating the Ka'bah (Tawaf). The Quran commands it:

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Al-Mudaththir 74:4**
**Translation:** "And your clothing purify."
{% endhint %}

Taharah has two types:

- **Purity from Hadath** (state of ritual impurity) achieved through Wudu, Ghusl, or Tayammum
- **Purity from Najasah** (physical impurity) cleaning impure substances from body, clothes, or place of prayer

For the step-by-step practical guides to Wudu, Ghusl, and Tayammum, see [Preparation for Prayer](../part-04-five-pillars/salah/preparation.md).

---

## Types of Impurity (_Najasah_)

### Heavy Impurity (_Najasah Ghalizah_)

- Human urine and feces
- Blood (flowing)
- Pork and products derived from pigs
- Carrion (dead animals not properly slaughtered)
- Alcohol (according to the Hanafi school; others differ on whether alcohol itself is Najasah)

**Removal:** Wash until no trace remains. For urine on fabric: wash three times minimum (Hanafi) or until clean (majority).

### Light Impurity (_Najasah Khafifah_)

- Urine of animals whose meat is permitted (Hanafi specific)

**Removal:** Sprinkling water until it covers the area is sufficient.

---

## Exceptions and Concessions

**What is pardoned (_Ma'fu 'anh_):**

- Small amounts of impurity difficult to avoid (e.g., splashes)
- Blood from wounds that flow involuntarily (in some positions)

**The Hanafi concept of Ma'fu amount:** Less than a dirham's size of solid impurity or less than the center of the palm for liquid impurity is pardoned without needing to clean before prayer.

---

## Special Cases

**Blood:** Flowing blood (more than a minimal amount) is impure. Dried blood on a wound that does not flow is pardoned in most schools.

**Vomit:** Impure if it fills the mouth; less than that is pardoned in the Hanafi school.

**Urine of animals:** Whose meat is permissible pure in Maliki and Shafi'i; impure in Hanafi and Hanbali.

---

## Istinja and Istijmar After Using the Bathroom

After using the toilet, one must clean appropriately:

- **Istinja** using water to clean private parts
- **Istijmar** using dry material (toilet paper, stones) minimum three wipes, odd number

Both together is Sunnah and most thorough.
