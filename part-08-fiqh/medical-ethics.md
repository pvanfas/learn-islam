## The Foundation Preserving Life

The preservation of life (_Hifz al-Nafs_) is one of the five objectives of Sharia. Islamic medical ethics flows from this with the general principle that medical treatment is obligatory when life is at risk.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sunan Abu Dawud (Hasan)**
_"Seek medical treatment for Allah has not created a disease except that He created a cure for it, except for one disease: old age."_
{% endhint %}

## Key Islamic Medical Ethics Positions

### Organ Donation

**Majority contemporary position (permissible):** Many major scholarly bodies including the Fiqh Academies of the OIC and several national bodies permit organ donation, both during life (kidney) and after death, when done voluntarily with no commercial transaction.

**Minority position (not permissible):** Some scholars hold that human body parts cannot be donated as they belong to Allah, not the person.

**Condition:** No financial transaction in organ donation buying and selling organs is prohibited.

### Abortion

| Stage          | Ruling (mainstream Sunni)                                          |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Before 40 days | Permitted for significant cause (rape, severe health risk) debated |
| 40120 days     | Generally prohibited except where mother's life is at risk         |
| After 120 days | Prohibited except direct threat to mother's life                   |

**Note:** The Hanafi school has historically been more permissive in early stages; Maliki and Hanbali are stricter.

### End-of-Life Care

**Active euthanasia:** Prohibited this is the taking of a human life, which belongs to Allah.

**Withdrawing extraordinary life support:** Permitted when there is no reasonable hope of recovery and the treatment is merely prolonging the dying process scholarly consensus.

**Pain management:** Administering pain medication that may as a side effect hasten death (double effect) is generally permitted to preserve dignity.

### Mental Health

Mental illness is a medical condition not a spiritual failing. The Prophet himself suffered from what modern scholars identify as post-traumatic distress after difficult experiences. Seeking mental health treatment is permitted and encouraged.

_"Seek treatment for Allah has not created a disease without creating a cure."_

### Cosmetic Surgery

**For medical need or defect correction:** Generally permitted.

**For pure vanity:** More controversial scholars differ, with many holding it is disliked or prohibited when it involves changing Allah's natural creation without medical necessity.
