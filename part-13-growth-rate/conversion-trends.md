## Who Converts to Islam

Research on Western converts to Islam shows a diverse population:

Converts come from all educational backgrounds, with a significant proportion being university-educated. Women consistently make up approximately 60-65% of Western converts to Islam a fact that surprises many given the assumption that Islam is oppressive to women. Many women cite finding in Islam a framework that dignifies their role and protects them from commodification.

Former Christians make up the largest group of converts globally, followed by those from secular backgrounds, then former Hindus and Buddhists.

---

## What Draws People to Islam

Research and personal testimonies consistently identify these factors:

The clarity and simplicity of Islamic monotheism a direct relationship with God without intermediaries, saints, or complex theological constructs.

The comprehensive, integrated worldview Islam provides answers about purpose, morality, death, and justice that secular frameworks leave unaddressed.

The community and belonging that Muslim communities provide, particularly in individualistic Western societies.

The Quran's literary and spiritual power many converts report encountering the Quran and being struck by its difference from any other text.

The character and example of the Prophet Muhammad particularly after reading the Seerah.
