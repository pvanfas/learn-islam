# Why Islam Is the Fastest-Growing Religion

Difficulty: Beginner | Part: Part 13 Islam's Growth Rate

---

## The Data

The Pew Research Center's 2015 report "The Future of World Religions" projected that Islam will surpass Christianity as the world's largest religion by approximately 2070. By 2050, the Muslim population is projected to reach approximately 2.76 billion nearly 30% of the world's population.

---

## The Primary Driver Demographics

The single largest factor in Islam's growth is not conversion but birth rates. Muslim-majority populations tend to be younger and have higher fertility rates than populations in other religious groups globally.

The median age of Muslims globally is approximately 24 years the youngest of any major religious group. This means a large proportion of the Muslim population is in or approaching prime reproductive years.

---

## Geographic Concentration

The majority of global Muslim population growth is occurring in Sub-Saharan Africa and South Asia regions with both high Muslim populations and high birth rates. Nigeria alone is projected to have one of the world's largest Muslim populations by 2050.

---

## Conversion

Conversion to Islam is a secondary factor significant, but smaller than demographic growth. Estimates suggest approximately 20,000-25,000 people convert to Islam annually in the United States alone. Globally, the numbers are much larger.

Notably, in Western countries, the rate of people leaving Islam (apostasy) is lower than the rate for most other major religions contributing to net growth even in societies where conversion both ways is common.
