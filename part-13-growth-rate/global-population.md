## Why Islam Is Growing Fastest

### Primary Driver: Demographics

The Pew Research Center's landmark 2015 report _"The Future of World Religions"_ identified three main reasons Islam is projected to grow faster than any other religion through 2050:

**1. High fertility rates in Muslim-majority regions:**
Muslim women have an average of 2.9 children, compared to the global average of 2.2 for all religious groups. In sub-Saharan Africa and parts of South Asia, Muslim fertility rates are even higher.

**2. Young age profile:**
The global Muslim population is younger than other religious groups. The median age of Muslims worldwide is approximately 24 years compared to 30 for Christians, 34 for Hindus, and 36 for Buddhists. A younger population means more people entering their reproductive years accelerating growth.

**3. Geographic concentration in high-growth regions:**
The regions with the highest overall population growth rates sub-Saharan Africa, South Asia, and parts of the Middle East happen to have large Muslim majorities.

---

### Secondary Driver: Conversions

Conversion adds to Islam's growth, particularly in the West. While conversion statistics are difficult to measure precisely:

- Approximately **20,00040,000 people** convert to Islam annually in the United States
- Several hundred thousand conversions per year are estimated globally
- Net conversion (accounting for those who leave Islam) is positive more people enter than leave
- **Women:** convert to Islam at higher rates than men in Western countries the opposite of most religious conversion patterns

## Conversion Trends by Region

### North America

The United States has approximately **3.45 million Muslims** (Pew 2017) growing steadily. The African American Muslim community (established through the legacy of the Nation of Islam's shift to Orthodox Islam under Malcolm X and Imam W. D. Mohammed) remains a significant demographic.

Converts come predominantly from Christian backgrounds (Protestant, Catholic), with growing numbers from atheist/agnostic backgrounds.

### Europe

Islam is the second-largest religion in most Western European countries. Germany (~5 million), France (~5.7 million), and the UK (~3.9 million) have the largest Muslim populations.

European Islam is growing through both immigration and conversion with France consistently reporting high rates of conversion, particularly among women.

### Sub-Saharan Africa

The fastest-growing Muslim region. Nigeria, Ethiopia, Tanzania, Kenya, Senegal, and Mali are all experiencing rapid Muslim population growth.

Nigeria split approximately 50-50 between Christians and Muslims will likely have more Muslims than any Arab country within decades.

### Latin America

The smallest but fastest-growing Muslim region proportionally. Small but growing Muslim communities exist in Argentina, Brazil, and across the Caribbean. Hispanic conversion to Islam is a notable trend in the United States.

## A Balanced View

This counterintuitive pattern is documented in multiple studies. The stereotype that Islam oppresses women is apparently not the experience of many Western women who engage with the religion directly.
