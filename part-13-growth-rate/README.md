# Part 13 — Islam's Growth Rate Today

Difficulty: Beginner | Chapters: 4 | Estimated reading time: 30 min

---

## Overview

Islam is the world's fastest-growing major religion. The Pew Research Center projects that by 2050 Muslims will represent approximately 30% of the global population, and that Islam will surpass Christianity as the world's largest religion by approximately 2070. This part examines the demographic data behind these projections, the reasons for Islam's growth, current conversion trends, and what the long-term numbers suggest.

## A Note on Perspective

Muslims should approach demographic data with appropriate detachment. The Prophet said: "Islam began as something strange, and it will return to being something strange — so glad tidings to the strangers." (Sahih Muslim) Large numbers are not the goal. A billion sincere Muslims would be worth more, in the scale of Allah, than two billion nominal ones. Demographic growth is a fact of our era — the obligation is to make sure what is growing has depth, character, and genuine connection to the Quran and Sunnah.
