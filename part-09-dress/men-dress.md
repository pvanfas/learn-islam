# Islamic Dress for Men — Modesty, the Beard, and Appearance

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Beginner &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Read time:** ~9 min &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Part:** Part 9 — Islamic Dress & Appearance

---

## 📌 In This Chapter

> Islamic modesty is a two-way obligation — commanded for both men and women. This chapter covers the Islamic requirements for men's dress, the ruling on the beard, prohibitions on certain clothing and adornment, and the broader concept of *Haya* (modesty) as it applies to Muslim men.

---

## 📖 Quranic Foundation

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah An-Nur 24:30**

**Translation:** "Tell the believing men to lower their gaze and guard their private parts. That is purer for them. Indeed, Allah is Acquainted with what they do."

*The Quran addresses men's modesty FIRST — before the women's verse in 24:31. This is deliberate: the responsibility for modesty is shared, and men are addressed first.*
{% endhint %}

---

## 📏 The Male Awrah — What Must Be Covered

The **Awrah** (area that must be covered) for men is from the **navel to the knee** — in the presence of other men, non-Mahram women, and in prayer.

| Situation | Awrah |
|-----------|-------|
| In prayer | Navel to knee (minimum) |
| In front of other men | Navel to knee |
| In front of non-Mahram women | Body — the full body is recommended to cover beyond the minimum |
| In front of wife | No Awrah restrictions |

**In prayer:** The majority of scholars hold that while the navel-to-knee is the minimum Awrah, prayer should be performed in decent clothing covering the shoulders as well — praying shirtless, while technically covering the Awrah, is disliked by most scholars.

## 🧔 The Beard (*Al-Lihyah*)

The beard is one of the most discussed aspects of men's Islamic appearance — and one of the most consistent Sunnah practices across all four madhabs.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith — Sahih al-Bukhari and Muslim**

The Prophet ﷺ said: *"Differ from the polytheists — let the beard grow and trim the moustache."*

And: *"Trim the moustache closely and leave the beard."*
{% endhint %}

**The scholarly position:**

| Madhab | Position on the Beard |
|--------|-----------------------|
| **Hanafi** | Obligatory (*Wajib*) to keep; trimming below a fistful is prohibited |
| **Maliki** | Obligatory to keep; trimming is permitted |
| **Shafi'i** | Sunnah (confirmed); shaving is forbidden or strongly disliked |
| **Hanbali** | Obligatory; shaving is prohibited |

The **majority position** is that shaving the beard is either prohibited (*Haram*) or at minimum strongly disliked (*Makruh Tahriman*) — based on the explicit commands in multiple authentic hadith. The minority position considers it a confirmed Sunnah whose omission is sinful but not categorically haram.

**The minimum length:** The Hanafi school holds that trimming the beard to less than a fistful (approximately 3–4cm) is prohibited. Other schools are less specific about minimum length.

## 🪙 Prohibition of Gold and Silk for Men

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith — Sahih al-Bukhari and Muslim**

*"Gold and silk have been made permitted for the women of my nation and prohibited for its men."*
{% endhint %}

**Gold:** Wearing gold — rings, chains, watches with gold cases — is prohibited for Muslim men. This includes gold-plated items according to the majority. Silver rings are permitted and were worn by the Prophet ﷺ.

**Silk:** Wearing garments of pure silk is prohibited for men. Mixed fabrics with less than 50% silk content are permitted according to some scholars. The Prophet ﷺ permitted silk up to four fingers' width for men with a skin condition — indicating the prohibition admits exceptions for medical necessity.

## 👖 Isbal — Wearing Clothing Below the Ankle

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith — Sahih al-Bukhari**

*"Whatever of the lower garment hangs below the ankles is in the Fire."*

And: *"Allah will not look at the one who drags his garment along the ground out of pride."*
{% endhint %}

**The Isbal ruling:** Wearing clothing (trousers, thobes, robes) that extends below the ankle out of arrogance is prohibited by consensus. The key question is whether the prohibition applies:

- **Only when worn out of pride (*Kibr*):** Some scholars hold this — if a man wears long trousers without pride, it is disliked but not prohibited
- **Absolutely — regardless of intention:** Other scholars (Hanbali position) hold that the prohibition applies categorically — the garment must be above the ankle

**Practical guidance:** The safe and Sunnah approach is to wear garments above the ankle — this is unambiguous and free from the debate.

---

## 💍 Rings and Jewelry for Men

**Silver rings** are permitted and Sunnah — the Prophet ﷺ wore a silver ring. He wore it on his right hand and sometimes the left.

**Gold rings** are prohibited for men — see above.

**Chains, bracelets, and other jewelry:** Subject to the gold/silver distinction. Silver is permitted; gold is prohibited. Non-precious metal jewelry is debated — avoiding it is the cautious approach.

## ✂️ Hair — Permitted and Prohibited Styles

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith — Sahih al-Bukhari**

*"The Prophet ﷺ forbade the Qaza' (haircut where some patches are shaved and some left — an uneven patchy style)."*
{% endhint %}

**Prohibited:**
- The *Qaza'* — shaving parts of the head and leaving others (unless for medical reasons)
- Imitating the styles of disbelievers in ways that are distinctive to them as religious markers
- The *Qarn* — a style where hair is gathered into a single horn-like projection

**Permitted:**
- Shaving the head completely (*Halq*)
- Cutting hair short uniformly
- Longer hair — the Prophet ﷺ had hair to his ear lobes and sometimes to his shoulders
- Braiding or tying back longer hair

**Dyeing hair:** The Prophet ﷺ encouraged dyeing grey hair and forbade leaving it completely white without any treatment (as a sign of neglect), while forbidding pure black dye (*Al-Aswad*) without any natural color. Henna-based and non-black dyes are widely permitted.

---

## 🌸 Perfume for Men

**Perfume is Sunnah for men** — the Prophet ﷺ loved beautiful fragrances and rarely went without them. He said: *"From this world, women and perfume have been made beloved to me."*

The principle: men's perfume should have a noticeable scent when applied, while women's perfume (in public) should be subtle. The Prophet ﷺ said a woman who passes men wearing strong perfume is like committing Zina — this is about deliberate attraction, not the existence of scent.

---

## ✅ Key Takeaways

- Men's modesty in Islam is real and commanded — the Quran addresses men's lowering of the gaze before women's dress.
- The male Awrah (navel to knee) must be covered in prayer and in front of non-Mahram women.
- The beard is a confirmed Sunnah commanded by explicit hadith — the majority of scholars consider shaving it prohibited or strongly disliked.
- Gold and silk are prohibited for Muslim men; silver rings and natural fabric are Sunnah.
- Isbal (wearing clothing below the ankle) is prohibited when done out of pride; the cautious position is to keep clothing above the ankle regardless.
- Perfume is a Sunnah for men — the Prophet ﷺ loved fragrance and encouraged its use.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

<details>

<summary>Is it sinful to shave the beard for a job or out of family pressure?</summary>

According to the majority of classical scholars — yes, shaving the beard is either prohibited or strongly disliked, regardless of the social pressure behind it. However, scholars also recognize that sincerity, immediate Tawbah (repentance), and the intention to grow it back matter. A Muslim who shaves his beard for his family's pressure while intending to return to the Sunnah is in a better state than one who shaves out of dismissal of the Sunnah entirely. Consult a qualified scholar about your specific situation.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Are Muslim men allowed to wear shorts in public?</summary>

If the shorts cover the knees, they cover the minimum Awrah. If they expose above the knee, they do not cover the Awrah and cannot be worn in prayer or in front of non-Mahram women. Most scholars recommend that Muslim men dress modestly beyond the minimum — full-length trousers are more in keeping with the Sunnah of dignity and Haya.

</details>
