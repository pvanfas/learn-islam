# The Beard Sunnah Evidence and Rulings

**Difficulty:** Beginner &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Read time:** ~5 min &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Part:** Part 9: Islamic Dress

---

## The Hadith Evidence

The beard is commanded by explicit, authentic Hadith:

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih al-Bukhari and Muslim**
_"Differ from the polytheists let the beard grow and trim the moustache."_

_"Trim the moustache closely and leave the beard."_
{% endhint %}

These are direct commands not suggestions. The majority of classical scholars derived an obligation from them.

---

## The Four Madhab Positions

| Madhab      | Position                                                         |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Hanafi**  | Obligatory to keep; trimming below one fistful is prohibited     |
| **Maliki**  | Obligatory to keep; some trimming permitted                      |
| **Shafi'i** | Confirmed Sunnah; shaving is forbidden or very strongly disliked |
| **Hanbali** | Obligatory; shaving is prohibited                                |

**The mainstream Ahlus Sunnah position:** Shaving the beard is either _Haram_ (prohibited) or _Makruh Tahriman_ (strongly disliked to the point of sinfulness). This is the position of the vast majority of classical scholars.

---

## Length

**The Hanafi ruling:** Trimming below a fistful is prohibited. Many scholars from other madhabs do not specify a minimum but leaving it to a visible, full beard is the Sunnah ideal.

---

## Social Pressure

Shaving for job interviews, family pressure, or social acceptance is a real challenge for many Muslim men. The Sunnah answer: the beard is not negotiable as a religious obligation. But the response to one who shaves should be wisdom and gentleness not condemnation.
