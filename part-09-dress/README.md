# Part 9 Islamic Dress & Appearance

**Difficulty:** Beginner &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Chapters:** 6 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Estimated reading time:** ~50 min

---

## What You Will Learn in This Part

Islam treats the body and its presentation as a form of worship modesty (_Haya_) is a branch of faith. This part covers the Islamic principles of dress for both men and women, the scholarly debates around the Hijab and Niqab, the rulings on the beard, and general appearance guidelines from the Sunnah.
