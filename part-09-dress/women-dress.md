## The Quranic Command

The Islamic dress requirements for women are derived from two primary Quranic verses:

**Surah An-Nur 24:31** Commands covering the _Zeenah_ (adornment) and drawing the Khimar (head covering) over the chest.

**Surah Al-Ahzab 33:59** Commands the outer garment (_Jilbab_) to be drawn over the body.

For the complete treatment of the Hijab, its Quranic basis, conditions, the Niqab debate, and responses to common criticisms, see the dedicated [Hijab chapter](hijab.md).

## The Conditions of Islamic Dress

The six conditions that scholars derive from the texts:

| Condition             | Meaning                                                |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Coverage**          | Body covered except face and hands (majority position) |
| **Loose**             | Not tight or body-revealing                            |
| **Opaque**            | Not see-through                                        |
| **Not attractive**    | Not designed to draw attention                         |
| **Not imitating men** | Feminine in character                                  |
| **Not prideful**      | Not worn for arrogance or showing off                  |

## Dress in Different Contexts

| Context                                               | Awrah requirement                               |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| **In front of Mahram men** (father, brother, husband) | Body from navel to knee (minimum)               |
| **In front of Muslim women**                          | Navel to knee (minimum)                         |
| **In front of non-Mahram men**                        | Full body covering per the conditions above     |
| **In prayer**                                         | Full covering except face and hands             |
| **In Ihram for Hajj/Umrah**                           | Face must remain uncovered; normal modest dress |

## Culture vs. Religion

Islamic dress requirements are universal they apply to Muslim women of every ethnicity and culture. However, _how_ those requirements are expressed varies enormously:

- A South Asian woman may wear a salwar kameez with dupatta
- A West African woman may wear a boubou
- An Arab woman may wear an abaya
- A Western Muslim woman may wear modest Western clothing with a headscarf

All of these can fulfill the Islamic conditions the religion does not mandate a specific cultural style.
