# The Niqab Scholarly Positions

**Difficulty:** Intermediate &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Read time:** ~6 min &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Part:** Part 9 Islamic Dress

---

## What Is the Niqab?

The **Niqab** (نِقَاب) is the face veil worn by some Muslim women covering the face while leaving the eyes visible (or sometimes covering the eyes as well with a separate piece).

---

## The Four Scholarly Positions

See the [Hijab chapter](hijab.md) for the full treatment of this topic, including the evidence for each position.

**Brief summary:**

| Position                                | Ruling                                    | Evidence                                                                        |
| --------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Hanbali / some Shafi'i**              | Obligatory (_Wajib_)                      | Face is part of Awrah; Companion women covered their faces                      |
| **Many scholars**                       | Highly recommended (_Sunnah Mu'akkadah_)  | Strong evidence from practice; not quite at obligation level                    |
| **Some scholars**                       | Recommended (_Mustahabb_)                 | Good; praiseworthy; not required                                                |
| **Hanafi, Maliki, one Shafi'i opinion** | Face is not Awrah covering it is optional | _La ilaha illallah_ argument; verse specifies covering adornment, face excluded |

---

## The Consensus Point

**What scholars unanimously agree on:** A woman who wears the Niqab is doing something praiseworthy and within the legitimate practice of Islamic scholarship. A woman who wears only the Hijab (headscarf) without Niqab is also within legitimate scholarship. Neither should condemn the other.

---

## Niqab and Ihram

During Hajj and Umrah Ihram, a woman **may not wear the Niqab** the face must remain uncovered. If a man approaches, she may temporarily cover her face, then uncover again.
