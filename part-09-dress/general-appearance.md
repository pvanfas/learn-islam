# General Appearance Rulings

**Difficulty:** Beginner &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Read time:** ~7 min &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Part:** Part 9: Islamic Dress

---

## The Prophetic Standard

The Prophet was beautifully groomed he loved cleanliness, pleasant appearance, and beautiful fragrance. Islamic appearance rulings are not about austerity they are about dignity, modesty, and the Sunnah.

---

## The Five Acts of Fitrah (Natural Disposition)

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih al-Bukhari and Muslim**
_"Five things are from the Fitrah: circumcision, removing pubic hair, plucking armpit hair, cutting fingernails, and trimming the moustache."_
{% endhint %}

These acts of personal hygiene are confirmed Sunnah scholars recommend not leaving them beyond 40 days.

---

## Prohibited Hairstyles

| Hairstyle                                        | Ruling                                                        | Evidence                                |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| **Qaza'** (shaving parts, leaving others patchy) | Prohibited                                                    | Sahih al-Bukhari                        |
| **Pure black hair dye** for grey                 | Prohibited (Hanbali, some Shafi'i); disliked (Hanafi, Maliki) | Multiple Hadith                         |
| **Imitating non-Muslim religious identity**      | Prohibited                                                    | Hadith on not imitating other religions |

**Permitted:** Shaving the head entirely, cutting short, growing long, natural colors, henna-based dyes.

---

## Tattoos

Permanently tattooing the skin is prohibited based on the hadith that the Prophet cursed the one who tattoos and the one who is tattooed. This applies to permanent tattoos.

Temporary henna or non-permanent body art: generally permitted for women; disliked for men (as it imitates women's adornment).

---

## Use of Perfume

**Men:** Perfume is Sunnah the Prophet loved it and rarely went without it.

**Women:** Perfume in public is restricted women should not wear strong perfume that attracts attention from non-Mahram men. The Prophet said a woman who passes men with strong fragrance is "like one committing Zina." For women at home with their husband and family perfume is encouraged.

---

## Rings

- **Silver rings:** Permitted and Sunnah for men the Prophet wore one
- **Gold rings:** Prohibited for men; permitted for women
- **Iron rings:** Disliked

---

## Looking in the Mirror

Looking in the mirror while saying:

_"Allahumma anta hassanta khalqi fa-hassin khuluqi"_
"O Allah, You have made my creation good, so make my character good."

(Musnad Ahmad Hasan)
