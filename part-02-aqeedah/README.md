## What You Will Learn in This Part

_Aqeedah_ (عَقِيدَة) from the Arabic root meaning "to tie" or "to fasten" is the set of beliefs that a Muslim's heart is anchored to. These are not vague spiritual feelings. They are specific, defined articles of faith with clear evidential grounding in the Quran and Sunnah.

Islamic theology identifies **six pillars of faith** (_Arkan al-Iman_), famously summarized in the Hadith of Jibreel: belief in Allah, in the Angels, in the Revealed Books, in the Prophets, in the Day of Judgement, and in Divine Decree. Each is explored in its own chapter here from its basic meaning to its deeper implications.

Understanding Aqeedah properly is foundational. Without it, worship becomes hollow routine and religious practice loses its meaning. With it, every prayer, every fast, every act of charity becomes a conscious act of love and submission to the One who created you.

## The Six Pillars of Faith at a Glance

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith of Jibreel Sahih Muslim**

When the Angel Jibreel (AS) appeared to the Prophet in human form and asked: _"What is faith (Iman)?"_ the Prophet replied:

_"It is to believe in Allah, His angels, His books, His messengers, the Last Day, and to believe in divine decree both its good and its evil."_
{% endhint %}

| Pillar | Arabic                        | What it means                                                       |
| ------ | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1      | الإِيمَان بِاللّٰه            | Belief in Allah His existence, oneness, names, and attributes       |
| 2      | الإِيمَان بِالْمَلَائِكَة     | Belief in the Angels created from light, assigned specific roles    |
| 3      | الإِيمَان بِالْكُتُب          | Belief in the Revealed Books Torah, Psalms, Gospel, Quran           |
| 4      | الإِيمَان بِالرُّسُل          | Belief in the Prophets and Messengers from Adam to Muhammad         |
| 5      | الإِيمَان بِالْيَوْمِ الْآخِر | Belief in the Last Day resurrection, judgement, heaven, hell        |
| 6      | الإِيمَان بِالْقَدَر          | Belief in Divine Decree everything is in Allah's knowledge and will |
