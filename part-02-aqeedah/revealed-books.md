# Belief in the Revealed Books

**Difficulty:** Beginner &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Read time:** ~8 min &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Part:** Part 2 Aqeedah

---

## In This Chapter

> The third pillar of Islamic faith is belief in all the divine scriptures the books Allah revealed to the prophets throughout human history. This chapter covers which books Muslims believe in, how Islam views the Torah, Psalms, and Gospel, why Muslims believe earlier scriptures were altered, and how the Quran differs in its preservation.

## The Books Muslims Believe In

### The Four Named Books

The Quran names four revealed scriptures:

| Book                     | Arabic      | Revealed to      | People          |
| ------------------------ | ----------- | ---------------- | --------------- |
| **The Torah** (_Tawrah_) | التَّوْرَاة | Musa (Moses AS)  | Banu Israel     |
| **The Psalms** (_Zabur_) | الزَّبُور   | Dawud (David AS) | Banu Israel     |
| **The Gospel** (_Injil_) | الإِنْجِيل  | Isa (Jesus AS)   | Banu Israel     |
| **The Quran**            | الْقُرْآن   | Muhammad         | All of humanity |

Beyond these four, the Quran indicates that Ibrahim (AS) received revealed pages (_Suhuf Ibrahim_) and that every prophet received some form of divine guidance making the total number of revealed scriptures far larger than what is named.

## What Muslims Believe About These Books

### 1 They Were Originally from Allah

Muslims believe that the Torah given to Musa (AS), the Psalms given to Dawud (AS), and the Gospel given to Isa (AS) were genuine divine revelations the word of Allah in their original form. They contained guidance, law, and truth.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Al-Ma'idah 5:44**

**Translation:** "Indeed, We sent down the Torah, in which was guidance and light. The prophets who submitted to Allah judged by it for the Jews..."
{% endhint %}

### 2 They Were Altered (_Tahrif_)

Muslims also believe the earlier scriptures did not survive in their original form. Over centuries of transmission through scribes, translations, editorial processes, and political pressures the original text was subject to _Tahrif_ (alteration): additions, deletions, mistranslations, and interpolations.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Al-Baqarah 2:79**

**Translation:** "So woe to those who write the scripture with their own hands and then say: 'This is from Allah' in order to exchange it for a small price. Woe to them for what their hands have written, and woe to them for what they earn."
{% endhint %}

This is not a claim that everything in the Bible is false but that it is a mixture of original divine truth and human addition, distortion, and mistranslation. Scholars have found this position increasingly supported by textual criticism of the Bible itself, which identifies multiple authors, editorial hands, and later insertions in both the Old and New Testaments.

### 3 The Quran Confirms and Corrects

The Quran came as a **guardian over** (_Muhaymin_) the earlier scriptures:

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Al-Ma'idah 5:48**

**Translation:** "And We have sent down to you the Book in truth, confirming that which came before of the Scripture and as a criterion over it..."
{% endhint %}

Where the Quran confirms something from the Bible it is correct. Where it differs the Quran's version is the preserved truth. The Quran explicitly confirms the virgin birth of Jesus, the miracles of Musa, and the prophethood of Ibrahim while correcting theological errors that entered the earlier traditions.

## Why Muslims Don't Follow the Torah or Gospel Today

A sincere question: if Muslims believe the Torah and Gospel were once divine why don't they follow them?

**Three reasons:**

**1. They are no longer in their original form.** Following an altered scripture risks following human additions mistaken for divine command.

**2. The Quran is the final, preserved scripture.** Allah preserved the Quran as its Guardian (Surah Al-Hijr 15:9) while earlier scriptures were left in human custody and underwent the alterations that human custody inevitably produces.

**3. Every scripture was for a specific time and people.** The Torah was given to Banu Israel. The Gospel to the same people through Isa (AS). The Quran was revealed for all of humanity until the Last Day it is the universal, final guidance.

---

## The Quran's Unique Preservation

Unlike any other ancient religious text:

- The Quran exists in its **original language** (Arabic) unchanged
- Over **10 million people** have memorized it completely an unbroken oral chain
- The Uthmanic Mushaf compiled within 20 years of the Prophet's death is **textually identical** to every Quran in the world today
- Not one word has been documented to have changed in 1,400 years

This is precisely what Allah promised:

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Al-Hijr 15:9**

**Translation:** "Indeed, it is We who sent down the reminder, and indeed, We will be its guardian."
{% endhint %}

The earlier scriptures were entrusted to their communities to preserve and the communities' preservation was imperfect. The Quran was preserved directly by Allah and this is why it stands as the criterion (_Al-Furqan_) over all other texts.

---

## Respecting People of the Book

Belief in the altered nature of earlier scriptures does not translate into disrespect for Jews and Christians as people. The Quran calls them _Ahl al-Kitab_ People of the Book recognizing that they trace their tradition to genuine divine revelation.

Muslims are commanded to engage with them in the best way:

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Al-Ankabut 29:46**

**Translation:** "And do not argue with the People of the Scripture except in the best manner except those who have done wrong among them. And say: 'We believe in what was revealed to us and what was revealed to you, and our God and your God is one, and we are to Him submitting.'"
{% endhint %}

## Key Takeaways

- Belief in all divine scriptures is the third pillar of Islamic faith.
- The four named books: Torah (to Musa AS), Psalms (to Dawud AS), Gospel (to Isa AS), and the Quran (to Muhammad ).
- Muslims believe these scriptures were originally from Allah but the earlier ones were subject to human alteration (_Tahrif_) over time.
- The Quran confirms and corrects the earlier scriptures it is the preserved final word of Allah.
- The earlier scriptures were for their specific peoples and times. The Quran is universal and final.
- Belief in earlier scriptures does not mean following them the Quran supersedes them with the preserved, complete, universal guidance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<details>

<summary>Do Muslims believe the Gospel of Jesus is the same as the four Gospels in the Bible?</summary>

No. The _Injil_ (Gospel) referred to in the Quran is the original revelation given to Isa (AS) a single, direct revelation from Allah. The four Gospels (Matthew, Mark, Luke, John) are accounts written by followers of Jesus decades after his life not direct revelation. They contain truth mixed with human narration and were themselves selected from many competing Gospel accounts by the Council of Nicaea (325 CE). The original _Injil_ as revealed to Isa (AS) is not preserved in its original form.

</details>

<details>

<summary>If the earlier scriptures were altered, does that mean the God of the Bible is different from Allah?</summary>

No. The God described in the earlier scriptures even in their current form is recognizably the same God: the Creator of the heavens and the earth, the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, the God who spoke to Moses. The theological differences the Trinity, the divinity of Jesus, the sonship of God are, in the Islamic understanding, later human additions to the revelation, not original teachings of the earlier prophets.

</details>
