## What You Will Learn in This Part

_Ahlus Sunnah Wal Jama'ah_ the People of the Sunnah and the Community is the name given to the mainstream tradition of Islamic belief and practice. It is not a sect or a movement. It is the broad, central tradition of Islam as transmitted by the Companions of the Prophet , preserved by generations of scholars, and followed by the overwhelming majority of Muslims throughout history.

Understanding what Ahlus Sunnah means its theology, its methodology, and its boundaries gives the student of Islam a stable compass with which to evaluate any claim, group, or interpretation they encounter. Without this understanding, navigating the diversity of Islamic opinion and the noise of sectarian claims becomes very difficult.
