# Part 11 Criticism and Arguments Against Islam With Responses

**Difficulty:** Intermediate &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Chapters:** 6 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Estimated reading time:** ~65 min

---

## What You Will Learn in This Part

Islam has been subjected to a wide range of criticisms historical, theological, social, scientific, and academic. This part engages with those criticisms directly, honestly, and with scholarly depth. The goal is not to "win arguments" but to provide Muslims with clear, evidence-based responses to questions they will inevitably encounter, and to provide honest seekers with the most intellectually rigorous engagement with these questions available.

---

## Our Approach

**Three commitments guide this part:**

1. **State the criticism fairly** as a serious critic would make it, not as a strawman
2. **Acknowledge genuine complexity** where a criticism has a valid point, say so
3. **Present the Islamic response with evidence** Quran, Sunnah, history, reason
