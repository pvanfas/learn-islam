## Beyond Basic Commentary

Advanced Tafsir study moves beyond simply reading what scholars said about a verse toward understanding why the methodological principles, the scholarly debates, and the application of linguistic analysis.

## The Major Methods of Tafsir

**Tafsir bil-Ma'thur (Transmitted Commentary):** Drawing exclusively on the Quran itself, the Sunnah, and the statements of the Companions and Tabi'un. The most reliable method it prioritizes what those closest to the revelation understood it to mean. Al-Tabari's Jami' al-Bayan is the master work of this approach.

**Tafsir bil-Ra'y (Commentary by Opinion):** Using reason, linguistic analysis, and scholarly deduction to explain the Quran, going beyond transmitted material. This is permitted when it does not contradict transmitted material and is performed by qualified scholars. Ibn Kathir, al-Zamakhshari, and al-Razi all employ rational elements alongside transmitted material.

**Tafsir al-Ishari (Allusive Commentary):** Seeking deeper, allusive meanings beneath the literal primarily in the Sufi tradition. Al-Qushayri's Lata'if al-Isharat is the major work. This method is controversial; its validity requires that the allusive meaning not contradict the apparent meaning and is not claimed as the primary or only meaning.

**Tafsir al-Ilmi (Scientific Commentary):** Seeking scientific implications in Quranic verses. Widely practiced in the 20th century; controversial because it risks reading modern science into the text rather than deriving meaning from it. Better used to show compatibility than to extract scientific information.

## Key Tools of the Advanced Mufassir

Asbab al-Nuzul (Occasions of Revelation): The historical circumstances surrounding the revelation of a verse essential for understanding verses about specific events, people, or legal situations.

Nasikh wal-Mansukh (Abrogation): Some verses abrogated the ruling of earlier verses, though the text of both remains. Understanding which verses relate to which stage of the prophetic mission is essential for legal application.

Al-Muhkam wal-Mutashabih (Clear and Ambiguous): Distinguishing between verses with clear, unambiguous meanings and those whose meaning requires interpretation the Quran itself makes this distinction (3:7).

Gharib al-Quran (Unusual Quranic Vocabulary): Some Quranic words appear only once or in unusual usages specialized lexicons document their meanings from classical Arabic poetry and usage.

## Recommended Advanced Tafsir Resources

Tafsir al-Tabari the foundation; available in Arabic; English selections being translated.
Tafsir Ibn Kathir most accessible classical Tafsir in English; available free online.
Tafsir al-Qurtubi essential for legal rulings derived from verses.
Al-Kashshaf (al-Zamakhshari) the masterwork of linguistic and rhetorical analysis; requires advanced Arabic.
Fi Zilal al-Quran (Sayyid Qutb) powerful modern literary Tafsir; read with awareness of some contested positions.
