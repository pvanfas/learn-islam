# Building an Islamic Home

**Difficulty:** Beginner &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Read time:** ~11 min &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Part:** Part 17 Living as a Muslim

---

## In This Chapter

> The home is the foundational institution of Islamic civilization. The Prophet gave more guidance on family life than on almost any other aspect of the deen because the home is where faith is lived, transmitted, and embodied. This chapter covers the Islamic vision of the home: its spiritual atmosphere, the rights and relationships within it, and practical guidance for establishing a home built on the deen.

---

## The Quran's Vision of the Home

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Ar-Rum 30:21**

**Translation:** "And of His signs is that He created for you from yourselves spouses that you may find tranquility in them, and He placed between you affection and mercy. Indeed in that are signs for a people who reflect."

_Three words define the Islamic home: sukun (tranquility), mawaddah (love), rahmah (mercy). The home built on these three is built on divine architecture._
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah At-Tahrim 66:6**

**Translation:** "O you who have believed, protect yourselves and your families from a Fire whose fuel is people and stones..."

_The primary Islamic obligation of the parent is not providing education or career opportunities it is protecting the family from the Fire. Everything else is in service of this._
{% endhint %}

## The Spiritual Atmosphere of the Islamic Home

The home has a spiritual quality that is directly influenced by what happens within it. The Prophet taught:

### Homes That Attract Angels

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih Muslim**

_"Recite Al-Baqarah in your homes for Shaytan does not enter a house in which Surah Al-Baqarah is recited."_
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih al-Bukhari**

_"Make some of your prayers in your homes and do not make them graveyards. Verily, Shaytan flees from the house in which Surah Al-Baqarah is recited."_
{% endhint %}

**Practices that bring blessings and angels into the home:**

- Regular Quran recitation especially Al-Baqarah
- The five prayers performed at home for those who do not go to the mosque
- Saying Bismillah when entering and leaving
- Dhikr of Allah in the morning and evening
- Greeting with Salam upon entering
- Keeping the home physically clean _"Cleanliness is half of faith"_

**Practices that drive angels out:**

- Images of animate beings (according to the majority of scholars)
- Dogs kept inside (though permitted for guarding/working scholars differ)
- Musical instruments (according to the majority of classical scholars)
- Persistent sin and negligence of prayer

## Relationships Within the Home

### The Husband-Wife Relationship

The marital relationship is the axis around which the entire home turns. Its health determines the home's spiritual temperature.

**The Prophet's model:**

- He helped with household tasks mending his sandals, sewing his clothes
- He ate from the same dish as Aisha (RA) and drank from the same spot on her cup
- He raced with Aisha in the desert
- He consulted his wives on important matters
- He never struck a woman

The Quran's command:

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah An-Nisa 4:19**

**Translation:** "And live with them in kindness. Even if you dislike them perhaps you dislike a thing and Allah places in it much good."
{% endhint %}

**Resolving conflict:** The Prophet said: _"Let not a believing man hate a believing woman if he dislikes one characteristic in her, he will be pleased with another."_ (Sahih Muslim) Marital conflict is normal the Islamic home does not demand perfection but teaches how to navigate imperfection with mercy.

### The Parent-Child Relationship

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Al-Isra 17:2324**

**Translation:** "And your Lord has decreed that you not worship except Him, and to parents, good treatment. Whether one or both of them reach old age with you say not to them even 'uff' and do not repel them but speak to them a noble word. And lower to them the wing of humility out of mercy and say: 'My Lord, have mercy upon them as they brought me up when I was small.'"
{% endhint %}

The obligations toward parents in Islam are second only to the obligation to Allah placed immediately after the command to worship Him alone. This is not cultural tradition it is explicit Quranic command.

**What children owe parents:**

- Respectful speech not even saying "uff" (a sound of irritation)
- Physical care in old age
- Financial support when needed
- Regular visits and communication
- Dua for them in life and after death

**What parents owe children:**

- Islamic education and upbringing
- Just treatment between siblings
- Choosing a righteous spouse for them
- Preparing them for independent Islamic life

## Protecting the Home from Harm

The home is either a fortress of faith or a doorway to corruption the difference is what enters it.

### Media and Entertainment

The screen whether television, phone, or computer is the primary vehicle by which values, behaviors, and images enter the home. The Islamic household must be intentional about what enters:

**Questions to ask:**

- Does this content normalize Zina (fornication)?
- Does this content mock religion or Islamic values?
- Does this content contain excessive violence or gore?
- Would I be comfortable watching this with the Prophet present?

**Practical approaches:**

- Limit screen time for children replace with Quran, Islamic stories, physical activity
- Use parental controls on all devices
- Family discussion of what is being watched and why
- Establish device-free zones the dining table, the bedroom, the prayer space

### Music

This is one of the most debated contemporary issues:

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Scholarl positions:**

**Majority position (classical scholars):** Musical instruments particularly those associated with entertainment and revelry (_ma'azif_) are prohibited. A hadith in Sahih al-Bukhari mentions groups who will permit "musical instruments" and this is cited as evidence.

**Contemporary minority position:** Some scholars permit music that is not lascivious, does not contain prohibited content, and is not accompanied by other sins. Nasheed (Islamic vocal music) without instruments is generally accepted.

The principle: whatever enters the home should not violate Sharia and should not habituate the heart to the dunya or weaken the connection to Allah.
{% endhint %}

## The Home as a Place of Worship

The Prophet encouraged making the home a place of worship not treating the mosque as the only place for prayer:

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih al-Bukhari and Muslim**

_"The best prayers for a man after the obligatory ones are those he prays in his home."_
{% endhint %}

**Ways to make the home a place of worship:**

- Designate a prayer area a clean, quiet space with a prayer rug and Qiblah marker
- Pray the Sunnah prayers at home as the Prophet did
- Hold Quran sessions at home even 10 minutes of family Quran reading
- Teach children Quran at home before they learn it elsewhere
- Make dua as a family especially for each other
- Keep Islamic books, art with Quranic calligraphy, and reminders of Allah visible

## The Islamic Home Daily Rhythms

The five daily prayers provide the natural rhythm of the Islamic day. A home organized around them has a built-in spiritual structure:

| Prayer      | Home practice                                                            |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Fajr**    | Wake together, pray together, begin the day with dhikr                   |
| **Dhuhr**   | Lunch together if possible Bismillah, eat together                       |
| **Asr**     | Check in as a family how is everyone's day                               |
| **Maghrib** | Iftar (breaking fast) in Ramadan; family dinner; evening adhkar together |
| **Isha**    | Family Quran or Islamic discussion before sleep; bedtime duas            |

A home where the Adhan is heard and prayers are performed on time has a fundamentally different spiritual quality from one where prayer is treated as optional or individual.

## Key Takeaways

- The Islamic home is built on three divine qualities: tranquility (_Sukun_), love (_Mawaddah_), and mercy (_Rahmah_).
- The primary Islamic obligation of parents is protecting the family from the Fire through Islamic education, modeling, and environment.
- Reciting Al-Baqarah drives Shaytan out and invites angels in the Quran is the home's best spiritual protection.
- The husband-wife relationship sets the home's emotional and spiritual temperature invest in it through kindness, consultation, and the Prophet's example.
- Parents owe children Islamic upbringing; children owe parents respect, care, and dua.
- Protect the home from media that normalizes what Allah has prohibited be intentional about what crosses the threshold.
- Designate a prayer area, maintain family Quran time, and organize the day around the five prayers.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<details>

<summary>Is it permissible to have photos of family members on the walls?</summary>

This is an area of scholarly difference. The majority of classical scholars prohibited images of animate beings (people and animals) displayed on walls in a way that resembles idol worship. Many contemporary scholars, including some from Al-Azhar, permit photographs (camera images) while maintaining the prohibition on hand-drawn or painted images of animate beings. Consult your madhab's ruling. In general: photographs displayed with dignity and not venerated are widely permitted; photographs of inappropriate content are not.

</details>

<details>

<summary>My spouse is not as religiously committed as I am how do I handle this?</summary>

With patience, mercy, and wisdom never coercion. The Prophet was the most religiously committed person alive, yet he had family members who were not Muslim. He treated them with kindness, maintained the relationship, and continued to be an example. Nagging rarely produces religious growth sincere love, the beauty of your own practice, and consistent dua for your spouse are far more effective than ultimatums.

</details>
