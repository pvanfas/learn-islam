# Community and Social Life in Islam

**Difficulty:** Beginner &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Read time:** ~10 min &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Part:** Part 17 Living as a Muslim

---

## In This Chapter

> Islam is not a private religion it is a community religion. The Prophet built a community (_Ummah_) before he built a state. This chapter covers the mosque's role, the rights of neighbors, social responsibility, relations with non-Muslims, and the practical ways a Muslim builds and contributes to community life.

---

## The Islamic Vision of Community

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Al-Hujurat 49:10**

_إِنَّمَا الْمُؤْمِنُونَ إِخْوَةٌ_

**Translation:** "The believers are but brothers so make settlement between your brothers, and fear Allah that you may receive mercy."
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih al-Bukhari and Muslim**

_"The believers in their mutual mercy, compassion, and love for each other are like one body if any organ suffers, the entire body responds with sleeplessness and fever."_
{% endhint %}

The Ummah is not a metaphor it is a real, binding obligation. Muslims are not individuals who happen to share a faith. They are members of a single body, responsible for each other's wellbeing across all boundaries of race, nationality, and class.

## The Mosque The Heart of Community

The **Masjid** (mosque) is not merely a prayer hall in the Islamic tradition it is the community center, the school, the court, and the shelter. The Prophet built the mosque as his very first act in Madinah before his own home.

### Rights and Etiquette of the Mosque

**When entering:**

- Enter with the right foot
- Pray two Rak'ahs of greeting (_Tahiyyat al-Masjid_) before sitting
- Maintain quiet do not disturb those in prayer
- Say the dua for entering: _"Allahumma iftah li abwaba rahmatik"_

**While in the mosque:**

- Do not conduct trade or business transactions inside
- Do not raise voices unnecessarily
- Do not step over people to find a seat fill gaps from the front
- The mosque is a sacred space maintain its cleanliness and sanctity

**The reward of the mosque:**

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih al-Bukhari and Muslim**

_"Prayer in congregation is 27 times more rewarding than prayer alone."_

_"Whoever goes to the mosque in the morning or evening, Allah prepares for him a gift in Paradise for each morning and evening he does so."_
{% endhint %}

### The Mosque Beyond Prayer

The mosque should be a living center of community life:

- **Islamic education** classes, halaqahs (study circles), Quran memorization
- **Social support** food banks, counseling, assistance for the needy
- **Conflict resolution** following the Prophet's example of the mosque as a place of justice
- **Youth programs** keeping young Muslims connected to the community
- **Da'wah** open days, interfaith events, information for non-Muslims

A mosque that is only open for five prayers is functioning below its potential. The Prophet's mosque in Madinah was open and active all day.

## Rights of Neighbors (_Huquq al-Jaar_)

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih al-Bukhari and Muslim**

The Prophet said: _"Jibreel kept advising me about the neighbor until I thought he would make the neighbor an heir."_

And: _"By Allah, he does not truly believe! By Allah, he does not truly believe! By Allah, he does not truly believe!" He was asked: "Who, O Messenger of Allah?" He said: "One whose neighbor is not safe from his harm."_
{% endhint %}

**Who is "the neighbor"?** The Prophet defined it as the **40 houses in every direction**. The rights of neighbors apply regardless of their faith, race, or character.

**The rights of neighbors in Islamic law:**

| Right                      | Detail                                                                 |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Safety from harm**       | No noise, no encroachment, no damage to their property                 |
| **Sharing food**           | When cooking, send some to neighbors the Prophet instructed this       |
| **Visiting in illness**    | Check on sick neighbors                                                |
| **Assisting in hardship**  | Help when they are struggling                                          |
| **Privacy**                | Do not peer into neighbors' homes; do not spread their private affairs |
| **Bearing harm patiently** | Tolerate minor irritations without escalating                          |

The Prophet specifically mentioned: _"If you cook a stew, add extra water and give some to your neighbors."_ (Sahih Muslim) This is not optional generosity it is a right.

## Social Responsibility _Amr bil-Ma'ruf wa Nahy 'anil-Munkar_

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Al-Imran 3:110**

**Translation:** "You are the best nation produced for mankind you enjoin what is right and forbid what is wrong and believe in Allah..."
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih Muslim**

_"Whoever among you sees an evil, let him change it with his hand. If he is unable to do so, then with his tongue. If he is unable to do so, then with his heart and that is the weakest of faith."_
{% endhint %}

**How this works in practice:**

- **With the hand:** Those in authority parents in the home, leaders in the mosque, rulers in the state have the right and obligation to actively stop wrong within their authority
- **With the tongue:** Speaking, writing, advising available to all Muslims with knowledge and wisdom
- **With the heart:** Hating the wrong inwardly without being able to act the minimum of faith

**Important limits:** This principle does not give every Muslim license to be a self-appointed moral police. The Prophet warned against harsh, divisive, or arrogant approaches to correction. Wisdom (_Hikmah_) and beautiful speech (_Maw'izhah Hasanah_) are commanded:

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah An-Nahl 16:125**

**Translation:** "Invite to the way of your Lord with wisdom and good instruction, and argue with them in a way that is best..."
{% endhint %}

## Relations with Non-Muslims

The Islamic community exists within a larger society and Islam is explicit about how to relate to it.

**The principle:**

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Al-Mumtahanah 60:8**

**Translation:** "Allah does not forbid you from being kind and equitable to those who have not fought you because of religion and have not driven you from your homes. Indeed, Allah loves those who act with equity."
{% endhint %}

**What Islam requires:**

- Justice to all regardless of faith
- Honoring agreements and contracts
- Truthfulness in dealings
- Kindness to neighbors and colleagues
- Participation in shared civic goods roads, schools, public safety

**The Prophet's example:** He had Jewish neighbors, Christian allies, and polytheist relatives. He paid the debts of non-Muslims. He visited sick non-Muslims. He stood in respect when a Jewish funeral procession passed. He honored the rights of non-Muslim subjects (_Dhimmis_) with formal legal protections.

## Civic Participation and Volunteering

A Muslim minority community has a responsibility to the broader society it lives within:

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih al-Bukhari**

_"The most beloved people to Allah are those who are most beneficial to people."_
{% endhint %}

**Forms of community service:**

- Volunteering at food banks, homeless shelters, and community organizations
- Participating in neighborhood improvement
- Supporting non-Muslim charitable causes that align with Islamic values
- Professional service Muslim doctors, lawyers, teachers, and counselors serving the whole community
- Environmental care _"The world is green and beautiful, and Allah has appointed you as His stewards"_

The Prophet praised the pre-Islamic _Hilf al-Fudul_ (Pact of Justice) that he had participated in as a young man showing that working for justice alongside non-Muslims in shared civic projects is honored in Islam.

---

## Key Takeaways

- The Ummah is a binding obligation believers are one body; one's suffering is everyone's concern.
- The mosque is the community center not just a prayer hall and should be active with education, support, and da'wah.
- Neighbors (40 houses in every direction) have specific rights: safety, food sharing, illness visits, and assistance in hardship.
- _Amr bil-Ma'ruf wa Nahy 'anil-Munkar_ (commanding good and forbidding wrong) is a community obligation done with wisdom and beautiful speech.
- Justice and kindness to non-Muslims is commanded civic participation in shared goods is encouraged.
- The most beloved people to Allah are those most beneficial to people service is worship.
