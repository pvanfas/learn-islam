## What You Will Learn in This Part

Islam is not confined to the mosque or the prayer mat. It is a complete way of life (_Deen_) that shapes how a Muslim wakes up, eats, speaks, works, loves, raises children, grieves, and relates to their community. This part translates Islamic belief and law into the texture of daily lived experience.

Whether you are a new Muslim figuring out how to practice in a non-Muslim environment, a lifelong Muslim seeking to deepen your practice, or a student of Islam wanting to understand what the religion looks like when lived this part is your guide.
