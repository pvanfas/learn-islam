# The Beginning of Revelation

**Difficulty:** Beginner &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Read time:** ~9 min &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Part:** Part 6 Seerah

---

## In This Chapter

> The night in the cave of Hira is the most important night in human history since the birth of Isa (AS). This chapter describes the first revelation in full detail, the Prophet's terrifying experience, Khadijah's response, and the beginning of the first Muslim community the small group who would change the world.

---

## The Night in the Cave of Hira 610 CE

It was the month of **Ramadan**, in the year **610 CE**. Muhammad was 40 years old. He was alone in the cave of Hira on Jabal an-Nur the Mountain of Light outside Makkah, in his customary solitary retreat.

Then it happened.

### The First Encounter with Jibreel

Without warning, a presence seized him compressed him with an embrace so overwhelming that he later described it as feeling that his very soul would leave his body. Then the presence released him and commanded:

{% hint style="info" %}
**اقْرَأْ**

_"Iqra'!"_ **"Read!"**
{% endhint %}

The Prophet , shaking, replied: _"I am not one who reads."_

The embrace came again crushing him and released him again. Again: _"Iqra'!"_

Again: _"I am not one who reads."_

A third time, the embrace. A third release. And then the words came the first words of divine revelation ever given to Muhammad :

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Al-Alaq 96:15**

_اقْرَأْ بِاسْمِ رَبِّكَ الَّذِي خَلَقَ ۝ خَلَقَ الْإِنسَانَ مِنْ عَلَقٍ ۝ اقْرَأْ وَرَبُّكَ الْأَكْرَمُ ۝ الَّذِي عَلَّمَ بِالْقَلَمِ ۝ عَلَّمَ الْإِنسَانَ مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ_

**Transliteration:** _Iqra' bismi rabbika alladhi khalaq. Khalaqal-insana min alaq. Iqra' wa rabbukal-akram. Alladhi 'allama bil-qalam. 'Allamal-insana ma lam ya'lam._

**Translation:** "Read in the name of your Lord who created created man from a clinging substance. Read, and your Lord is the Most Generous who taught by the pen taught man that which he knew not."

_These are the first words of the Quran. Revelation began with a command to read to learn from the Lord who taught humanity everything it knows._
{% endhint %}

---

## Running to Khadijah "Cover Me, Cover Me"

The Prophet fled the cave, trembling violently, his heart pounding. He ran home to Khadijah and cried: _"Zammiluni! Zammiluni!"_ **"Cover me! Cover me!"**

She wrapped him in a garment. He lay there shaking until the fear subsided. Then he told her everything that had happened.

Khadijah's response was immediate, calm, and unwavering. She did not doubt him for a moment. She said:

_"By Allah, Allah will never humiliate you. You maintain family ties, you bear the burdens of others, you help the destitute, you serve your guests generously, and you assist those who have been struck by calamity."_

Then she took him to her cousin **Waraqah ibn Nawfal** an elderly Christian scholar who had read the Torah and the Gospel in their original languages. When Muhammad described what had happened, Waraqah said:

_"This is the same Namus (Angel of Revelation Jibreel) that Allah sent to Musa (Moses). Would that I were young and could live until the time when your people will drive you out!"_

The Prophet was astonished: _"Will they drive me out?"_

_"Yes. No man has ever brought what you bring except that his people turned against him and fought him. If I live to that day, I will support you with all my strength."_ But Waraqah was very old, and he died shortly after.

---

## The Period of Pause Al-Fatrah

After the first revelation, there was a pause a period of silence in which no new revelation came. The Prophet fell into deep distress and longing. He missed the presence of revelation. He would sometimes climb to the mountaintops, overwhelmed by the separation.

Then one day, while walking, he heard a voice from the sky. He looked up and saw Jibreel sitting on a throne between the heavens and the earth, filling the entire horizon with his true form.

Overwhelmed, the Prophet returned home trembling, and said: _"Cover me! Cover me!"_ Once wrapped, the next revelation came:

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Al-Muddaththir 74:17**

**Translation:** "O you who covers himself arise and warn. And your Lord glorify. And your garments purify. And unclean things avoid. And do not confer favor to acquire more. And for the sake of your Lord, be patient."
{% endhint %}

This was the moment the prophetic mission formally began. The first revelation (_Al-Alaq_) announced who was speaking Allah, the Lord who creates and teaches. This second revelation gave the command: _arise and warn._ The mission was on.

## The First Muslims Who Believed First?

The Prophet began sharing the message quietly, privately, with those closest to him.

### The First Four

| Person                           | Relationship                         | Category                 |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| **Khadijah bint Khuwaylid (RA)** | His wife                             | First woman, first adult |
| **Ali ibn Abi Talib (RA)**       | His young cousin, living in his home | First child              |
| **Zayd ibn Harithah (RA)**       | His freed slave, like an adopted son | First freed slave        |
| **Abu Bakr al-Siddiq (RA)**      | His closest friend                   | First free adult man     |

Abu Bakr (RA) then used his influence and social connections to bring several other prominent Makkans to Islam among them Uthman ibn Affan, Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf, Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas, and others who would become the most prominent Companions.

---

## The House of Al-Arqam The First School

The early Muslims gathered secretly at the house of **Al-Arqam ibn Abi al-Arqam** a young Companion whose home on Safa hill became the first gathering place for the Muslim community. There the Prophet taught the Quran as it was revealed, answered questions, and built the spiritual foundations of the first generation.

For three years, the mission was conducted quietly building a core community of sincere, deeply committed believers before the public declaration.

---

## The Public Declaration Three Years In

When Allah revealed:

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Al-Hijr 15:94**

**Translation:** "So proclaim what you are commanded and turn away from the polytheists."
{% endhint %}

The Prophet climbed to the top of Mount Safa and called out to the Quraysh tribe by tribe. When they gathered, he asked them: _"If I told you that an army was coming from behind this mountain would you believe me?"_

They said: _"We have never known you to lie."_

He said: _"Then I am a warner to you before a severe punishment."_

His uncle **Abu Lahab** immediately replied: _"Destruction to you! Did you gather us for this?"_

The reaction was immediate and hostile. The mission had been declared. The persecution was about to begin.

---

## Key Takeaways

- The first revelation came in the cave of Hira in 610 CE the month of Ramadan when Muhammad was 40 years old.
- Jibreel (AS) embraced the Prophet three times and then recited the first five verses of Surah Al-Alaq.
- Khadijah (RA) was the first to believe responding with immediate faith and calm support.
- Waraqah ibn Nawfal recognized the revelation as the same that came to Musa (AS).
- After the period of pause (_Fatrah_), Surah Al-Muddaththir gave the formal command to warn the mission had begun.
- The first Muslims were Khadijah, Ali, Zayd, and Abu Bakr (RA) then a small circle who gathered secretly in the house of Al-Arqam.
- Three years of private calling was followed by a public declaration on Mount Safa met with immediate hostility from the Quraysh.
