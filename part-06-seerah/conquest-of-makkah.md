# The Conquest of Makkah — 8 AH / 630 CE

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Beginner &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Read time:** ~8 min &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Part:** Part 6 — Seerah

---

## 📌 In This Chapter

> The Conquest of Makkah is one of the most remarkable events in human history — an army of 10,000 entering the city that had persecuted them for 21 years, and responding with a general amnesty that left almost no blood spilled. This chapter covers how it happened and what it reveals about the character of the Prophet ﷺ and the nature of the religion he brought.

---

## 🌙 How the Conquest Happened

The Treaty of Hudaybiyyah established ten years of peace. It was broken when the Quraysh-allied Banu Bakr tribe attacked the Muslim-allied Banu Khuza'ah — a violation of the treaty's terms.

The Prophet ﷺ moved with extraordinary speed and secrecy. He mobilized **10,000 Companions** — an army the like of which Arabia had never seen — and marched toward Makkah.

The Quraysh leadership, led by Abu Sufyan ibn Harb (their chief), went out to assess the situation. He encountered Abbas, the Prophet's ﷺ uncle, who brought him to the Prophet ﷺ himself.

The Prophet ﷺ said to Abbas: *"Take Abu Sufyan and keep him at the mountain pass until the armies of Allah pass by him."*

Abu Sufyan watched — army after army of Muslims passing in silence, organized discipline, and numbers beyond anything he had imagined. He said to Abbas: *"Your nephew has acquired a mighty kingdom."*

Abbas replied: *"This is not a kingdom — this is prophethood."*

Abu Sufyan embraced Islam that day.

---

## 🕊️ The General Amnesty — "Go, You Are Free"

The Prophet ﷺ entered Makkah on horseback, his head bowed in humility and gratitude — reciting Surah Al-Fath. He went directly to the Ka'bah, circled it, and destroyed the 360 idols — reciting:

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Al-Isra 17:81**

*وَقُلْ جَاءَ الْحَقُّ وَزَهَقَ الْبَاطِلُ إِنَّ الْبَاطِلَ كَانَ زَهُوقًا*

**Translation:** "And say: Truth has come and falsehood has departed. Indeed, falsehood was bound to depart."
{% endhint %}

Then the moment came that every Makkan had feared. The Prophet ﷺ gathered the Quraysh — including those who had tortured his Companions, killed his family members, and driven him from his home — and asked them:

*"O Quraysh, what do you think I will do with you?"*

They said: *"Good — you are a noble brother and the son of a noble brother."*

He said: *"Go — for you are free."*

*Irhabuu fa antum al-tulaqaa'.*

Not one act of revenge. Not a single execution of those who had persecuted the Muslims for 21 years. Even Hind — the woman who had ordered the killing of Hamza (RA) and chewed on his liver on the battlefield of Uhud — was pardoned.

The only exception was a small list of individuals guilty of specific crimes (such as murder) — and even several of these were eventually pardoned.

---

## 🕌 The First Adhan from the Ka'bah

The Prophet ﷺ called Bilal (RA) to the top of the Ka'bah — the very structure that had housed 360 idols — and commanded him to give the call to prayer.

Bilal — the enslaved man who had been tortured in this very city for saying *"Ahad! Ahad!"* — now stood above the Ka'bah and called out:

**"Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar..."**

The people of Makkah entered Islam in their thousands.

---

## ✅ Key Takeaways

- The Conquest of Makkah in 8 AH (630 CE) came after the Quraysh violated the Treaty of Hudaybiyyah.
- 10,000 Muslims entered Makkah — the largest Muslim force in history to that point.
- The Prophet ﷺ declared a general amnesty: *"Go, you are free"* — to those who had persecuted him for 21 years.
- The Ka'bah was cleansed of idols and restored to the pure monotheistic sanctuary of Ibrahim (AS).
- Bilal (RA) gave the first Adhan from the top of the Ka'bah — a moment of profound historical symmetry.
