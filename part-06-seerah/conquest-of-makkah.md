# The Conquest of Makkah 8 AH / 630 CE

**Difficulty:** Beginner &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Read time:** ~8 min &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Part:** Part 6 Seerah

---

## In This Chapter

> The Conquest of Makkah is one of the most remarkable events in human history an army of 10,000 entering the city that had persecuted them for 21 years, and responding with a general amnesty that left almost no blood spilled. This chapter covers how it happened and what it reveals about the character of the Prophet and the nature of the religion he brought.

---

## How the Conquest Happened

The Treaty of Hudaybiyyah established ten years of peace. It was broken when the Quraysh-allied Banu Bakr tribe attacked the Muslim-allied Banu Khuza'ah a violation of the treaty's terms.

The Prophet moved with extraordinary speed and secrecy. He mobilized **10,000 Companions** an army the like of which Arabia had never seen and marched toward Makkah.

The Quraysh leadership, led by Abu Sufyan ibn Harb (their chief), went out to assess the situation. He encountered Abbas, the Prophet's uncle, who brought him to the Prophet himself.

The Prophet said to Abbas: _"Take Abu Sufyan and keep him at the mountain pass until the armies of Allah pass by him."_

Abu Sufyan watched army after army of Muslims passing in silence, organized discipline, and numbers beyond anything he had imagined. He said to Abbas: _"Your nephew has acquired a mighty kingdom."_

Abbas replied: _"This is not a kingdom this is prophethood."_

Abu Sufyan embraced Islam that day.

---

## The General Amnesty "Go, You Are Free"

The Prophet entered Makkah on horseback, his head bowed in humility and gratitude reciting Surah Al-Fath. He went directly to the Ka'bah, circled it, and destroyed the 360 idols reciting:

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Al-Isra 17:81**

_وَقُلْ جَاءَ الْحَقُّ وَزَهَقَ الْبَاطِلُ إِنَّ الْبَاطِلَ كَانَ زَهُوقًا_

**Translation:** "And say: Truth has come and falsehood has departed. Indeed, falsehood was bound to depart."
{% endhint %}

Then the moment came that every Makkan had feared. The Prophet gathered the Quraysh including those who had tortured his Companions, killed his family members, and driven him from his home and asked them:

_"O Quraysh, what do you think I will do with you?"_

They said: _"Good you are a noble brother and the son of a noble brother."_

He said: _"Go for you are free."_

_Irhabuu fa antum al-tulaqaa'._

Not one act of revenge. Not a single execution of those who had persecuted the Muslims for 21 years. Even Hind the woman who had ordered the killing of Hamza (RA) and chewed on his liver on the battlefield of Uhud was pardoned.

The only exception was a small list of individuals guilty of specific crimes (such as murder) and even several of these were eventually pardoned.

---

## The First Adhan from the Ka'bah

The Prophet called Bilal (RA) to the top of the Ka'bah the very structure that had housed 360 idols and commanded him to give the call to prayer.

Bilal the enslaved man who had been tortured in this very city for saying _"Ahad! Ahad!"_ now stood above the Ka'bah and called out:

**"Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar..."**

The people of Makkah entered Islam in their thousands.

---

## Key Takeaways

- The Conquest of Makkah in 8 AH (630 CE) came after the Quraysh violated the Treaty of Hudaybiyyah.
- 10,000 Muslims entered Makkah the largest Muslim force in history to that point.
- The Prophet declared a general amnesty: _"Go, you are free"_ to those who had persecuted him for 21 years.
- The Ka'bah was cleansed of idols and restored to the pure monotheistic sanctuary of Ibrahim (AS).
- Bilal (RA) gave the first Adhan from the top of the Ka'bah a moment of profound historical symmetry.
