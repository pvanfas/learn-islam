## What You Will Learn in This Part

*Seerah* (سِيرَة) means biography — specifically the biography of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ. But it is far more than a historical account. The Seerah is the living proof of the Quran applied in human form. It is the record of how a man transformed himself, his family, his city, his people, and ultimately the world — guided entirely by revelation.

The Prophet ﷺ is central to Islam in a way that is unique: Muslims believe in the Quran because of the Prophet ﷺ, and they understand the Quran through the Prophet ﷺ. His life is therefore not merely inspirational — it is *constitutive* of the religion itself.

Non-Muslim historians, philosophers, and writers have also recognized the extraordinary nature of his life and impact. Michael Hart, in his book *The 100: A Ranking of the Most Influential Persons in History*, ranked Muhammad ﷺ first — the single most influential human being in history. This part covers his life from birth to death — with the context, the emotion, and the spiritual significance that the bare facts alone cannot convey.

## 📖 Opening — Why Study the Seerah?

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Al-Ahzab 33:21**

*لَّقَدْ كَانَ لَكُمْ فِي رَسُولِ اللَّهِ أُسْوَةٌ حَسَنَةٌ*

**Translation:** "There has certainly been for you in the Messenger of Allah an excellent pattern of conduct — for whoever hopes in Allah and the Last Day and remembers Allah often."

*This verse is the entire justification for studying the Seerah: the Prophet ﷺ is the living model. To know what Islam looks like in action — look at his life.*
{% endhint %}
