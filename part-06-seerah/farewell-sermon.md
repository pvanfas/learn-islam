# The Farewell Sermon and the Prophet's Final Days

**Difficulty:** Beginner &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Read time:** ~9 min &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Part:** Part 6 Seerah

---

## In This Chapter

> In 10 AH (632 CE), the Prophet performed his only Hajj known as the Farewell Pilgrimage (_Hajjat al-Wada'_). He delivered a sermon on the plain of Arafah that is considered one of the greatest speeches in human history. Two months later, he was gone. This chapter covers his final words, his final days, and the moment that shook the Muslim world.

---

## The Farewell Pilgrimage 10 AH / 632 CE

The Prophet performed Hajj only once in his life in the tenth year after the Hijrah. He was accompanied by between 90,000 and 124,000 Companions a gathering of a size Arabia had never seen.

On the ninth of Dhul Hijjah, standing on the plain of **Arafah** the central station of Hajj the Prophet delivered a sermon that would be the last he gave to a mass gathering. He seemed to know it was his last.

He began: _"O people listen to my words carefully, for I do not know whether I will meet you in this place after this year."_

---

## The Farewell Sermon Key Teachings

The sermon addressed the entire human family not only the Muslims present. Its principles remain among the most powerful declarations of human rights and equality ever spoken:

### On Human Equality

> _"O people your Lord is One and your father (Adam) is one. There is no superiority of an Arab over a non-Arab, nor a non-Arab over an Arab. There is no superiority of a white person over a black person, nor a black person over a white person except through taqwa (God-consciousness) and good deeds."_

In a world stratified by race, tribe, and birth this declaration was revolutionary.

### On the Sanctity of Life and Property

> _"Your blood, your property, and your honor are sacred as sacred as this day of yours, in this month of yours, in this city of yours."_

### On Women's Rights

> _"Fear Allah regarding women. You have taken them as a trust from Allah, and intimacy with them has been made lawful for you by the word of Allah. Your rights over them are that they should not allow anyone you dislike to come into your homes. Their rights over you are that you provide for them and clothe them with kindness."_

### On Economic Justice

> _"Every form of interest (Riba) is abolished. The first interest I abolish is that of my uncle Abbas ibn Abd al-Muttalib."_

### On the Preservation of the Message

> _"I am leaving behind me two things; if you hold to them you will never go astray the Book of Allah and my Sunnah."_

### The Final Question

The Prophet then asked the vast assembly: _"Have I conveyed the message?"_

The crowd answered: _"Yes!"_

He raised his finger to the sky and said: _"O Allah, bear witness. O Allah, bear witness. O Allah, bear witness."_

Then the final revelation of the Quran came:

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Al-Ma'idah 5:3**

_الْيَوْمَ أَكْمَلْتُ لَكُمْ دِينَكُمْ وَأَتْمَمْتُ عَلَيْكُمْ نِعْمَتِي وَرَضِيتُ لَكُمُ الْإِسْلَامَ دِينًا_

**Translation:** "This day I have perfected for you your religion and completed My favor upon you and have approved for you Islam as religion."

_When Umar (RA) heard this verse, he wept. Someone asked him why he was crying at such a joyful revelation. He said: "After perfection comes nothing but decline."_
{% endhint %}

## The Final Illness Safar and Rabi' al-Awwal, 11 AH

Two months after the Farewell Pilgrimage, the Prophet fell seriously ill. He requested permission from his wives to spend his illness in the room of Aisha (RA) they agreed.

In his final days, he:

- Led prayer while barely able to stand, then asked Abu Bakr (RA) to lead the people in prayer
- Said: _"Allah has cursed the Jews and Christians who made the graves of their prophets into places of worship do not make my grave a place of worship"_
- Said: _"Prayer, prayer and those whom your right hands possess"_ (care for the weak and vulnerable)
- Repeatedly said: _"Al-Rafeeq al-A'la the Highest Companion"_ indicating his choice to depart to Allah

### The Final Moments

Aisha (RA) described his final moments:

_"The Prophet was lying with his head in my lap when I sensed a change. I looked at him and saw his eyes were fixed upward. I heard him say: 'With the highest companions in Paradise.' And then his hand went heavy in mine."_

He was 63 years old. It was Monday, 12 Rabi' al-Awwal, 11 AH corresponding to June 632 CE.

---

## The Impact of His Death

The news of his death shook the community to its foundations.

Umar (RA) the powerful, fearless man who would become the second Caliph drew his sword and declared that anyone who said the Prophet had died would be killed. He could not accept it.

Abu Bakr (RA) the most beloved friend of the Prophet composed himself, entered, uncovered his face, kissed him between the eyes, and said: _"My father and mother be your ransom you were beautiful in life and beautiful in death."_

He then went to Umar and addressed the people:

_"O people whoever worshipped Muhammad, Muhammad has died. And whoever worshipped Allah Allah is Ever-Living and never dies."_

He then recited:

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Al-Imran 3:144**

**Translation:** "Muhammad is not but a messenger. Other messengers have passed on before him. So if he dies or is killed, will you turn back on your heels?"
{% endhint %}

Umar said: _"By Allah, it was as if I had never heard that verse before."_

---

## His Burial

He was washed, wrapped in three white Yemeni cloths, and buried in Aisha's room where he had died. The room later became part of the expanding Masjid an-Nabawi.

Muslims today visit his grave and send blessings upon him with the deep conviction that:

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih Abu Dawud**

_"Whoever sends blessings upon me at my grave I hear him. And whoever sends blessings upon me from afar it is conveyed to me."_
{% endhint %}

---

## Key Takeaways

- The Farewell Sermon on the plain of Arafah stands as one of the greatest declarations of human equality and rights in history.
- Its core message: human equality regardless of race, the sanctity of life and property, the rights of women, the abolition of interest, and the Quran and Sunnah as the preserved legacy.
- The final Quranic revelation declared the religion complete and perfected.
- The Prophet died in Aisha's (RA) arms on 12 Rabi' al-Awwal, 11 AH aged 63.
- Abu Bakr's (RA) response anchored the community: Allah lives and the mission continues.
