# Prayer Times Fajr to Isha

**Difficulty:** Beginner &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Read time:** ~7 min &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Part:** Part 4: Five Pillars Salah

---

## In This Chapter

> Understanding when each prayer is due and what marks its beginning and end is essential for every Muslim. This chapter explains the five prayer times, their Quranic basis, how they are determined astronomically, and practical guidance for maintaining prayer times in any part of the world.

---

## Quranic Foundation

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah An-Nisa 4:103**

**Translation:** "Indeed, prayer has been decreed upon the believers at specified times."

_The five prayers are not merely recommended at certain times they are obligatory at specific, divinely appointed intervals. Missing a prayer time without excuse is a serious matter._
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Hud 11:114**

**Translation:** "And establish prayer at the two ends of the day and at the approach of the night. Indeed, good deeds do away with misdeeds. That is a reminder for those who remember."

_This verse references Fajr, Maghrib, and Isha showing the Quran itself structured the day around prayer._
{% endhint %}

## The Five Daily Prayer Times

Each prayer has a **beginning time** (when it becomes valid to pray) and an **ending time** (after which it has been missed and must be made up). The times are determined by the position of the sun.

---

### Fajr The Dawn Prayer

**Rak'ahs:** 2 obligatory; 2 Sunnah before

**Time begins:** At **true dawn** (_Fajr Sadiq_) when a horizontal white light appears along the entire eastern horizon (not the false dawn, _Fajr Kadhib_, which appears as a column of light then disappears)

**Time ends:** At **sunrise** the moment the sun's disc begins to appear

**The significance:** Fajr is prayed in darkness before the world wakes, before the day's distractions begin. The Prophet said: _"The prayer most burdensome to the hypocrites is Isha and Fajr. But if they only knew what was in them, they would come even if crawling."_ (Sahih al-Bukhari)

**The 2 Sunnah Rak'ahs of Fajr** are among the most emphasized of all Sunnah prayers: _"The two Rak'ahs of Fajr are better than the world and all it contains."_ (Sahih Muslim)

---

### Dhuhr The Midday Prayer

**Rak'ahs:** 4 obligatory; 4 Sunnah before + 2 after

**Time begins:** When the sun passes its **zenith** (highest point) and begins to decline the shadow of an object begins to grow after being at its shortest

**Time ends:** When the shadow of any object equals its own length (plus the shadow at noon) roughly mid-afternoon

**Replaced by Jumu'ah on Friday:** For men attending the Friday congregation, Dhuhr is replaced by the 2 Rak'ahs of Jumu'ah prayer.

---

### Asr The Afternoon Prayer

**Rak'ahs:** 4 obligatory; 4 Sunnah before (confirmed but not as strongly emphasized)

**Time begins:** When the shadow of an object equals its own length (Hanafi: when the shadow equals twice its length)

**Time ends:** At **sunset**

{% hint style="warning" %}
**The Hanafi-Other madhab difference on Asr time:** The Hanafi school holds that Asr begins when the shadow equals twice the object's length making Asr begin later than in the other three madhabs. Both positions are valid; follow your madhab's ruling.
{% endhint %}

**The most guarded prayer:** The Quran specifically calls Asr "the middle prayer" (_As-Salat al-Wusta_ (Surah Al-Baqarah 2:238)) and commands it be guarded above all others. It is also the prayer most commonly missed by busy people in the middle of the working day set a reminder.

---

### Maghrib The Sunset Prayer

**Rak'ahs:** 3 obligatory; 2 Sunnah after

**Time begins:** Immediately at **sunset** the moment the sun's disc disappears completely below the horizon

**Time ends:** When the red twilight disappears from the sky (according to the majority); when the white twilight disappears (Hanafi)

**The iftar prayer:** Maghrib is the prayer of breaking the fast the most joyful daily moment in Ramadan. The Prophet commanded hastening to break the fast and then pray Maghrib before eating the full meal.

---

### Isha The Night Prayer

**Rak'ahs:** 4 obligatory; 2 Sunnah after; Witr (at least 1 Rak'ah) at the end of the night

**Time begins:** When the red twilight disappears (majority) / white twilight disappears (Hanafi)

**Time ends:** Before **Fajr** but the preferred time is before midnight; delaying beyond midnight is disliked though still valid

**Preferred time:** Slightly after its beginning the Prophet loved to delay Isha when it was not burdensome on the community.

Ask your local mosque or scholar which method to use in your region.

## Prohibited Prayer Times

The Prophet identified three times during which voluntary prayers (Nawafil) should not be prayed:

1. **At sunrise:** until the sun has fully risen and is the height of a spear above the horizon (approximately 20 minutes)
2. **When the sun is at its zenith** (exactly at midday) until it begins to decline
3. **From Asr until sunset**

**Exception:** Obligatory prayers (Fard) may be made up at any time if you missed Fajr and realize it at noon, pray it immediately regardless of the time prohibition.

## Combining Prayers (_Jam'_)

Islamic law permits combining Dhuhr + Asr, and Maghrib + Isha in specific circumstances:

**When combining is permitted:**

- During travel (_Safar_) when covering a qualifying distance (approximately 80km)
- During heavy rain (Maliki and Hanbali praying in congregation at the mosque)
- During illness that makes separate prayer difficult
- During extreme hardship

**Combining methods:**

- _Jam' Taqdim_ (advance combination) praying the second prayer early at the first prayer's time
- _Jam' Ta'khir_ (delay combination) delaying the first prayer to the second prayer's time

**Madhab differences:** The Hanafi school is more restrictive on combining permitting it primarily during travel, and even then some Hanafi scholars prefer praying at the correct time whenever possible. Follow your madhab's specific conditions.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Each of the five prayers has a specific, divinely appointed time window beginning and ending at astronomical markers.
- **Fajr:** True dawn → sunrise. **Dhuhr:** Sun zenith → Asr. **Asr:** Shadow = height → sunset. **Maghrib:** Sunset → twilight. **Isha:** Twilight → Fajr.
- The Hanafi school differs from others on Asr's start time shadow equals twice the object's height.
- Prayer time apps provide accurate local times choose the calculation method used by scholars in your region.
- Three voluntary-prayer-free times exist; obligatory prayers may be made up at any time.
- Combining prayers is permitted during travel, illness, and hardship with madhab-specific conditions.
