# Performing the Prayer Step by Step

**Difficulty:** Beginner &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Read time:** ~15 min &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Part:** Part 4 The Five Pillars Salah

---

## What This Guide Covers

> This is the complete, step-by-step guide to performing Salah the Islamic prayer. By the end, you will know every movement, every position, and every word recited in a standard two-Rak'ah prayer, with Arabic text, transliteration, and translation for everything.

---

## Before You Begin Prerequisites

{% hint style="warning" %}
**You must have the following before praying:**

- A state of **Wudu** (ritual purity) see [Preparation Wudu, Ghusl & Tayammum](preparation.md)
- Clean body, clothes, and place of prayer
- Awrah (area of body that must be covered) covered
- Knowledge of the direction of the **Qiblah** (facing Makkah)
- Prayer time must have entered see [Prayer Times](prayer-times.md)
  {% endhint %}

## Islamic Basis

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Al-Baqarah 2:238**

**Translation:** "Maintain with care the [obligatory] prayers and the middle prayer, and stand before Allah devoutly obedient."
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih al-Bukhari**

The Prophet said: _"Pray as you have seen me pray."_

_This single hadith is the foundation of how Salah is performed. The Prophet demonstrated prayer physically, and the Companions transmitted that demonstration to their students an unbroken chain of physical transmission over 1,400 years._
{% endhint %}

## Structure of a Prayer

A prayer is made of **Rak'ahs** (units). Each of the five daily prayers has a different number:

| Prayer           | Rak'ahs |
| ---------------- | ------- |
| Fajr (Dawn)      | 2       |
| Dhuhr (Midday)   | 4       |
| Asr (Afternoon)  | 4       |
| Maghrib (Sunset) | 3       |
| Isha (Night)     | 4       |

This guide will teach you one complete Rak'ah, then show how they connect.

## Step-by-Step Guide

> **Tip:** Read through all steps once before attempting. Then follow step by step.

---

### Step 1 The Intention (_Niyyah_)

**What to do:** Stand upright facing the Qiblah (the direction of Makkah). Make the intention in your heart for which prayer you are about to perform.

**Important:** The Niyyah does not need to be spoken aloud. It is an act of the heart, not the tongue. Simply intend: _"I am praying the Fajr prayer (or whichever prayer), for the sake of Allah."_

{% hint style="tip" %}
**Note:** The intention is a condition of the prayer, not a verbal formula. Many people incorrectly recite a specific phrase aloud before every prayer this was not the practice of the Prophet and is unnecessary.
{% endhint %}

---

### Step 2 The Opening Takbir (_Takbirat al-Ihram_)

**What to do:** Raise both hands to the level of your shoulders (or earlobes), palms facing forward, and say:

{% hint style="info" %}
**اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ**

**Transliteration:** _Allahu Akbar_

**Translation:** "Allah is the Greatest"
{% endhint %}

**Why:** This Takbir is called _Takbirat al-Ihram_ the Takbir of entering the sacred state. With these words, you have entered the prayer. From this moment, all worldly concerns are to be set aside. You are in the presence of Allah.

After saying _Allahu Akbar_, lower your hands and fold them over your chest right hand over left.

---

### Step 3 The Opening Supplication (_Du'a al-Istiftah_)

**What to do:** Quietly recite the opening supplication (_Sunnah recommended, not obligatory_):

{% hint style="info" %}
**سُبْحَانَكَ اللَّهُمَّ وَبِحَمْدِكَ وَتَبَارَكَ اسْمُكَ وَتَعَالَىٰ جَدُّكَ وَلَا إِلَٰهَ غَيْرُكَ**

**Transliteration:** _Subhanakal-lahumma wa bihamdika wa tabarakasmuka wa ta'ala jadduka wa la ilaha ghairuk_

**Translation:** "Glory be to You, O Allah, and with Your praise. Blessed is Your name and exalted is Your majesty. There is no god but You."

_(Source: Abu Dawud, authenticated)_
{% endhint %}

---

### Step 4 Reciting Al-Fatihah

**What to do:** Recite _Surah Al-Fatihah_ the Opening chapter of the Quran. This is **obligatory (Fard)** in every Rak'ah of every prayer. The prayer is invalid without it.

{% hint style="info" %}
**بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ**

**Bismillahir-rahmanir-rahim**
_In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful_

**الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ**

**Alhamdu lillahi rabbil-'alamin**
_All praise is for Allah, Lord of all worlds_

**الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ**

**Ar-rahmanir-rahim**
_The Most Gracious, the Most Merciful_

**مَالِكِ يَوْمِ الدِّينِ**

**Maliki yawmid-din**
_Master of the Day of Judgement_

**إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ**

**Iyyaka na'budu wa iyyaka nasta'in**
_You alone we worship, and You alone we ask for help_

**اهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ الْمُسْتَقِيمَ**

**Ihdinas-siratal-mustaqim**
_Guide us to the straight path_

**صِرَاطَ الَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ الْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا الضَّالِّينَ**

**Siratal-ladhina an'amta 'alayhim ghayril-maghdubi 'alayhim wa lad-dallin**
_The path of those You have blessed not those who have earned anger, nor those who have gone astray_

**آمِين Ameen**
{% endhint %}

The Prophet said: _"Whoever prays a prayer in which he does not recite Al-Fatihah, his prayer is deficient."_ (Sahih Muslim)

### After Al-Fatihah Recite an Additional Surah

In the first two Rak'ahs, after Al-Fatihah, recite any Surah or portion of the Quran. The shortest and most commonly recited is:

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Al-Ikhlas قُلْ هُوَ اللَّهُ أَحَدٌ**

**Transliteration:**
_Qul huwa Allahu ahad. Allahus-samad. Lam yalid wa lam yulad. Wa lam yakun lahu kufuwan ahad._

**Translation:**
_Say: He is Allah, the One. Allah, the Eternal Absolute. He neither begets nor was He begotten. And there is nothing comparable to Him._
{% endhint %}

---

### Step 5 The Bowing Position (_Ruku'_)

**What to do:** Say _Allahu Akbar_, then bend forward at the waist until your back is flat and parallel to the ground. Place your hands firmly on your knees, fingers spread. Keep your head level neither raised nor lowered.

**What to say (repeat 3 times):**

{% hint style="info" %}
**سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْعَظِيمِ**

**Transliteration:** _Subhana rabbiyal-'azim_

**Translation:** "Glory be to my Lord, the Great"
{% endhint %}

---

### Step 6 Rising from Ruku'

**What to do:** Rise from bowing to a fully upright standing position while saying:

{% hint style="info" %}
**سَمِعَ اللَّهُ لِمَنْ حَمِدَهُ**

**Transliteration:** _Sami'allahu liman hamidah_

**Translation:** "Allah hears whoever praises Him"
{% endhint %}

Once fully upright, say:

{% hint style="info" %}
**رَبَّنَا وَلَكَ الْحَمْدُ**

**Transliteration:** _Rabbana wa lakal-hamd_

**Translation:** "Our Lord, and to You is all praise"
{% endhint %}

---

### Step 7 The Prostration (_Sujud_)

**What to do:** Say _Allahu Akbar_, then go down to the ground in prostration. **Seven body parts** must be in contact with the ground:

1. Forehead and nose
2. Both palms (flat on the ground)
3. Both knees
4. The toes of both feet (touching the ground)

Your arms should not rest on the ground keep your elbows raised. Your head should be between your hands.

**What to say (repeat 3 times):**

{% hint style="info" %}
**سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْأَعْلَىٰ**

**Transliteration:** _Subhana rabbiyal-a'la_

**Translation:** "Glory be to my Lord, the Highest"
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="tip" %}
**Spiritual Insight:** Sujud is the closest a human being comes to Allah in prayer. The Prophet said: _"The closest that a servant is to his Lord is when he is in prostration so make much supplication in it."_ (Sahih Muslim). After the obligatory dhikr, make personal dua in your own language.
{% endhint %}

---

### Step 8 Sitting Between the Two Prostrations

**What to do:** Rise from prostration saying _Allahu Akbar_, and sit briefly. Then say:

{% hint style="info" %}
**رَبِّ اغْفِرْ لِي**

**Transliteration:** _Rabbighfir li_

**Translation:** "My Lord, forgive me"
{% endhint %}

Then go down for a **second Sujud**, repeating the same as in Step 7.

---

### Step 9 Rising to the Second Rak'ah

After the second Sujud, rise while saying _Allahu Akbar_ back to a standing position. This completes the **first Rak'ah**. The second Rak'ah begins from Step 4 (reciting Al-Fatihah) without the opening supplication.

---

### Step 10 The Sitting After the Second Rak'ah (_At-Tashahhud_)

After the second Sujud of the **second Rak'ah**, sit down. In a 2-Rak'ah prayer (like Fajr), this is the final sitting. In a 3 or 4-Rak'ah prayer, you will rise again for more Rak'ahs.

**Sitting position:** Sit on your left foot with it folded under you, right foot upright with toes pointing toward the Qiblah. Rest your left hand flat on your left knee. Form a circle with the thumb and middle finger of your right hand and extend your index finger pointing it toward the Qiblah.

**Recite the Tashahhud:**

{% hint style="info" %}
**التَّحِيَّاتُ لِلَّهِ وَالصَّلَوَاتُ وَالطَّيِّبَاتُ...**

**Transliteration:**
_At-tahiyyatu lillahi was-salawatu wat-tayyibat. As-salamu 'alayka ayyuhan-nabiyyu wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh. As-salamu 'alayna wa 'ala 'ibadillahis-salihin. Ashhadu alla ilaha illallah, wa ashhadu anna Muhammadan 'abduhu wa rasuluh._

**Translation:**
"All greetings, prayers, and pure words are for Allah. Peace be upon you, O Prophet, and the mercy of Allah and His blessings. Peace be upon us and upon the righteous servants of Allah. I bear witness that there is no god but Allah, and I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and messenger."
{% endhint %}

**Then recite the Salawat (blessings upon the Prophet ):**

{% hint style="info" %}
**اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَىٰ مُحَمَّدٍ...**

**Transliteration:**
_Allahumma salli 'ala Muhammadin wa 'ala ali Muhammadin, kama sallayta 'ala Ibrahima wa 'ala ali Ibrahim. Innaka hamidun majid. Allahumma barik 'ala Muhammadin wa 'ala ali Muhammadin, kama barakta 'ala Ibrahima wa 'ala ali Ibrahim. Innaka hamidun majid._

**Translation:**
"O Allah, send prayers upon Muhammad and upon the family of Muhammad, just as You sent prayers upon Ibrahim and the family of Ibrahim. Verily, You are the Most Praiseworthy, Most Glorious. O Allah, bless Muhammad and the family of Muhammad, just as You blessed Ibrahim and the family of Ibrahim. Verily, You are the Most Praiseworthy, Most Glorious."
{% endhint %}

---

### Step 11 The Closing Salutation (_Taslim_)

**What to do:** Turn your head to the **right** and say:

{% hint style="info" %}
**اَلسَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ وَرَحْمَةُ اللَّهِ**

**Transliteration:** _As-salamu 'alaykum wa rahmatullah_

**Translation:** "Peace be upon you and the mercy of Allah"
{% endhint %}

Then turn your head to the **left** and repeat the same.

**The prayer is now complete.**

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Mistake 1 Rushing through the prayer**
→ The Prophet told a man who prayed quickly to _"go back and pray, for you have not prayed."_ Each position must be held with stillness (_Tuma'ninah_) at least until the body is fully still.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Mistake 2 Not reciting Al-Fatihah in every Rak'ah**
→ The prayer is invalid without it. Even in a prayer led by an Imam, the majority of scholars hold that each person must still recite Al-Fatihah quietly.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Mistake 3 Looking around during prayer**
→ The Prophet said looking around during prayer is "snatching" that the devil takes from the servant's prayer. Keep eyes focused on the place of prostration.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Mistake 4 Forgetting which Rak'ah you're on**
→ If in doubt, take the lesser number (assume you've done fewer Rak'ahs) and add a prostration of forgetfulness (_Sujud al-Sahw_) after the Taslim.
{% endhint %}

## Scholarly Notes Differences Between Madhabs

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Madhab differences:**

- **Hanafi:** The index finger is raised once at the Tashahhud (at _la ilaha_) then lowered.
- **Maliki:** The index finger is moved side to side throughout the Tashahhud.
- **Shafi'i:** The finger is raised at _illa Allah_ and kept raised until Taslim.
- **Hanbali:** Similar to Shafi'i finger raised and kept up.

All four positions are valid. Follow the opinion of your madhab.
{% endhint %}

## Frequently Asked Questions

<details>

<summary>What if I don't know Arabic yet? Can I pray in my own language?</summary>

Al-Fatihah and the obligatory dhikr must be in Arabic this is the scholarly consensus. However, a new Muslim who has not yet learned Arabic may pray with whatever Arabic they know and learn the rest gradually. The personal dua (supplication) within Sujud may be made in any language.

</details>

<details>

<summary>What do I do if I miss a Rak'ah praying behind an Imam?</summary>

You join the prayer from wherever the Imam currently is, and complete the missed Rak'ahs after the Imam finishes with Taslim. You do not follow the Imam's Taslim you stand and complete your remaining Rak'ahs.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Is it valid to pray sitting down?</summary>

Yes. If a person cannot stand due to illness or disability, they may pray sitting. If they cannot sit, they pray lying down. Islam provides concessions at every level of capability. The Prophet said: _"Pray standing; if you cannot, then sitting; if you cannot, then on your side."_ (Sahih al-Bukhari)

</details>

## Dua After Prayer

{% hint style="info" %}
**After the Taslim, recite:**

_Astaghfirullah_ (×3) _"I seek forgiveness from Allah"_

_Allahu Akbar_ _"Allah is the Greatest"_

_Subhanallah_ (×33) _"Glory be to Allah"_

_Alhamdulillah_ (×33) _"All praise is for Allah"_

_Allahu Akbar_ (×33) _"Allah is the Greatest"_

Then: _La ilaha illallahu wahdahu la sharika lah, lahul-mulku wa lahul-hamd, wa huwa 'ala kulli shay'in qadir._

**Translation:** _"There is no god but Allah alone, with no partner. To Him belongs the dominion and all praise, and He is over all things capable."_

_(Source: Sahih Muslim the Prophet taught this after every obligatory prayer)_
{% endhint %}
