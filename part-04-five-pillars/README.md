# Part 4 The Five Pillars of Islam (_Arkan al-Islam_)

**Difficulty:** Beginner → Intermediate &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Chapters:** 20 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Estimated reading time:** ~3 hours

---

## What You Will Learn in This Part

The Five Pillars of Islam are the foundational acts of worship that structure every Muslim's life. They are not merely religious duties they are the architecture of a life consciously oriented toward Allah. Each pillar is a different mode of total surrender: the tongue (Shahada), the body (Salah), the wealth (Zakat), the appetite (Sawm), and the self (Hajj).

This part covers each pillar in depth from the basic "what" to the deep "why" including step-by-step practical guides, the spiritual philosophy behind each act, and the scholarly nuances that a serious student needs to know.

---

## The Five Pillars at a Glance

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih al-Bukhari and Muslim**

The Prophet said: _"Islam is built upon five: the testimony that there is no god but Allah and that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah, establishing prayer, giving Zakat, pilgrimage to the House, and fasting Ramadan."_
{% endhint %}

| Arabic              | English              | Frequency                                    |
| ------------------- | -------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| الشَّهَادَة Shahada | Declaration of Faith | Once at entry into Islam, renewed constantly |
| الصَّلَاة Salah     | Prayer               | 5 times daily                                |
| الزَّكَاة Zakat     | Obligatory Charity   | Once per year                                |
| الصَّوْم Sawm       | Fasting              | Every day of Ramadan                         |
| الحَجّ Hajj         | Pilgrimage           | Once in a lifetime (for the capable)         |
