# The Spiritual Dimensions of Ramadan

**Difficulty:** Intermediate &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Read time:** ~9 min &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Part:** Part 4: Five Pillars Sawm

## Ramadan Is a School

The Quran states the purpose of Ramadan in one word: _Taqwa_ God-consciousness. Fasting is the curriculum; Taqwa is the graduation.

Imam Ibn al-Qayyim described Ramadan as the month in which the _nafs_ (ego) is trained: denied its habitual demands, forced into a discipline it does not choose, and thereby strengthened against the weaknesses of the rest of the year.

## Fasting Belongs to Allah Alone

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Qudsi Sahih al-Bukhari**

Allah says: _"All deeds of the son of Adam are for himself, except fasting it is for Me, and I will reward it. The fasting person leaves his food, drink, and desires for My sake."_
{% endhint %}

Fasting is the only act of worship that is entirely internal no one but Allah can verify it. This is why its reward is without account. It is pure sincerity.

## The Quran and Ramadan

The Prophet reviewed the entire Quran with Jibreel (AS) every Ramadan and in his final year, twice. The month of Ramadan is inseparable from the Quran. Muslims should:

- Set a Quran completion goal at least one _Khatm_ (completion)
- Attend Tarawih prayers where the Quran is recited in full
- Reflect (_Tadabbur_) on what is being recited not just race through pages

## The Ramadan Ladder Ascending Each Third

Islamic scholars describe Ramadan in three ascending phases:

| First 10 days | Second 10 days          | Final 10 days                          |
| ------------- | ----------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| _Rahma_ Mercy | _Maghfirah_ Forgiveness | _Itq min al-Nar_ Freedom from the Fire |

Each third deepens the opportunity. Do not coast through the first two thirds and sprint only for Laylatul Qadr the entire month is precious.

## Controlling the Tongue and Behavior

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih al-Bukhari**

_"Whoever does not give up false speech and acting on it, Allah has no need of him giving up his food and drink."_
{% endhint %}

The stomach fasts but the tongue, eyes, ears, and heart must also fast. Ramadan is the month of abandoning:

- Backbiting (_Ghibah_)
- Idle talk and social media time-wasting
- Anger and harsh speech
- Looking at what is prohibited

The person who fasts from food but not from sin has achieved hunger without reward.

## The Community Dimension

Ramadan transforms communities. The shared fast creates:

- Solidarity with the poor experiencing hunger by choice produces empathy for those who have no choice
- Communal Tarawih prayer the mosque becomes alive at night
- Shared Iftar breaking fast together is one of the most powerful acts of community building

The Prophet said: _"Whoever feeds a fasting person will have the same reward as him, without the fasting person's reward being diminished in the slightest."_ (Tirmidhi Sahih)
