# Rules of Fasting What Is Required and What Breaks the Fast

**Difficulty:** Beginner &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Read time:** ~8 min &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Part:** Part 4 Five Pillars Sawm

---

## The Intention (_Niyyah_)

The intention for fasting must be made **before Fajr** each night of Ramadan. It need not be verbal the sincere internal intention to fast the following day for the sake of Allah is sufficient. Some scholars hold the intention for the entire month made at the beginning is enough; others require a nightly renewal. The cautious approach: renew it each night.

---

## What Invalidates the Fast

### Definite Invalidators (require making up that day):

| Invalidator                                   | Notes                                                                                                            |
| --------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Eating or drinking** anything intentionally | Even a single swallow                                                                                            |
| **Sexual intercourse**                        | Also requires _Kaffarah_ (expiation) freeing a slave, or fasting 60 consecutive days, or feeding 60 poor persons |
| **Deliberate vomiting**                       | Accidental vomiting does not break the fast                                                                      |
| **Menstruation beginning**                    | Even moments before Maghrib                                                                                      |
| **Post-natal bleeding beginning**             | Same ruling as menstruation                                                                                      |
| **Intention to break the fast**               | Changing the intention itself breaks it                                                                          |

### What Does NOT Break the Fast:

| Action                                | Status                      |
| ------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Swallowing saliva                     | Does not break              |
| Accidental swallowing (water, dust)   | Does not break              |
| Rinsing the mouth without swallowing  | Permitted                   |
| Eye drops, ear drops                  | Does not break (majority)   |
| Injections (not nutritional)          | Does not break (majority)   |
| Blood tests or blood donation         | Does not break              |
| Using toothbrush (no paste) / Siwak   | Permitted and Sunnah        |
| Kissing spouse without further action | Permitted with self-control |
| Swimming (without swallowing)         | Permitted                   |
| Applying kohl (eyeliner)              | Permitted (majority)        |
| Dreaming of eating while asleep       | Does not break              |

## Who May Break the Fast (and Must Make It Up)

| Person                                           | Ruling         | Make-up required?     |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------------- | --------------------- |
| Traveler (qualifying distance ~80km+)            | May break fast | Yes                   |
| Ill person (temporary illness)                   | May break fast | Yes                   |
| Pregnant or breastfeeding woman (if harm feared) | May break fast | Yes + possibly Fidyah |

## Who Cannot Fast (and Pays Fidyah Instead)

| Person                                    | Ruling                                    |
| ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Elderly person permanently unable to fast | Pays Fidyah feeds one poor person per day |
| Chronically ill permanent incapacity      | Same Fidyah replaces fasting              |

**Fidyah amount:** Feeding one poor person a full meal (or equivalent food value) for each missed day.

---

## The _Kaffarah_ for Breaking Fast by Intercourse

This is the most serious violation of the fast. The expiation (_Kaffarah_) must be performed in order:

1. Free a slave (if applicable)
2. If unable: Fast 60 consecutive days
3. If unable: Feed 60 poor persons

This applies per occurrence, not per Ramadan.

## Madhab Differences on Fasting Rules

| Issue                           | Hanafi             | Maliki             | Shafi'i            | Hanbali        |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------ | ------------------ | ------------------ | -------------- |
| Cupping/bloodletting            | Does not break     | Breaks fast        | Does not break     | Breaks fast    |
| Tasting food without swallowing | Does not break     | Does not break     | Does not break     | Does not break |
| Intentional vomiting            | Breaks if mouthful | Breaks if mouthful | Breaks if mouthful | Breaks         |
