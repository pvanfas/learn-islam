# Laylatul Qadr The Night of Power

**Difficulty:** Beginner &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Read time:** ~9 min &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Part:** Part 4 Five Pillars Sawm

---

## In This Chapter

> Laylatul Qadr the Night of Power is the single most spiritually valuable night in the Islamic calendar. The Quran describes it as better than a thousand months of worship. This chapter covers everything the Quran and Sunnah say about it: when it is, what to do in it, what happens on it, and how to maximize its extraordinary potential.

---

## The Quranic Description

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Al-Qadr 97:15**

_إِنَّا أَنزَلْنَاهُ فِي لَيْلَةِ الْقَدْرِ ۝ وَمَا أَدْرَاكَ مَا لَيْلَةُ الْقَدْرِ ۝ لَيْلَةُ الْقَدْرِ خَيْرٌ مِّنْ أَلْفِ شَهْرٍ ۝ تَنَزَّلُ الْمَلَائِكَةُ وَالرُّوحُ فِيهَا بِإِذْنِ رَبِّهِم مِّن كُلِّ أَمْرٍ ۝ سَلَامٌ هِيَ حَتَّىٰ مَطْلَعِ الْفَجْرِ_

**Translation:**
"Indeed, We sent the Quran down during the Night of Power. And what can make you know what is the Night of Power? The Night of Power is better than a thousand months. The angels and the Spirit descend therein by permission of their Lord for every matter. Peace it is until the emergence of dawn."

_A single night of sincere worship on Laylatul Qadr exceeds the worship of 1,000 months approximately 83 years. More than a complete human lifetime._
{% endhint %}

## When Is Laylatul Qadr?

The exact night is not specified and this is deliberate. The Prophet said:

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih al-Bukhari**

_"Seek Laylatul Qadr in the odd nights of the last ten days of Ramadan."_
{% endhint %}

The odd nights of the last ten days: 21st, 23rd, 25th, 27th, and 29th of Ramadan.

The **27th night** is widely considered the most likely based on several narrations including the Companion Ubayy ibn Ka'b's confident statement that it is the 27th. However, the Prophet himself never fixed it definitively and the wisdom in that is that:

1. **Seeking it across multiple nights** multiplies the worship of the final ten days
2. **Uncertainty motivates more effort** if everyone knew it was the 27th, the other nights would be neglected
3. **The entire last ten days become blessed** through the seeking

The practice of the Prophet was to **make I'tikaf** (seclusion in the mosque) during the last ten days spending every night in prayer, Quran, and dua.

## What Happens on Laylatul Qadr

### The Quran Was Revealed

The Quran descended to the lowest heaven on Laylatul Qadr the beginning of the 23-year revelation process. This night is therefore the anniversary of the greatest gift Allah gave humanity.

### Angels Descend in Extraordinary Numbers

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Musnad Ahmad (Hasan)**

The Prophet said: _"On the Night of Power, Jibreel descends with a group of angels who send prayers upon every person they find standing or sitting, remembering Allah."_
{% endhint %}

The number of angels on earth on Laylatul Qadr exceeds the number of pebbles on earth the celestial hosts converge on a world full of worshippers.

### Divine Decrees Are Determined

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Ad-Dukhan 44:34**

**Translation:** "Indeed, We sent it down on a blessed night... On that night every precise matter is decided."

_The decrees for the coming year births, deaths, provision, events are determined and assigned to the angels to execute. A person worshipping on this night is present as the decree for their year is being written._
{% endhint %}

### Peace Descends Until Dawn

The Quran describes it as _Salam_ (peace) an overwhelming divine tranquility that settles upon the earth until Fajr. Those who worship sincerely on this night often report an indescribable quality to the night that distinguishes it from others.

## What to Do on Laylatul Qadr

### The Essential Dua

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Tirmidhi (Sahih)**

Aisha (RA) asked: _"O Messenger of Allah if I witness Laylatul Qadr, what should I say?"_

He replied: _"Say:"_

_اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّكَ عَفُوٌّ تُحِبُّ الْعَفْوَ فَاعْفُ عَنِّي_

**Transliteration:** _Allahumma innaka 'afuwwun tuhibbul-'afwa fa'fu 'anni_

**Translation:** "O Allah, You are the Pardoner, You love to pardon so pardon me."

_Notice the Prophet chose Al-'Afuw (the Pardoner who erases completely) not merely Al-Ghafur (the Forgiver). 'Afw means the sin is erased as if it never existed. This is the dua for the greatest night._
{% endhint %}

### The Complete Program

**From Maghrib to Fajr the Night of Qadr:**

| Time            | Activity                                                |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| After Isha      | Tarawih or Tahajjud prayer                              |
| Night           | Quran recitation as much as possible                    |
| Between prayers | Personal dua in Arabic or your own language             |
| Sujood          | Extended personal supplication pour your heart out      |
| Final third     | Intensify _"Who is calling upon Me that I may answer?"_ |
| Before Fajr     | The Sayyid al-Istighfar and Morning Adhkar              |

**What to ask for:**

The Prophet 's chosen dua is for _Afw_ (pardon) so that should be primary. After that, ask for everything:

- Guidance and firm faith
- Forgiveness for yourself and your family
- Entry into Paradise
- Protection from the Fire
- Health, provision, and good worldly outcomes
- Guidance for loved ones who have strayed
- For the Muslim Ummah
- For your community and your orphan care programs
- For Sadaqah Jariyah that continues after your death

The Prophet said in this night _"every matter is decided"_ so ask as if this is your annual petition before the King of Kings. Because it is.

## I'tikaf Seclusion in the Last Ten Days

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih al-Bukhari**

_"The Prophet used to observe I'tikaf during the last ten days of Ramadan until he passed away. Then his wives observed I'tikaf after him."_
{% endhint %}

I'tikaf is spending the last ten days continuously in the mosque leaving only for necessities. It is a confirmed Sunnah. Those who cannot do the full ten days can do a partial I'tikaf with the intention for as long as they remain.

The one in I'tikaf is:

- Cut off from worldly distraction
- Continuously in a state of worship prayer, Quran, dhikr, dua
- Available on every odd night of the last ten

Even if unable to do I'tikaf intensify your worship on the odd nights of the last ten days.

## Signs of Laylatul Qadr

Some narrations describe signs associated with the night:

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih Muslim**

_"On the morning following Laylatul Qadr, the sun rises with no rays as a disc."_
{% endhint %}

Other narrations describe the night as moderate in temperature, with a soft quality to the air. However, these are signs after the fact not reliable means of identification beforehand. Seek it in all the odd nights and trust that sincere effort on any of them will be rewarded.

## Key Takeaways

- Laylatul Qadr is better than 1,000 months of worship approximately 83 years.
- It falls in the odd nights of the last ten days of Ramadan particularly the 27th night.
- Its exact date is hidden to maximize the believer's effort across all ten nights.
- Angels descend in extraordinary numbers; divine decrees for the coming year are determined.
- The Prophet's prescribed dua: _"Allahumma innaka 'afuwwun tuhibbul-'afwa fa'fu 'anni."_
- Fill the night with Tahajjud, Quran recitation, and personal dua especially in Sujood.
- I'tikaf in the mosque for the last ten days is the Sunnah way to ensure you catch this night.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<details>

<summary>Can Laylatul Qadr be found if you pray at home, not in the mosque?</summary>

Yes. The night's blessing is not contingent on being in a mosque though the mosque is the Sunnah for those who do I'tikaf. A person who prays in their home, recites Quran, and makes sincere dua through the odd nights can receive the full blessing of Laylatul Qadr. The mosque is the optimal environment; the home is perfectly valid.

</details>

<details>

<summary>What if I missed the last ten nights due to illness or travel?</summary>

Allah knows your circumstances. Make up as many of the remaining odd nights as possible. If the 27th has passed and you are now well, pray the remaining nights with full sincerity you may still catch it if it has not yet come. And remember: even missing Laylatul Qadr, the other nights of Ramadan carry enormous blessing. Do not despair.

</details>
