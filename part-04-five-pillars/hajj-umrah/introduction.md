## Hajj The Fifth Pillar

Hajj (_الحَجّ_) is the pilgrimage to Makkah the fifth and final pillar of Islam. It is obligatory **once in a lifetime** for every Muslim who is physically and financially able.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Al-Imran 3:97**
**Translation:** "...Pilgrimage to the House is a duty owed to Allah by people who are able to undertake it. And whoever disbelieves then indeed, Allah is free from need of the worlds."
{% endhint %}

## The History of Hajj

The rituals of Hajj trace directly to **Ibrahim (AS)** who built the Ka'bah with his son Ismail (AS) by Allah's command, and was then instructed to call humanity to pilgrimage:

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Al-Hajj 22:27**
**Translation:** "And proclaim to the people the Hajj they will come to you on foot and on every lean camel; they will come from every distant pass."
{% endhint %}

The rituals the Tawaf, the Sa'i, standing at Arafah, the stoning of the Jamarat all commemorate moments from Ibrahim's (AS), Hajar's (AS), and Ismail's (AS) lives.

## Hajj vs. Umrah

| Feature                | Hajj                         | Umrah              |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------ |
| **Status**             | Obligatory (once)            | Highly recommended |
| **Time**               | Specific days in Dhul Hijjah | Any time of year   |
| **Duration**           | 56 days                      | Half a day or less |
| **Standing at Arafah** | Required                     | Not included       |
| **Stoning Jamarat**    | Required                     | Not included       |

## The Spiritual Reality of Hajj

Hajj is often described as a rehearsal of the Day of Judgement millions of souls from every nation, in the same simple white garments, equal before Allah, standing on the plain of Arafah with nothing but their deeds.

The Prophet said: _"An accepted Hajj has no reward except Paradise."_ (Sahih al-Bukhari)

## Who Must Perform Hajj?

Hajj is obligatory upon a Muslim who:

- Is **adult** (post-puberty)
- Is **sane**
- Is **free** (not enslaved)
- Is **physically able** to make the journey
- Has **financial means** the travel cost plus provision for dependents left behind

If a person meets these conditions and dies without performing Hajj, they have died with an unfulfilled obligation.
