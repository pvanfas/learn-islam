# Umrah — The Lesser Pilgrimage

**Difficulty:** Beginner &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Read time:** ~14 min &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Part:** Part 4 — The Five Pillars — Hajj & Umrah

---

## What This Guide Covers

> Umrah is the voluntary pilgrimage to Makkah that can be performed at any time of year. This chapter covers what Umrah is, how it differs from Hajj, its steps in full detail with the relevant duas, common mistakes, and practical preparation tips for anyone planning to make this blessed journey.

## Islamic Basis

{% hint style="info" %}
**Surah Al-Baqarah 2:196**

**Translation:** "And complete the Hajj and Umrah for Allah..."

_This verse makes the completion of Umrah, once begun, obligatory. Starting Umrah and abandoning it without excuse is not permitted._
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith — Sahih al-Bukhari**

The Prophet ﷺ said: _"Follow up the Hajj and Umrah, because they eliminate poverty and sins the way that a bellows eliminates the impurities of iron, gold, and silver."_
{% endhint %}

## What Is Umrah?

Umrah is a set of acts of worship performed in Makkah that includes entering a state of spiritual purity (_Ihram_), circling the Kaaba (_Tawaf_), walking between the hills of Safa and Marwa (_Sa'i_), and shaving or cutting the hair. Unlike Hajj, which is obligatory once in a lifetime and can only be performed during specific days of the Islamic calendar, Umrah:

- Is **highly recommended** (_Sunnah Mu'akkadah_ according to Hanafi and Maliki scholars; **obligatory** according to Shafi'i and Hanbali scholars)
- Can be performed **at any time of year**
- Can be **repeated** — many scholars consider each Umrah an expiation for sins between it and the previous one
- Takes **less than half a day** to complete the actual rituals

## Best Times to Perform Umrah

| Time                             | Note                                                                      |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Ramadan**                      | Most rewarded — the Prophet ﷺ said Umrah in Ramadan equals Hajj in reward |
| **First 10 days of Dhul Hijjah** | Highly blessed but crowds are lower than Hajj itself                      |
| **Outside Hajj season**          | Less crowded, easier logistics                                            |
| **Avoid the 5 days of Hajj**     | Ihraam for Umrah conflicts with Hajj pilgrims' movements                  |

## The Four Steps of Umrah

---

### Step 1 — Enter the State of Ihram at the Miqat

**The Miqat** is the boundary around Makkah at which a pilgrim must enter the sacred state of Ihram before proceeding. You cannot pass the Miqat without being in Ihram if you intend Umrah.

**There are five Miqat boundaries:**

| Miqat                     | For travelers coming from |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Dhul Hulayfah (Abyar Ali) | Madinah direction         |
| Al-Juhfah                 | Syria, Morocco, Egypt     |
| Qarn al-Manazil           | Najd / Riyadh direction   |
| Yalamlam                  | Yemen, India, Pakistan    |
| Dhat Irq                  | Iraq, Iran                |

For those **already in Makkah or between Makkah and the Miqat**, the Miqat is the boundary of the Haram itself.
For those **flying over the Miqat**, you must enter Ihram before your plane crosses it — check the flight route.

**How to Enter Ihram:**

1. **Purify yourself** — make Ghusl (ritual bath) with the intention of Ihram. If not possible, Wudu is sufficient.
2. **Dress in Ihram garments:**
   - **Men:** Two unstitched white cloths — one wrapped around the lower body (_Izar_), one draped over the upper body (_Rida'_). No underwear, no shoes that cover the ankle. Wear sandals.
   - **Women:** Regular modest Islamic dress — any color. The face and hands must remain uncovered during Tawaf (but may be covered otherwise). No face veil (_Niqab_) while in Ihram.
3. **Pray two Rak'ahs** of Sunnah prayer (if not a disliked time).
4. **Make the intention (Niyyah):** Say:

{% hint style="info" %}
**لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ عُمْرَةً**

**Transliteration:** _Labbayka Allahumma 'umrah_

**Translation:** "Here I am, O Allah, for Umrah"

_With this intention, you have entered the state of Ihram._
{% endhint %}

5. **Begin reciting the Talbiyah** continuously — aloud for men, quietly for women:

{% hint style="info" %}
**لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ لَبَّيْكَ، لَبَّيْكَ لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ لَبَّيْكَ، إِنَّ الْحَمْدَ وَالنِّعْمَةَ لَكَ وَالْمُلْكَ، لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ**

**Transliteration:** _Labbayka Allahumma labbayk. Labbayka la sharika laka labbayk. Innal-hamda wan-ni'mata laka wal-mulk. La sharika lak._

**Translation:** "Here I am, O Allah, here I am. Here I am — You have no partner — here I am. Surely all praise, grace, and sovereignty belong to You. You have no partner."
{% endhint %}

**The Talbiyah is a declaration of total surrender.** Recite it at every opportunity until you begin Tawaf.

### Ihram Restrictions

Once in Ihram, the following are **prohibited** until you exit Ihram:

| Prohibited                | Men        | Women                 |
| ------------------------- | ---------- | --------------------- |
| Cutting hair or nails     |            |                       |
| Using perfume/fragrance   |            |                       |
| Sexual relations          |            |                       |
| Marriage contract         |            |                       |
| Hunting                   |            |                       |
| Wearing stitched garments |            | (normal modest dress) |
| Covering the head         | (men only) |                       |
| Covering the face         | (men)      | (women in Ihram)      |

### Step 2 — Tawaf (Circling the Kaaba)

**Upon arriving at Masjid al-Haram**, stop reciting the Talbiyah as you begin Tawaf.

**What to do:** Circle the Kaaba **seven times counter-clockwise**, starting and ending at the **Black Stone** (_Hajar al-Aswad_), which is set in the eastern corner of the Kaaba.

**Before beginning Tawaf:**

- Make sure you are in Wudu
- Men uncover their right shoulder (_Idtiba'_) — the upper cloth draped only over the left shoulder for the duration of Tawaf

**The Black Stone:**

- If close enough: kiss it, or touch it with your right hand and kiss your hand
- If too far (which is usually the case in crowded conditions): face it, raise your right hand toward it, and say:

{% hint style="info" %}
**بِسْمِ اللَّهِ، اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ**

**Transliteration:** _Bismillah, Allahu Akbar_

**Translation:** "In the name of Allah, Allah is the Greatest"
{% endhint %}

**During each circuit:** There is no fixed dua for Tawaf. Make whatever dua, dhikr, or Quran recitation you wish. One recommended dua at the Yemeni Corner (the corner before the Black Stone):

{% hint style="info" %}
**رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ**

**Transliteration:** _Rabbana atina fid-dunya hasanatan wa fil-akhirati hasanatan wa qina 'adhaban-nar_

**Translation:** "Our Lord, give us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire."
_(Surah Al-Baqarah 2:201)_
{% endhint %}

**Men's Raml:** In the first three circuits of Tawaf, men walk briskly with short quick steps (_Raml_). The final four circuits are walked normally.

After completing **seven circuits**, pray **two Rak'ahs behind Maqam Ibrahim** (the standing place of Ibrahim AS). If not possible due to crowds, pray anywhere in the Masjid.

---

### Step 3 — Sa'i (Walking Between Safa and Marwa)

After Tawaf, proceed to the **hill of Safa** to begin Sa'i — seven passes between Safa and Marwa, commemorating Hajar (Hagar AS), the mother of Ismail, who ran between these hills searching for water for her infant son.

**At Safa**, face the Kaaba and recite:

{% hint style="info" %}
**إِنَّ الصَّفَا وَالْمَرْوَةَ مِن شَعَائِرِ اللَّهِ**

**Transliteration:** _Innas-Safa wal-Marwata min sha'a'irillah_

_(Surah Al-Baqarah 2:158)_

Then raise your hands toward the Kaaba and make dua:

_Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar. La ilaha illallahu wahdahu la sharika lah. Lahul-mulku wa lahul-hamd, wa huwa 'ala kulli shay'in qadir..._

Then: _La ilaha illallah wahdah, anjaza wa'dah, wa nasara 'abdah, wa hazamal-ahzaba wahdah._

**Translation of the final phrase:** "There is no god but Allah alone. He fulfilled His promise, gave victory to His servant, and defeated the confederates alone."
{% endhint %}

Walk from Safa to Marwa (Pass 1), then Marwa to Safa (Pass 2), continuing until you complete **7 passes** — ending at **Marwa**.

**Men jog** between the two green marker lights in the middle of the Sa'i walkway (commemorating Hajar's running). Women walk at a normal pace throughout.

During Sa'i, make dua, recite dhikr, or recite Quran — there is no fixed recitation required between the hills.

---

### Step 4 — Shaving or Cutting the Hair (_Halq / Taqsir_)

After completing Sa'i, the Ihram is exited by:

- **Men:** Shaving the entire head (_Halq_) — this is superior. Or cutting at least a finger-length from all parts of the head (_Taqsir_).
- **Women:** Cutting approximately one fingertip's length from the end of the hair — from all or some of it. Women do **not** shave.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith — Sahih al-Bukhari**

The Prophet ﷺ said: _"O Allah, have mercy on those who shave their heads."_ The Companions said: "And those who cut their hair, O Messenger of Allah?" He said it again three times, then: _"And those who cut their hair."_
{% endhint %}

With the hair cut or shaved, **Umrah is complete.** All Ihram restrictions are lifted and you are free to dress normally, use perfume, and resume regular activities.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Mistake 1 — Crossing the Miqat without Ihram**
→ If this happens accidentally, you must either return to the Miqat, or sacrifice an animal (_dam_) in Makkah as expiation.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Mistake 2 — Women covering their faces during Tawaf**
→ The face must remain uncovered during Ihram. If a man approaches, briefly covering is permitted — but the Niqab as worn normally is not permitted in the Ihram state.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Mistake 3 — Starting Tawaf from the wrong place**
→ Tawaf must begin and end at the Black Stone. Starting from elsewhere means that circuit does not count.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Mistake 4 — Men covering their heads in Ihram**
→ No hat, cap, umbrella resting on the head, or towel on the head is permitted. Holding an umbrella above without resting it on the head is allowed.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Mistake 5 — Doing Sa'i without Wudu**
→ Sa'i does not require Wudu — it is not an act of worship requiring purity. However, it is recommended to be in Wudu.
{% endhint %}

## Can Umrah Be Repeated?

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Scholarly discussion:** The Prophet ﷺ performed Umrah four times in his lifetime. He also said: _"An Umrah to another Umrah expiates whatever sins occur between them."_ (Sahih al-Bukhari)

Most scholars permit and encourage multiple Umrahs throughout one's life. Some scholars (a minority) discourage performing multiple Umrahs during a single trip without returning home first, but this is not the dominant position.

The safest and most widely accepted view is that repeating Umrah is not only valid but highly encouraged, especially during Ramadan.
{% endhint %}

## Zamzam Water

Don't leave without drinking **Zamzam water** — the blessed water from the well that Allah caused to spring for Hajar and Ismail. It has been flowing for over 4,000 years and is available throughout Masjid al-Haram.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith — Sahih Muslim**

The Prophet ﷺ said: _"The water of Zamzam is for whatever it is drunk for."_

Drink it with the intention of health, knowledge, acceptance of Umrah, or any sincere dua — and make dua while drinking facing the Kaaba.
{% endhint %}

## Key Takeaways

- Umrah consists of four steps: Ihram → Tawaf → Sa'i → Halq/Taqsir.
- Ihram must be entered at the Miqat boundary — crossing without it is a violation requiring expiation.
- The Talbiyah is the great declaration of arrival and surrender — recite it continuously until Tawaf begins.
- Tawaf is seven counter-clockwise circuits around the Kaaba, beginning and ending at the Black Stone.
- Sa'i is seven passes between Safa and Marwa, starting at Safa, ending at Marwa, commemorating Hajar AS.
- Umrah in Ramadan is narrated to carry the reward of Hajj.
- Umrah can be repeated — each one expiates sins between it and the previous.
