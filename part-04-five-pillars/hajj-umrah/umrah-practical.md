## Before You Travel

### What to Memorize Before Departure

| Item                                       | Why                                         |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- |
| The Talbiyah (full text)                   | Recited continuously from Miqat until Tawaf |
| Du'a for entering Masjid al-Haram          | Etiquette of the sacred mosque              |
| Du'as at the Black Stone                   | _Bismillah, Allahu Akbar_ + optional du'a   |
| Du'a between Yameni Corner and Black Stone | _Rabbana atina fid-dunya hasanatan..._      |
| Du'a at Safa and Marwa                     | _Allahu Akbar_ + the remembrance            |
| Du'a after Wudu                            | For purity before Tawaf                     |

### Essential Items to Pack

- Ihram garments (men: 2 white unstitched cloths; women: modest Islamic dress)
- Ihram belt/safety pins
- Sandals (not covering the ankle)
- Unscented soap, shampoo, and deodorant (for Ihram state)
- Tasbeeh (prayer beads) for dhikr counting
- Small du'a book for Umrah
- Comfortable walking shoes for Sa'i

### Knowing Your Miqat

Before booking flights, confirm which Miqat applies to your route. If flying, you will enter Ihram on the plane before crossing the Miqat boundary. Airlines serving pilgrims usually announce it.

## Day of Umrah Practical Tips

### At the Miqat

1. Shower and put on Ihram garments before the Miqat
2. Pray 2 Rak'ahs of Ihram Sunnah
3. Make the intention: _Labbayka Allahumma 'Umrah_
4. Begin the Talbiyah men aloud, women quietly

### At Masjid al-Haram

- Enter with right foot, say the du'a for entering the mosque
- Stop Talbiyah when you first see the Ka'bah
- Make du'a at that moment it is answered
- Begin Tawaf from the Black Stone corner

### During Tawaf

- Stay hydrated the marble floor gets very hot in summer
- If crowded at the Black Stone, gesture from a distance do not push or harm others
- Use the upper floors if ground level is too crowded
- Men: uncover right shoulder (_Idtiba'_) for the duration

### During Sa'i

- Comfortable walking shoes are essential it is 450m each way × 7 = 3.15km
- Men jog between the green lights only
- Use the air-conditioned enclosed walkway
- Drink Zamzam water at the stations along the way

### Hair Cutting

- Men: have a barber in Makkah shave or cut many are available near the mosque
- Women: cut a fingertip's length discreetly no barber required

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Arguing or showing anger in Ihram _"No sexual relations, no wrongdoing, no arguing"_ (Quran 2:197)
- Touching or kissing the Ka'bah walls not established in the Sunnah
- Praying toward the Ka'bah from very close range during the prayer times (stand in the prayer rows instead)
- Assuming Umrah is complete after Sa'i it requires the hair cutting to exit Ihram
