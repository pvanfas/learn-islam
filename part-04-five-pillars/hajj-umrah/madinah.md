# Visiting Al-Madinah al-Munawwarah

**Difficulty:** Beginner &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Read time:** ~8 min &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Part:** Part 4 Five Pillars Hajj & Umrah

---

## The City of the Prophet

Al-Madinah al-Munawwarah the Illuminated City is the second holiest city in Islam. It is where the Prophet migrated, built his community, lived, and was buried. Visiting it is not part of Hajj or Umrah it is a separate act of love that carries its own immense reward.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih al-Bukhari**
_"Whoever visits me after my death, it is as if he visited me during my life."_ (Daraqutni scholars debate its grading, but the spirit is captured in the broader tradition of visiting the Prophet's mosque)
{% endhint %}

## Masjid an-Nabawi The Prophet's Mosque

**Prayer in Masjid an-Nabawi is equivalent to 1,000 prayers elsewhere except Masjid al-Haram (Makkah), which is 100,000.** (Sahih al-Bukhari)

### The Rawdah

Between the Prophet's grave and his minbar (pulpit) is a special area called the **Rawdah** (_ar-Rawdah al-Mutahharah_):

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hadith Sahih al-Bukhari**
The Prophet said: _"Between my house and my minbar is a garden from the gardens of Paradise."_
{% endhint %}

Pray in the Rawdah if possible and make du'a there with full presence.

### Visiting the Prophet's Grave

**Correct Adab:**

- Approach with reverence and humility
- Send Salam upon the Prophet : _"As-salamu 'alayka ya Rasulallah wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh"_
- Then send Salam upon Abu Bakr (RA) and Umar (RA) who are buried beside him
- Make du'a face the Qiblah, not the grave, when making du'a

**What to avoid:**

- Wiping or kissing the barrier around the grave
- Making du'a directly _to_ the Prophet (asking him for what only Allah can give)
- Making du'a while facing the grave rather than the Qiblah
- Excessive crowding, shouting, or emotion that disrupts others

## Other Sites in Madinah

| Site                    | Significance                                                                               |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Masjid al-Quba**      | First mosque ever built by the Prophet . Prayer there equals an Umrah (_Sahih al-Bukhari_) |
| **Masjid al-Qiblatayn** | Where the Qiblah changed from Jerusalem to Makkah                                          |
| **Jabal Uhud**          | Site of the Battle of Uhud; Hamza (RA) and other martyrs are buried here                   |
| **Al-Baqi' Cemetery**   | Many Companions buried here visit with adab and appropriate du'a                           |

## Du'a Upon Entering Madinah

{% hint style="info" %}
_اللَّهُمَّ هَذَا حَرَمُ نَبِيِّكَ فَاجْعَلْهُ وِقَايَةً لِي مِنَ النَّارِ وَأَمَانًا مِنَ الْعَذَابِ وَسُوءِ الْحِسَابِ_

"O Allah, this is the sanctuary of Your Prophet make it a shield for me from the Fire, a security from punishment and a poor accounting."
{% endhint %}
